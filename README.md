# Polynomial presentations of the integers over the rationals

Complete proofs and exact computational specifications for integer polynomials

```math
F(X,Y_1,\ldots,Y_m)
```

whose rational input projection is the integers, under the arithmetic premises stated in each construction.

**Every defining-polynomial variable count includes the input.** The separate [undecidability category](#fewest-variables-currently-recorded-for-undecidability) counts existential rational unknowns; its instance input is encoded in the coefficients. **The 22 / 24 / 11 bounds for defining the integers are not in conflict with the 48 / 50 / 36 unrestricted-degree undecidability bounds:** they answer different questions and use different variable-counting conventions. Degree means total degree. Coefficient height is the maximum absolute integer coefficient after the fixed parameters have been substituted. We optimize variable count, then degree, then coefficient height. No global minimum is claimed.

## Open reductions and proved obstructions

The current unrestricted-degree counts **22 / 24 / 11** and quartic counts **161 / 175 / 52** over Q include the reciprocal-gate and denominator-sensitive contact improvements; the function-field audit itself did not change them. The [exact-square checkpoint](computation/actual_square_v83/README.md) isolates the remaining actual-trace requirement and proves further prepared-fibre obstructions. The general quartic improvements retain the shared square coordinate for `R²`. The [v88 obstruction](negative_results/R_square_reweighting_v88/R_square_obstruction_20261123_v88.tex) excludes deleting it by diagonal Laurent reweighting in both existing units; different charts and nonlinear row combinations remain open. A proved [parity obstruction](papers/quartic/hyperbolic_parity_20261119_v79.tex) rules out one proposed full-pair compression in the retained integral Laurent chart; it does not establish a lower bound for arbitrary quartics.

The new [elliptic divisibility detector](papers/elliptic_divisibility/prepared_eds_divisibility_20261120_v82.tex) uses five coordinates for a prepared subrelation in which every nonzero integral quotient occurs, under ordinary abc and the published pole realization. It does not identify that quotient with an external rational input. A [one-parameter genus-one criterion](papers/pole_parameters/genus_one_sound_criterion_20261120_v82.tex) would give ten coordinates if its actual detector fibers have rational points. Every prepared scalar fiber is locally soluble, but an exact prepared counterexample rules out inferring rational points from those local conditions alone. Neither result is a new definition of the integers.

The [uniform-pole geometric obstruction](papers/pole_parameters/uniform_pole_geometric_obstructions_20261124/README.md) proves that the prepared scalar set is non-thin, so a standalone generically finite pole cannot be complete and sound. Under all-prime local soundness, a complete one-parameter menu must have a geometrically integral branch of genus at least one, and every multisection has even ramification at the scalar's infinity. These obstructions do not settle the actual-source genus-one realization problem or exclude a detector-coupled replacement.

The [joint elliptic-divisibility construction](papers/elliptic_divisibility/joint_divisibility_one_pole_20261124/README.md) uses **k+4 rational coordinates** to certify k simultaneous nonzero integer index quotients on the rational curve y²=x³−2x, including different fixed shifts of the reference point. Only one prepared BB pole is required. Exact regression tests cover 56 true-multiple and 34 rejection fixtures; the shifted true-multiple tests check the complete prime-to-exclusion denominator. The theorem retains the v82 ordinary-abc primitive-divisor and rank-one inputs. It is a new arithmetic subrelation, not a smaller defining polynomial: controlling the size of the hidden integer quotient relative to an independent rational input remains unresolved.

The [height-anchored quotient relation](papers/elliptic_divisibility/height_anchored_quotient_20261124/README.md) combines a height bound on the rational input with high-precision comparison to an integral elliptic quotient in six ordinary coordinates, using only one BB pole and no tailored prime supplier. It also proves actual nonintegral false positives: an incorrect quotient can always be made sufficiently large by CRT. A new quotient-size cap is required for the nine-variable target. The [duplication-mass and bounded-quotient continuation](papers/elliptic_divisibility/duplication_mass_and_bounded_quotients_20261124/README.md) gives a bounded reduced denominator of the elliptic quotient under every fixed weak rational abc exponent below 2 (denominator one for exponents below 8/5), and sharpens the precision at simple denominator primes to N+3. Under ordinary abc, for each fixed admissible nonintegral input the false witnesses can have log(1+|j/h|) asymptotic to (N+3) times the canonical height of the reference point. The [geometric no-cap theorem](negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/quotient_cap_pole_obstruction_20261124.md) proves, under ordinary rational abc, that finitely many additional uncoupled prepared rational-function poles on the same input and two elliptic points cannot give a quotient-size cap while retaining arbitrarily-large-reference integer completeness. The [finite Kummer extension](negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/quotient_cap_kummer_obstruction_20261124.md) further excludes repairing the cap by any fixed finite collection of independent square or higher-power tests in the existing input and two points: moving branch divisors force genus at least two on large multiplication graphs, while finite étale fibers preserve unbounded arithmetic progressions of numerator indices. These obstructions do not rule out new free auxiliary geometry, coupled covers or different completeness mechanisms.

The [Lucas-free simple-denominator amplifier](papers/elliptic_divisibility/simple_denominator_amplifier_20261124/README.md) gives **19/21-coordinate conditional alternatives** to the two-/three-scale general constructions, replacing the two Lucas labels and their three numerator comparisons by a single elliptic point and one rational pole. The additional (SD) hypothesis is eventual logarithmic mass at least `|c|` of rational primes of maximum formal depth exactly one for `[c]P`. The [duplication-mass continuation](papers/elliptic_divisibility/duplication_mass_and_bounded_quotients_20261124/README.md) strengthens the sufficient weak abc exponent over the quadratic carrier field to below `3/2` and gives a two-pole **21/23-coordinate conditional alternative** for every fixed exponent below `2`. The [sublinear bounded-odd-mass continuation](papers/elliptic_divisibility/sublinear_odd_mass_amplifier_20261124/README.md) further shows that 19/21 coordinates need only a lower bound of order `|c|^eta` for **any fixed positive eta** on the weighted logarithmic mass of rational primes with maximum formal depth odd and bounded by some fixed d. Increasing the scalar precision changes fixed degree, not variable count. The [bounded-depth arithmetic checker](scripts/verify_sublinear_odd_mass_20261124.py) passes 39,360 valuation profiles and 6,208 scalar precision inequalities. None of these alternatives derives the necessary mass from the old modular premises alone. A completely specified three-scale coefficient needs effective data for the additional mass hypothesis used; with an ineffective threshold the new 21-coordinate statement is an existence theorem. These conditional alternatives do not update the current no-additional-abc 25/27 records or improve the 11-coordinate weak/ordinary rational abc routes under overlapping hypotheses. The [exact arithmetic checker](scripts/verify_duplication_quotients_20261124.py) tests the finite multiplicity, valuation, gcd and exponent inequalities; it does not test the conjectural height hypotheses.

The [torsion-Belyi mass theorem](papers/elliptic_divisibility/torsion_belyi_mass_20261124/README.md) combines Streng's full primitive-part asymptotic with a weak-abc surplus bound. Under ordinary abc the exponent-one primitive denominator mass has the same quadratic asymptotic as the full primitive part on these carriers. On the v82 rational curve the eventual mass lower bound improves from `1/5` to `3/5` of the quadratic canonical height, and remains positive under the fixed weak rational abc exponent `27/25`. The six-coordinate quotient relation consequently works under this weaker fixed exponent after changing its scalar contacts. The [exact checker](scripts/verify_eds_amplifiers_20261124.py) passed 85,932 valuation profiles, 360 exact quadratic-field rejection fixtures, and the stated arithmetic identities; this is not a check of the additional arithmetic hypotheses.

The [modular-contact auxiliary carrier](papers/elliptic_divisibility/modular_contact_carrier_20261124/README.md) derives directly from the retained five-contact modular premise, without additional abc, the lower bound `log R_odd(c-1)+log R_odd(c+1) >= log|c|-log(1+log|c|)-O(1)` on an explicit full-two-torsion rational elliptic curve. A Pell specialization proves infinitely many primes of odd initial formal denominator depth on that curve. All five four-contact double covers have nonintegral rational j-invariants, so none is geometrically isogenous to the original CM carrier. This proves a concrete weaker mass result and an obstruction to exact square cancellation on the original carrier, not the required bounded-depth power-growth mass. The [coupled CM norm conics](papers/elliptic_divisibility/coupled_cm_norms_20261124/README.md) use quadratic subfields of inert prime-power torsion residue fields to give actual global norm witnesses on the multiplication graphs, including a seven-coordinate extension of the height-anchored quotient on its original rational curve. The norm detects odd-depth foreign denominator primes inert in the selected field; it still accepts each noninteger `X=ell^(-r)` with unbounded positive prime-power quotients, by compatible exponent congruences and the original ordered pole preparation. Thus these genuinely coupled conics do not supply a quotient cap or an all-integer defining polynomial. Both accompanying exact checkers passed locally; their scope is finite algebra and congruences, not new estimates for arbitrary indices.

The [all-prime foreign charge](papers/elliptic_divisibility/all_prime_foreign_charge_20261124/README.md) transfers every odd-depth foreign denominator prime of the numerator point into a single rational witness, using nine ordinary coordinates. Its optimal charge height on each fixed true multiplier `m` is asymptotic to `3(m^2-1)` times the canonical height of the reference. The [one-conic alternative](papers/elliptic_divisibility/one_conic_foreign_charge_20261124/README.md) achieves **seven-coordinate** complete extensions instead: one rational conic charges all odd-depth foreign primes into a single numerator, or an alternative conic charges **all depths, including even depths**, into two witness denominators with one finite root. Both have explicit rational parametrizations and exact torsion-divisor height estimates. They retain every old witness, including nonintegral false positives. A nonlinear global upper height bound on the charge remains necessary; the original five-contact modular height bound with odd support contained in a bounded-height anchor cannot provide it, since ordinary abc forces a linear height bound incompatible with the true multiplier family. The [foreign-boundary obstruction](negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/foreign_boundary_cover_obstruction_20261124.md) extends this limitation to arbitrary coupled algebraic covers whose local rejection of simple foreign primes leaves only a fixed proper closed exception locus. Under ordinary abc such a rejection, in particular a boundary having only higher-multiplicity components in a proper resolved cover, excludes all sufficiently large fixed integer multipliers with arbitrarily large references. The [executed checker](scripts/verify_foreign_charge_20261124.py) passed 113,600 synthetic valuation profiles, 815 exact rational BB local fixtures, 163 odd-depth and 184 all-depth conic fixtures, eight prepared-scalar checks, and the torsion-grid degree census. The extra BB rational witnesses are supplied by the retained theorem, not computed by that checker.

The [adaptive joint quotient transfer](papers/elliptic_divisibility/adaptive_joint_quotient_20261124/README.md) imports the v136 recursive odd-support union and the direct-index polynomial-residual comparisons of v111/v127 from the undecidability project. Under ordinary rational abc and the retained rank-one and BB inputs, one reference and one pole serve `k` external rational inputs and `r` hidden integer quotient labels in **k+r+4 ordinary coordinates**. Exact quotient divisibility follows from the full simple-denominator mass and a gcd argument. All prescribed polynomial residuals are tested to arbitrarily high fixed prime-adic precision, with a quantitative input-height anchor and complete true-integer witnesses. A simultaneous CRT theorem also proves that these local tests alone cannot bound the quotients. The [shared charge](papers/elliptic_divisibility/shared_charge_20261124/README.md) adds only one ordinary coordinate for the whole tuple: distinct odd powers of the numerator-point square roots detect the full simple-prime mass of the largest index after a negligible higher-depth loss. Its conic forces `h_W(s) >= q J²-o(J²)` with `J=max|j_i|`. A complete cap `log(1+h_W(s)) <= A q h²+O(1)` costing at most two further coordinates would yield a `k+r+7` transfer, in particular the nine-variable target for `k=r=1`; this cap is **not established**. The [standalone-cap obstructions](negative_results/papers/elliptic_divisibility/standalone_charge_cap_20261124.md) prove that a nondegenerate finite algebraic projection over the input/reference base has only a linear height budget, insufficient even for one true reference per unbounded input; they also exclude rationally parametrized standalone caps and bounded-height pointed rank-zero elliptic cap families. The v143 integer Lucas growth argument does not directly apply to rational witnesses or to finite-precision index residuals, as shown by explicit rational Lucas solutions and the new CRT theorem. The [executed exact checker](scripts/verify_adaptive_joint_quotient_20261124.py) passed 1,485 recursion-prime checks, 26,490 local inequality profiles, 62 Lucas CRT fixtures, 14,070 shared-charge valuation profiles, 512 rational conics, and 2,693 elliptic prime fixtures. These tests supplement the proofs; no new defining-polynomial record is claimed.

The [25 November ico cap](papers/elliptic_divisibility/ico_exponential_cap_20261125/README.md) establishes a uniform global upper bound of the previously required exponential scale, using the effective Mordell theorem of Fan and von Känel ([arXiv:2501.17155v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.17155v2), Corollary 7.6). A fixed rational surface chart and a reference-dependent nondegenerate hyperplane give `log(1+h_W(s)) <= 240 q h²+O(1)` with just one extra ordinary coordinate on the seven-coordinate charge relation. The same cap directly bounds the numerator-point square root without the charge, for seven total coordinates. Both relations have soundness under ordinary rational abc, the old chart and pole premises, and the newly used effective Mordell theorem, at precision 130 and a sufficiently large fixed reference cutoff. **Completeness is proved only for inputs ±1**, not for all integers; no new defining-polynomial count follows. The generic ico fibers are nonisotrivial genus nine. The fixed-family function-field height inequality and Faltings imply only finitely many reference indices on the direct cap for each sufficiently large fixed true multiplier. The [coupled curve obstructions](negative_results/papers/elliptic_divisibility/coupled_curve_cap_obstructions_20261125.md) now rule out fixed rank-zero Jacobian families (including genus-one torsors with no specified rational origin), positive-rank genus-one fibers with just the original prepared-pole condition, and isotrivial higher-genus families. The [bounded-reference continuation](papers/elliptic_divisibility/bounded_reference_20261125/README.md) proves existence of true witnesses in the original six-coordinate relation for every nonzero integer input m with `0<|h| <= C(1+|m|)^8100`, by real Dirichlet approximation near the identity and an explicit finite-place progression. Thus one-reference-per-input completeness is compatible with the parent source; it is not yet established for the cap. The [exact checker](scripts/verify_ico_cap_20261125.py) passed the symbolic surface and inverse-chart identities, discriminants, branch invariants, radical-degree and precision calculations, 32 rational elliptic chart fixtures and 1000 Taylor-exponent checks. The external effective Mordell theorem has not been independently audited in full. The remaining arithmetic task is to realize the ico constraint for every integer, perhaps using the height bound's insensitivity to the sizes of **integer** off-diagonal coefficients; uncontrolled rational coefficients would invalidate this argument after denominator clearing.

The [optimal ico interpolation theorem](papers/elliptic_divisibility/ico_boundary_interpolation_20261125/README.md) makes the integer off-diagonal freedom exact: for a rational nonboundary ico point Z the least diagonal radical of any integral nondegenerate homogeneous form through Z equals its coordinate-point reduction radical B(Z). The degree-fifteen form `d sigma1^15 + a sigma3^5 + b sigma5^3` with integer coefficients achieves it, so the Fan--von Känel bound becomes `h(Z) <= 10^(10^12) B(Z)^24`. Equivalently, two coefficients in a localized integer ring suffice to certify the permitted boundary support. This gives a nine-coordinate mixed-domain cap template on the direct numerator coordinate, not a rational polynomial definition: both a global boundary-controlled lift and the coefficient denominator-support certificate remain missing. Every prescribed numerator coordinate different from zero and one has a nonboundary local lift at every prime above eleven; weak approximation handles any finite set but cannot control new primes. Under ordinary abc, any fixed rational graph of the numerator coordinate fails the required uniform boundary budget even with a single reference per unbounded input. The [multiplication-boundary theorem](negative_results/papers/elliptic_divisibility/ico_multiplication_boundary_20261125.md) further uses Javanpeykar--Litt weak boundedness and marked Belyi contacts to rule out arbitrarily-large-reference completeness by a finite menu of rational maps for each sufficiently large fixed multiplier, without requiring a bound on their degrees or uniform formulas in the multiplier. This does not exclude choosing new arithmetic lifts at individual references. The [executed exact checker](scripts/verify_ico_interpolation_20261125.py) passed 277,082 projective finite-field points, 2,890 local lifts, 1,090 rational and 32 elliptic interpolation fixtures, and the exact resultant identities. No all-integer completeness or new defining bound is claimed.

The [one-coordinate interpolation and rational coefficient anchor](papers/elliptic_divisibility/ico_coefficient_anchor_20261125/README.md) eliminates the second mixed coefficient as a rational function and applies the adaptive scalar height anchor to the input and both coefficients while comparing only the input to the hidden quotient. This gives a fully rational **eight-coordinate sound relation**, with `log B(Z) <= q h²/100+O(1)` and `log(1+|j/h|) <= 3 q h²/25+O(1)` at fixed comparison precision one. It does not require an S-integer predicate, but the numerical budget is not an exact prescribed-support condition. General completeness remains unproved: the reference must be prepared at the support of the actual coefficient-dependent scalar, so interpolation after reference selection is not enough. The same note reduces fixed-reference boundary-controlled lifts to the exact two-term S-unit equation for the union of the allowed primes and the support of `w(1-w)` (including poles), followed by the boundary gcd test. The [generalized-Szpiro obstruction](negative_results/papers/elliptic_divisibility/ico_szpiro_obstruction_20261125.md) proves that a uniform boundary budget at even one arbitrarily chosen reference per unbounded integer multiplier would violate the generalized Szpiro conjecture for abelian surfaces over fields of bounded degree. It also proves under ordinary rational abc a necessary quadratic-in-the-multiplier mass of decomposable good-reduction primes in the indecomposable Torelli locus. Generalized Szpiro is an additional conjecture, not an established consequence of ordinary rational abc; the obstruction is conditional, not an unconditional nonexistence theorem. The [executed checker](scripts/verify_ico_coefficient_anchor_20261125.py) passed 2,240 interpolation and symmetry fixtures, 51,468 local contact fixtures, 125 three-argument anchor fixtures, the exact height margins and ten Thomae multidegrees. No complete defining-variable record changes.

The original-hypothesis **21/23/9-variable targets remain unproved**. For the general routes the missing step is deriving the amplifier mass from the retained hypotheses, or replacing it. For the ordinary-abc route the six-coordinate quotient needs an independent size bound, or a new global pole realization with every physical lift and denominator condition satisfied. Local solubility, a point on an auxiliary cover, and finite searches do not establish these steps.

The [research directions](docs/research_directions.md), [actual-trace criteria](data/criteria_20261117_v64.json), [physical-lift checkpoint](docs/results_20261117_v76.md), and [reference-normalization theorem](docs/results_20261117_v77.md) give precise formulations. [Proved obstructions](negative_results/README.md) exclude specified constructions and specializations; they do not establish a global lower bound on the number of variables.

For number fields, the [definability reductions](papers/number_fields/number_field_definability_20261109_v12.tex) relate the ring of integers, the rational integers and the rational subfield. No counterexample to Diophantine definability for a number field, or theorem covering every number field, is established here.
