## Notes

The established 25-variable general and 193-variable quartic results retain their explicitly stated arithmetic premises and, where applicable, an ineffective sufficient defining parameter. The 21-variable general and 9-variable ordinary-abc sources remain sound but their global completeness is **not proved**. Local solubility and positive real complements do not establish the necessary rational points.

The repository's older summaries may be superseded by the v75–v77 results; prefer the newest precise criteria and manuscripts.
