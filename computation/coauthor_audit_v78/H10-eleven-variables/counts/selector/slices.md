# Fixed-twist witness slices for the intrinsic pole

Fix a prepared $d$ with $1-d$ nonsquare and fix one twist $n$ supplied by the intrinsic pole theorem. Its rational witnesses come from two independently chosen elliptic points on $E_n$ and $E_{n(1-d)}$, where $E_m:y^2=x^3-m^2x$. With the notation of v56, $x(R)=da^2$, $x(R')=db^2$, $e=n/x(R)$ and $s=b/a$. The identities
$$
 U=v^2=d\,\frac{x(R)-n}{x(R)+n},\qquad
 T=s^2\,\frac{d+v^2}{d-v^2}=\frac{x(R')}{n}
$$
show that $(U,T)$ consists of independent nonconstant rational functions on the two factors. These formulas apply on the v56 nontorsion prepared family.

Let $F(U,T)$ be a nonzero polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ with no vertical or horizontal line among its geometric components. Only finitely many of the prepared witness pairs from this **fixed** twist can satisfy $F=0$. Its pullback is a proper union of curves in the product of the two elliptic curves. Mordell–Lang for abelian varieties over number fields says that a component containing infinitely many rational points is a translate of a positive-dimensional abelian subvariety. An irreducible curve with infinitely many rational points is defined over $\mathbb Q$: distinct Galois-conjugate curves have only finitely many intersection points. Its translation stabilizer is consequently a rational elliptic subgroup of the product. If both projections of this subgroup were nonzero it would furnish a rational isogeny between the curves.

Here there is no such isogeny. The two curves are quadratic twists by the nontrivial positive rational square class of $1-d$. A twisting isomorphism is defined over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{1-d})$ and changes sign under its other embedding. All geometric CM endomorphisms of $E_n$ are defined over $\mathbb Q(i)$. The two quadratic fields are distinct, so an automorphism fixing $\mathbb Q(i)$ and changing the sign of $\sqrt{1-d}$ negates every homomorphism between the twists. Thus every rational homomorphism is zero. The only possible rational elliptic subgroups are the separate factors. Their translated images in $(U,T)$ are exactly horizontal and vertical lines, contrary to the hypothesis.

A proposed polynomial relation in the original variables $v,s$ can be tested by taking the norm over their independent sign changes, substituting $v^2=U$, $s^2=T(d-U)/(d+U)$ and clearing denominators. On the prepared chart this is a generically finite map of planes, so the new relation is nonzero. In particular $s=cv$ with any nonzero fixed $c$ gives the nonconstant graph $T=c^2U(d+U)/(d-U)$, hence retains only finitely many witnesses from a fixed twist. The exceptional loci that can retain infinitely many prepared points fix either $v$ up to sign, or $s^2=k(d-v^2)/(d+v^2)$ for constant $k$. This conclusion does not exclude finitely many suitable witnesses per twist or changing the twist with the proposed slice. The file zero-ordinate.md treats the torsion specialization separately with an explicit counterexample.
