## Algebraic restrictions and fresh comparison primes

Let the conditional source use E(K) with scalar quotient P, A=[n]P and guarded auxiliary H and U. Write the free labels of H and U as h and j in half of the integers. The source requires H and U to reduce to O at one place v above a successful odd comparison prime p, and H-P and U-A to reduce to O at its conjugate place v'. Consider any one nontrivial affine rational relation

$$a n+b h+d j=e.$$

Clear denominators to get integer coefficients, doubling the group identity to remove the possible two-torsion. Reduction at the two places gives, respectively,

$$[2(an-e)]P=O\pmod v,\qquad [2((a+d)n+b-e)]P=O\pmod {v'}.$$

If the two displayed indices both vanish, the resulting pair of affine equations has at most one value of n. Indeed, if both polynomials were identically zero, a=e=0 and a+d=b-e=0, contradicting nontriviality. At any other fixed n one of the two fixed points is nonidentity. It reduces to the identity at only finitely many good places. Thus every possible successful prime belongs to a finite set determined by n and the chosen relation. The proof allows h and j to range arbitrarily and does not require j=nh, even for a true input.

For a finite menu, take the union of exceptional n and the union of finite prime sets. By the Mordell–Lang affine-hyperplane criterion in the upstream index-products paper, the same conclusion holds for any fixed proper algebraic subvariety of the three point roles, including restriction of scalars if the cyclic generator is Zariski dense there. To include the two-torsion cosets apply [2] to all point roles first. This is a finite isogeny, carries proper closed subsets to proper closed subsets, and yields integral labels. The native ordered completeness proof allows arbitrary fresh lower bounds after n is fixed. It cannot retain that freedom on such a proper subvariety, even after abandoning exact auxiliary multiplication. This leaves open other proofs using the finite admissible primes or input-dependent subvarieties.

For the general source with several roots replace an by a·n and dj by d·j. The two fixed indices become a·n-e and (a+d)·n+b-e. Given a finite menu, each exceptional root tuple satisfies a nonzero affine equation on the root labels. The source's finite-menu quadratic obstruction excludes all these equations on infinitely many inputs, under its stated hypotheses. For each fixed admissible root tuple on those inputs all the possible primes again lie in a finite set. The argument applies to the ordered fresh-prime interface of the nine-point source.

## Fixed pole square classes and noninteger perturbations

Suppose a source fixes all elliptic witnesses and other base coordinates at a true rational witness over X=n, leaving an external rational input X. Assume its scalar pole inputs are finitely many rational functions b_i(X)=d_i r_i(X)^2, with d_i fixed nonzero rational numbers. At n the functions are defined and nonzero. Assume each pole is realized from the usual open real and finite-place preparation plus square reduced denominator, and every other residual involving X is an open guard, not an additional equation. Include primes dividing each d_i and all exceptional local preparation primes in S.

For p outside S every b_i(X) has even valuation whenever defined and nonzero. Thus the square-denominator condition is automatic there. At primes in S, by continuity and nonvanishing at n one may preserve all valuations and any required unit square classes in a sufficiently small neighborhood. The real preparation is open as well. Weak approximation supplies a nonintegral rational X in all these neighborhoods and away from every excluded root, for example by also prescribing valuation minus one at a fresh prime. Intrinsic-pole realization supplies the remaining rational pole coordinates. Where whole-character guards are required, apply the stated dense realization/avoidance interface, for the fixed surviving base character. Finite auxiliary characters introduce only finitely many additional open exclusions near a good prepared configuration.

In particular, the amplified rational-elliptic index-ratio detector (v60), with only its two rational points and intrinsic pole left free, admits false nonintegral X for each multiplicity T. Keep any of its prepared true witnesses fixed and vary X as above. Here b(X)=4u/(1+u)^2 times W(X)^2 and u is fixed with H. The same conclusion holds after adjoining any fixed finite list of rational comparison functions to W, provided a prepared witness still exists. A second detector whose odd square class depends on the input could evade this argument.

## A restriction on constructing the fixed prime set from split contact fibers

Let C be a smooth projective curve over an algebraically closed field, and let f be a rational function on C×C which depends on both factors. If three distinct fibers of the associated rational map to the projective line have their divisors supported only on vertical and horizontal irreducible curves, then f actually depends on just one factor.

To see this, change target coordinates to make those fibers 0, infinity and 1. Then both div(f) and div(1-f) are sums of vertical and horizontal curves. Any rational function g with this property factors as u(x)v(y). Indeed restrict its divisor to a generic horizontal and a generic vertical section, choose their rational functions (the corresponding restrictions of g), and take their product. Its divisor equals div(g); the quotient is constant on the projective surface. Consequently f=u(x)v(y) and 1-f=r(x)s(y). The identity 1=u⊗v+r⊗s in the tensor product of the two function spaces says that a sum of two tensors has rank one. If u,r and v,s were each linearly independent, that sum would have rank two. Thus u,r are proportional, or v,s are proportional; substituting in the identity then forces a function of one variable on each side to be constant, and f depends only on one factor. In particular a genuinely conjugation-symmetric f on the split Weil restriction cannot have three such fibers.

This rules out a particular replacement for arbitrary fresh primes. Trying to force each of three or more contact fibers of a single rational base parameter to consist entirely of reductions of prescribed constant elliptic multiples in one of the two slots cannot work geometrically. It does not exclude finite irregular subsets of the arithmetic reduction locus or arguments using more than one function on C×C.
