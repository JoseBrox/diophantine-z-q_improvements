## Soundness

Its simple-prime part obeys exactly the same lower bound as its odd-prime part: at order e=1 the inequality 2≤e+1 is an equality, and at e≥2 the inequality 2≤e holds. Choose the larger-height slot t=x or y. Discard simple primes where the other contact product has order at least K. Their logarithmic sum is at most (6/K)h(t)+O(1) by clearing the six homogeneous linear factors of the opposite slot. At a retained simple prime in the x-slot, the other argument is integral or has valuation s<0. In the integral case m=v(A)=1, n=v(B')≥0. If n=0, v(D)=0 for the denominator D.
