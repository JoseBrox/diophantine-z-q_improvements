# Attribution of the supplied v78 research notes

The notes supplied as `H10-quartic-constructions.zip` and
`H10-eleven-variable-constructions.zip` are **notes of Gabe Conant with
Astra 6.2**. This is the attribution specified by the project contributor
who supplied them. It replaces the nonspecific word “coauthor” in the
integration prose; it does not attribute the subsequent audit work to the
source authors.

The supplied contributions include the reductions **193→165, 229→180,
68→62 and 13→11**, shared detector products and gates, positive-factor
finite-algebra encodings, and the associated proof and realization notes.
Their original files are preserved in `H10-quartics/` and
`H10-eleven-variables/`. `ORIGINAL_SHA256SUMS` in each directory preserves
the original archive's checksum list. Those source bytes are unchanged.

The subsequent independent reconstruction, strengthened local extraction,
smaller explicit anchor, constant-free conditional gates, coefficient
normalizations, full auxiliary charts, four-contact scalar and first-scale
twelve-contact construction are project extensions recorded separately in
`work_general/`, `work_abc/`, `work_weak11/` and `work_root/`. Their proofs
identify which supplied construction they use.

Historical directory names, JSON schema identifiers, command-line options
and bibliography keys containing `coauthor` are stable technical
identifiers. Their spelling is retained for exact reproduction; it does
not replace this named attribution. The earlier 198-variable archive has
its own original Gabriel Conant and ChatGPT source notice and is a
separate contribution.
