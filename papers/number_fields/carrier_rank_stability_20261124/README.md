# Direct transfer with rank stability of the original carrier

## Conditional theorem

Let L0=Q(sqrt(2)), E/L0:y^2=x^3-(11-6sqrt(2))x, with the original v110 hypothesis 2E(L0)=Z P and its trace-height, comparison, simultaneous prime-pattern and recurrence inputs. Let K be a number field linearly disjoint from L0. Assume ordinary abc over K, the BB rational prepared-pole realization, and the additional hypothesis

    rank E(K L0) = rank E(L0) = 1.

Then Z^k in K^k has an integer-coefficient polynomial definition in **3k+8** total K-coordinates, including the k inputs. In particular Z inside K uses **11** coordinates and O_K in degree d uses **3d+8** coordinates (14 in a quadratic field satisfying these hypotheses). The sufficient shift is ineffective. This is an alternative to the [54/69 theorem](../ambient_selective_detector_20261124/README.md): it replaces the general stable-rank hypothesis on some E/Q by the stronger, different rank-stability assumption on the particular auxiliary carrier over L0. It does not follow from the previous general stable-rank theorem and is not asserted for all quadratic fields. The disjointness assumption is essential to this proof.

## Proof

By finite generation and rank equality, E(K L0)/2E(L0) is a finite abelian group. Choose a fixed positive integer M annihilating it. Then M E(K L0) is a subgroup of 2E(L0)=Z P, of positive rank one. Thus there exists a fixed positive integer c for which M E(K L0)=Z P_*=Z [c]P. The finite existence of M and c suffices; neither is a quantified polynomial coordinate.

Use the ambient selective detector of the companion note, but replace its k+2 rational point charts by arbitrary affine preimages Q_i,Q_H,Q_U on E over K L0, applying the fixed multiplication map [M]. Their images A_i,H,U automatically lie in Z P_*. In particular every image is rational over L0, without a separate rationality selector. Because K and L0 are linearly disjoint, the unique expression of an image coordinate as a+b sqrt(2), a,b in K, has a,b in Q whenever the image lies in L0. This is false without the disjointness hypothesis: the rank-two algebra would split and its two slots could then choose unrelated images.

Retain two ordinary K-components of the abscissa of each preimage, adjoining the two ordinate components via the same rank-four monic square-root algebra as in v110, with j^2=x^3-Dx in place of j^2=h^4-D. Compute [M] by its fixed guarded division-polynomial formulas over L0. The affine image and comparison guards exclude identity images and zero denominators. Every nonzero multiple of P_* has an affine preimage by the definition of the image subgroup; no preimage of a nonzero point is the identity. Thus the representation is exact at the selected characters and complete for every nonzero point of the subgroup. Guard the actual formulas for the image coordinates and all field inverses by their rank-two norms. The norm and trace operations are the same rational-coefficient operations as in the ambient proof.

In the raw comparison tests replace P by P_*. The other point and target tests and the new eighth-power height tree are unchanged. Write A_i=[n_i]P_*, H=[h]P_*, U=[j]P_*. The first four formal conditions give the same logarithmic ratios j/h and (j-n_i)/(h-1) because the nonzero logarithm of P_* cancels. (Equivalently use a common multiple in the formal group.) The argument of v22 and the companion proof therefore gives Y_i congruent to n_i at every active prime of K. The old rational trace-height inequality for [c n_i]P bounds (c n_i)^2, hence also n_i^2, by A h(T_i)+J with the same constants. Thus the full ambient-field height contradiction applies: Y_i=X_i+c_i=n_i, where the finite shift c_i in {0,1} is distinct from the fixed subgroup index c.

For integer input completeness, choose n_i=X_i+c_i!=0 and A_i=[n_i]P_*. Make the original v110 ordered real and finite-place preparations with this fixed multiple P_*. All finite point exclusions are still finitely many labels: H=0,+/-P_*, U=0,+/-A_i. The two-index CRT lemma uses annihilator moduli of the original P, not a new prime supplier for P_*. If the prescribed original multiple [h]P (or [h-1]P) is formal at a prime, its c-multiple is also formal. The corresponding property of [j]P or [j-n_i]P likewise transfers by multiplication by c. The exact formal ratios on the new cyclic subgroup are unchanged. Prescribe the old compatible CRT congruences for h,j using P, discarding the fixed primes dividing c if desired. The finite-neighborhood/real recurrence step applies to the new cyclic subgroup since P_* is non-torsion: choose safe indices in the multiples of 64 and use continuity, exactly as the common-numerator proof. The bounded-neighborhood suppression of the raw tests and the class-seven local preparation depend only on the fixed positive rational scale a=M_height^4, not on the chosen generator. The BB product-pole theorem then supplies rational terminal witnesses for the actual rational pole input. For the point charts choose any preimages in E(K L0), which need not be rational over L0. All other arithmetic output data are in L0 or Q as required. This establishes completeness without a rational-point approximation assumption on a new curve.

The coefficient recipe is exactly the ambient selective detector with the fixed multiplication map inserted after each rank-four preimage point algebra, the changed comparison point P_* (fixed in L0), and all its rationality tests omitted. All operations on L0 elements have rational coefficients in the two-component basis; after clearing fixed rational denominators the final selected-character norm/determinant polynomial has integer coefficients. Use the finite Hankel-rank/selected-character encoder over K, not a sum of squares when K is totally imaginary. Its one coordinate encodes every point ordinate and terminal root, and all selected guards. The ordinary count is

    k + 2(k+2) + 1 slope + 2 pole parameters + 1 encoder = 3k+8.

The integral-basis substitution gives the ring of integers with coefficients in O_K and the same total count. No new variable represents the subgroup index or multiplication map. All coefficients have a finite prescription in fixed B, M, c, and the source data; no effective numerical B cutoff or uniform bound on M,c is asserted.

## Limits and research direction

This shows exactly which additional rank theorem would remove the entire cost of rationality descent in the ordinary-abc route. It is not enough to know that some elliptic curve over Q has stable positive rank over K. One needs rank stability for this specific E over L0 in the compositum, or a different carrier for which the group, local comparison and compatible prime-pattern inputs are reestablished. For a quadratic K disjoint from L0 this condition is equivalent to rank zero of the corresponding quadratic twist of E over L0. We have not proved that rank-zero theorem for all such quadratic twists, and it need not hold for every K. The fully specified non-abc sources have different index-anchor and height constraints; replacing their generator requires a separate proof and their explicit cutoff is not transferred by this argument.
