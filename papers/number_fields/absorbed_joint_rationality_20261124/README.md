# Absorbing a polynomial witness into the joint rationality encoder

## Theorem

Fix a number field K satisfying the stable-positive-rank elliptic-curve hypothesis, the six-point truncated-height bound of v81 (in particular ordinary abc over K suffices), and the class-seven pole completeness input. Keep any arithmetic hypotheses of the integer definition being transferred.

If an integer-coefficient polynomial in N rational coordinates, with d distinguished inputs and at least one existential coordinate, has input projection Z^d over Q, then Z^d has a definition over K in **7N+2 total coordinates**, with integer coefficients. Its witnesses can be rational on rational integer inputs. No effective correction or degree claim independent of the v81 correction is made.

In particular the 11-coordinate integer definition gives **79** coordinates for Z inside K. With the joint ordinary-abc integer definition of [v110](../../../undecidability/abc_selective_pole_v110/README.md), N=3d+8; expressing the distinguished element of K in an integral basis gives **21d+58** coordinates for O_K over K, with coefficients in O_K. For quadratic K this is **100** (previously 220, then 157). For the latter statement retain ordinary rational abc and all the arithmetic hypotheses in v110; the quadratic rank-stability input is the same cited theorem as in v81. These are number-field counts, not quartic bounds.

## 1. A selected-character encoder over arbitrary number fields

Let R=Z[X_1,...,X_l], let A be a finite free commutative unital R-algebra of rank r with an explicit basis and polynomial multiplication matrices, generated by theta_1,...,theta_m. Let g in A be the product of a finite list of guards. The desired condition after specialization to K is existence of a K-algebra character chi with chi(g)!=0. No reducedness or all-character nonvanishing is assumed.

Over an algebraic closure of K, the finitely many distinct characters of A number at most r. For guarded characters the vector

    (chi(g), chi(g theta_1), ..., chi(g theta_m))

is nonzero, and distinct guarded characters give distinct vectors (divide by their first component). Add the zero vector. For each h in the fixed integer menu

    1 <= h <= m*binom(r+1,2)+1

put a_h=g*(1+sum_{i=1}^m h^i theta_i). Every pair of distinct vectors excludes at most m values of h, by the nonzero polynomial of degree at most m obtained by taking the difference. Hence some h separates all guarded characters from one another and from zero. Unguarded characters always have a_h value zero.

Let B_h=diag(M_{a_h},0), of size r+1, and form the Hankel matrix H_h with entries Tr(B_h^(i+j)) for 0<=i,j<=r, including B_h^0=I. Its rank equals the number of distinct geometric eigenvalues of B_h. To prove this, extend scalars to an algebraic closure, triangularize B_h, and denote its distinct eigenvalues by lambda_j and their positive integer algebraic multiplicities by n_j. Then H_h=V diag(n_j) V^t, with V_{ij}=lambda_j^i. The Vandermonde matrix V has full column rank, and its transpose is surjective. Since the field has characteristic zero, diag(n_j) is invertible. This proves the rank assertion even for nonreduced algebras.

Consequently the maximum rank over the menu is exactly one plus the number of guarded geometric characters. A maximum-rank h separates them all from zero and from each other.

Use a finite disjunction over h, integers s=1,...,r+1, and s-by-s minors Delta of H_h. For that branch impose all (s+1)-minors of every H_j equal to zero (no such minors for s=r+1), and

    det(I_r - Z Delta M_{a_h})=0.

The last equation implies Delta!=0, so the rank of H_h is at least s and the rank-upper equations make h a maximizer. It also gives a nonzero eigenvalue lambda=1/(Z Delta) in K. The eigenvalues of a multiplication matrix are precisely its values at geometric characters, repeated with local-algebra multiplicities; this follows by decomposing the finite algebra into its Artin local factors and using nilpotence of their maximal ideals. Since lambda!=0 it corresponds to a guarded character, and the maximizing choice makes this character unique. Galois invariance of lambda implies invariance of that character, so its values lie in K. Conversely if a guarded K-character exists, choose a separating h and nonzero maximal minor, then Z=1/(Delta chi(a_h)) is in K and all equations hold. If all base values and the selected character are rational, this Z is rational.

To encode a finite conjunction of s0 polynomial equalities without introducing coordinates, choose a rational prime p unramified in K and use the norm form of the power basis of K[root_{s0}(p)]/K, where X^s0-p is Eisenstein at every prime above p. Its norm vanishes precisely when all s0 residuals vanish. For s0=1 the identity suffices. Its coefficients are integers, since the norm is the determinant of the integral companion multiplication matrix. Encode the finite disjunction by the product of the branch polynomials. This works over totally imaginary K as well, unlike a sum of squares. The procedure is finite; no numerical efficiency claim is made. Additional branch equalities in the ordinary coordinates can be included in the same conjunction.

## 2. Monic absorption of one existential coordinate

Write the given polynomial as P(w)=sum_{i=0}^D a_i w^i in any one existential coordinate w, with coefficients in the remaining N-1 coordinates. For each possible positive specialized degree j impose a_i=0 for i>j, and add a_j to the selected guard. Adjoin the root W of the monic polynomial

    W^j + sum_{i=0}^{j-1} a_i a_j^(j-1-i) W^i.

On the selected guard, W=a_j w is a bijection of roots, since this polynomial is a_j^(j-1) P(W/a_j) on the degree-j stratum. For the degree-zero case impose every a_i=0 and take w=0 as a constant. The other constant stratum has no solutions. This covers degree drops, identically zero fibers, and repeated roots; no generic-finiteness assumption on the original projection is needed.

Use the [joint rationality construction](../joint_rationality_20261124/README.md) for the entire list of N original coordinates, including w=W/a_j (or 0 on the constant-zero stratum). In this construction retain the two shared denominator point abscissas, the 2N numerator point abscissas, and the 4N ordinary pole parameters. Adjoin the point ordinate roots to the monic algebra, using their original polynomial square relations, then the 2N pole roots. Every rational expression in the original selected guard and the pole equations is calculated in numerator-denominator form in the preceding algebra. Retain all denominators as selected guards. For a radicand A/B, adjoin V^2=AB and recover the actual square root V/B on B!=0. This gives a free monic tower over the N-1 ordinary coordinates and the retained abscissas and pole parameters. Clear fixed rational constants by scaling the finite generators, or work over Q and clear constant denominators in the final polynomial. The coefficient recipe is finite.

Use the actual v81 input and point-chart guards, including H(t_i), all map denominators, and the nonzero prepared pole inputs. One may impose the full other-character norms and radicand guards of the joint rationality proof on the point/pole subalgebra at the selected original inputs; alternatively retain only the guards for the chosen point character, as the original local extraction theorem uses only these. For soundness of the latter choice, the v81 source theorem gives rationality from the two recovered pole equations and H(t_i)!=0; no all-character determinant hypothesis enters that arithmetic implication. For completeness the preceding joint rationality proof supplies rational witnesses and satisfies even its stronger guards when the original tuple is rational. No guard is imposed at other roots of the original P.

Apply Section 1 to the full finite algebra, incorporating the ordinary stratum equalities. A K-valued guarded character recovers an original root w and rationality witnesses for all N original coordinates. By v81 all those coordinates are rational. Therefore P vanishes over Q on that tuple, and the d inputs lie in Z. Conversely any integer input tuple has a rational solution of P. Choose its degree stratum and rational W, then choose rational joint rationality witnesses by the previous theorem. The resulting guarded character is rational. Section 1 supplies the final encoder. In the zero-polynomial stratum w=0 is legitimate and rational, regardless of which rational w was used originally.

The count is N-1 ordinary original coordinates + 2N+2 point abscissas + 4N pole parameters + 1 final encoder = **7N+2**. The old polynomial witness and both systems' finite generators do not count as ordinary coordinates. All d distinguished inputs remain ordinary coordinates.

## 3. Ring of integers

Take an integral basis 1,omega_2,...,omega_d of O_K (1 extends to a Z-basis because O_K/Z is torsion-free). With a definition of Z^d, replace its first input n_1 by T-sum_{i=2}^d n_i omega_i, leaving n_2,...,n_d as existential coordinates. The resulting polynomial vanishes for precisely those T that are integer linear combinations of this basis, with the same total coordinate count. This is a polynomial over O_K. For v110 the census N=3d+8 gives 7N+2=21d+58. At d=2 this is 100; for the single Z definition N=11 gives 79. If using d independent 11-coordinate definitions instead, one can monically absorb one existential coordinate of each, with independent strata and one combined guarded algebra, giving 7*11*d-d+3 rather than 7*11*d+3. This is inferior to v110 at d>=2.

## Scope

This is a transfer improvement. It does not reduce the 11-coordinate polynomial over Q itself, the single-input 10-coordinate rational-subfield definition, or any quartic count. It uses no additional rank or pole-density hypothesis. The selected-character argument is a finite algebraic proof; the companion checker tests identities, rank and counts, not the inherited arithmetic statements. Attribution: v81 rationality descent, Bhargava–Burungale pole input, v110 simultaneous integer selector, and the earlier joint denominator construction are retained.
