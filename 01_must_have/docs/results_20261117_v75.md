## 1. The even-valuation lemma

The curve is

```math
E:y^2=x^3-Dx,\qquad D=11-6\sqrt2,\qquad N(D)=49.
```

At an integer-valued valuation with `v(D)=0`, a nonzero abscissa has even valuation. If `m=v(x)>0`, then `2v(y)=m`. If `m<0`, then `2v(y)=3m`. The case `m=0` is immediate. This covers every guarded affine point, including the full auxiliary domain, without assuming that its abscissa is a global square.

Outside the retained exceptional primes 2 and 7, it applies to all reciprocal-abscissa tests and their conjugates. No new exceptional prime is introduced.

### The derivative source

For the first four tests, replace the old balancing by

```math
D_i=\frac{C_i}{1+uC_i}\quad(1\le i\le4).
```

The two comparison tests retain `C_i^2/(1+u*C_i^2)`. If `nu=v(u)` is odd and `m=v(C_i)` is even, then `nu+m` cannot vanish and

```math
v(D_i)=m-\min(0,\nu+m)=\max(m,-\nu).
```

This preserves the positive-odd extraction and automatically controls new negative odd valuations of the physical weight. The implementation uses direct inverse-abscissa formulas, while the native checker evaluates the distinct displayed rational expression. The original denominator and norm guards are equivalent on the retained domain.

The complete witness construction still uses its original `2E(K)` subfamily inside the enlarged auxiliary domain. Its points and differences have positive abscissae at every prepared real character. Thus all required real and whole-character guards survive. The finite algebra retains rank 128, 32 recorded guards and 38 norm factors; the revised circuit has 1,865 arithmetic nodes.

```math

```

The six actual tests are `c,c,conjugate(c),conjugate(c),0,0`, where `c=sqrt(2)-1`. This is a calculation at actual elliptic points, not an independently chosen trace.

For the even-valuation balancing put

```math
\begin{aligned}
D(u)&=u^2(1-2u-u^2)^2,\\
R(u)&=D(u)+2u^3+4u^2+6u+1,\\
\Pi(u)&=4D(u)^4(1+u)^2+2uR(u)^2(R(u)^2-D(u)^2).
```

The detector identities are `W=(R/D)^2` and `V=2D^2/R^2`. After clearing a square denominator, the actual trace test becomes `Y0^2=Pi(u)`. The polynomial has degree 26 and is squarefree, hence the weight curve has genus 12.

## 4. A common slope from the coupled physical weights

Fix the general source's nonzero rational scalar `rho` and positive rational scale `a`. With its ten positive rational contacts and `P(t)` their monic product, the physical pair is

```math
u_0(\lambda)=\rho\frac{P(\lambda)}{(\lambda+1)^{10}},\qquad
u_1(\lambda)=\rho\frac{P(a\lambda-4)}{(a\lambda+2)^{10}}.
```

The [coupled-weight theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/coupled_weight_lifting_20261117_v74.tex) proves

```math
\mathbf Q(u_0,u_1)=\mathbf Q(\lambda)
=\mathbf Q\left(\frac{u_0}{(1+u_0)^2},\frac{u_1}{(1+u_1)^2}\right).
```

For distinct poles, a nontrivial common composition would be a power map in a coordinate sending the two poles to zero and infinity. The positive rational contact set rules out every power of degree greater than one. At the coincident scale `a=2`, an exact derivative-coprimality certificate proves the same conclusion. The normalized result uses the reciprocal quadratic branches and the incompatibility of a single pole of order ten with ten simple zeros under a fractional linear transformation.

For a proposed rational pair `(v0,v1)`, take the polynomial gcd of

```math
\rho P(t)-v_0(t+1)^{10},\qquad
\rho P(at-4)-v_1(at+2)^{10}.
```

A linear gcd gives the unique finite rational lift, subject to the original poles. A constant gcd rejects the pair. A higher-degree exceptional gcd requires rational-root testing; birationality does not by itself resolve every special fiber. The normalized pair has an equally exact degree-at-most-20 test, or can be lifted through at most four reciprocal branch pairs.

The [exact package](../computation/pole_parameters/general21_recovery_v74) includes independent modular Bezout verification, positive lift fixtures and mismatched-pair rejection. Its example scalar and scale are algebraic tests, not prepared elliptic witnesses. The physical scalar is fixed by the input and coefficient parameter, and `a=1+rho*T^2` is determined by the root points; neither is an extra free coordinate.
