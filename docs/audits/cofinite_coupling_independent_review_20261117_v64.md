# Independent review of the cofinite coupling theorem

The full proof was checked against the published twelve-symmetry theorem and its exact inert contacts.

For each of the ten quadratic branches, the earlier square gap gives a nonzero rational nonsquare at the prescribed input one. Such a number is nonsquare at some finite prime: an odd valuation suffices, and the remaining negative-square case is excluded at three. Local square classes are open, using 1+8 Z_2 at two. Choosing neighborhoods at every place above each rational prime makes the rational trace continuous, also at split primes. Denominators need only be nonzero at the starting point; their valuations can be preserved by shrinking those neighborhoods.

The decisive recurrence statement uses small open subgroups of the local elliptic groups, followed by their finite index. Compactness by itself would not justify persistence for every later multiple. The subgroup argument works at bad reduction and at two and produces one integer period preserving all finitely many input and point neighborhoods. At the selected good inert primes, preserving the scale modulo p^2 preserves valuation exactly one and all relevant units. The original inert-pole contradiction is uniform in the other auxiliary points because its reference translator remains P.

For every positive integer n, the root [n]P is a nonzero double. Its abscissa is positive at both real embeddings, and the trace exceeds six. This excludes the identity branch for every exponent in the stated menu, including odd exponents. The ten nonsquare branches and the remaining inert branch therefore fail on one infinite positive-input progression.

The uniform coefficient refinement is valid: take product neighborhoods in the coefficient, input and point at one obstructed starting tuple, then impose a finer coefficient congruence and the common elliptic/input period. No compactness statement over an unbounded real coefficient set is needed; the real positivity bound holds separately for every positive input and every allowed coefficient.

Finally, finitely many input-only factors add only finitely many rational inputs. They cannot fill the omitted arithmetic progression. The theorem requires the exact root A=[n]P; equality only modulo unspecified torsion does not supply this hypothesis. The conclusion remains limited to the obstructed coefficient progressions and this contact architecture. It establishes neither an all-shift obstruction nor a minimum variable count.
