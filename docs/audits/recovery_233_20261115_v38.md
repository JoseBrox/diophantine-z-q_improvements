# Recovery of the two reattached v36 archives

Both reattached ZIPs were opened and every member inventoried and hashed.
They are byte-identical to the earlier copies. The repository archive has
11 members; the TeX archive has three. Neither contains the claimed
233-variable proof, the effective torsion-height proof, or the 233-variable
quadratic/coefficient tables.

Both delivery receipts say `RECOVERY_NOT_CLOSED`, with no commands,
no candidate worktrees, no successful clean-checkout verification and no
remote publication. Their blocker is explicit: no working tree containing
the 233-variable proof and manifest was found. The only mathematical TeX
file is the general guarded-monomial elimination criterion, not an
application proving 233 variables. One supplied search script also has a
syntax error and its replay file records no search receipt.

The prior conversational assertion of 233 variables, N=10220, 3352
monomials and 148258 binary coefficient digits is therefore withdrawn as
an unsupported result, not entered into the theorem table. No claim is
made that a 233-variable construction is impossible. The precise source
inventory and receipt contents are in the companion JSON file.

Recovered valid inputs are handled separately: the uploaded 198-variable
archive was reproduced through all thirteen supplied commands; the
78-variable family was independently reconstructed. The new 196- and
258-variable results are proved and checked directly from those pinned
parents, not inferred from missing v36 output. The 258-variable polynomial
retains N=9682 and has 139819 binary digits in its largest coefficient.

The v34 manuscript's escaped-control-character typo before `boldsymbol`
was replaced by the literal LaTeX command. No mathematical formula was
changed. All earlier byte-pinned parent files remain unchanged.
