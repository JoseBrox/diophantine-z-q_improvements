### Findings and repairs

**S1 — No essential flaw established in the reconstructed reduction.** This is a scoped outcome, not a complete verification of the underlying arithmetic theorem. A missing recursive source check is recorded as such, not classified as a mathematical disproof.

**S2 — Bibliographic version ambiguity.** Reference [10] gives an unversioned arXiv source whose successive versions have different titles, counts and numbering. The theorem applications should be bound to a precise version and exact statements. This is a repairable citation/application issue unless comparing the intended source reveals a genuinely weaker theorem. We have not inferred such a failure merely from the version ambiguity.

**S3 — Potential misuse of the product-height statement is ruled out.** It is false for unrestricted nonzero indices. The pair P,2P has exact defect log(41). Sun imposes oddness, so this is not a counterexample to his lemma or theorem. The new exact criterion identifies the full valid index range.

**S4 — Precision is unnecessarily high for the final inequalities.** Under the same inputs, orders 49,13,25 suffice. The full proof, including the local and real completeness checks for the changed normalizers, is in the precision paper. The degree-64 logarithm is retained. No numerical degree or coefficient improvement is claimed without compiling the changed source.

## PR30: the changed mathematical arguments

### Algebra and local valuations (P03-P07)

The source uses beta=51x^2+2504, c=|a|, the reference curve over Q(sqrt(2)), and the quarter-trace of a **sum** of four abscissas. These are not Sun's beta, scalar index or product scale. The q-inverse equations remove q=0 and do not claim a bijection on that discarded stratum. The argument t=1, where q=-1, remains allowed; contact points are excluded algebraically before division.

For every positive odd contact depth nu, the generic factor terms have disjoint valuation classes: odd, 2 modulo 4, and 4 modulo 8. In the special factor the two odd classes are -nu and nu modulo 4. Zero tests are harmless. The exact checker verifies all residue classes for odd nu modulo 8, which proves the congruence classification; arbitrary valuations with those residues are covered by the written minimum argument.

The trace/pole inequality gives ell>-nu/2 at a maximizing place. It supplies order at least three for the deep comparison and Lucas tests, positive order for the input test, and positive even reciprocal-abscissa valuations. Integrality precedes the formal-logarithm congruences. Half-integral reference indices cause no division by 2 problem at consumed odd primes. The resulting M_j^3|Delta and M_j|J are separate divisibilities, without an extra scalar factor or assumed coprimality between scales.

### Aggregate height and uniform soundness (P08-P12)

The sum argument is not invalidated by the mixed-valuation product counterexample. The proposed geometric proof excludes constant translates using positive same-block pole coefficients and independence of the two conjugate generating points. The passage from this condition to the published family-height theorem, its exact embedding hypothesis and all source hypotheses still requires a complete primary-source review before whole-theorem acceptance; reading the application note is not a substitute.

At the stated-premise level, the exponent calculation and quantifiers are correct. The full-family supplier gives alpha=2103/1024. Two disjoint blocks give alpha/2. The published note weakens alpha to 7/3, obtaining Lucas exponent 14/9. Keeping alpha gives 701/512, and the input-height coefficient improves from 7/5 to 2103/1993. The sufficient-threshold power becomes 512/323 instead of 9/4. These are proved algebraic consequences of the same supplier, not new verification of the supplier. They neither change the 214 coordinates nor make the threshold effective.

### Completeness and the base field (P13-P20)

The scalar real estimate is uniform in the integer input because r4>=1 and R_s>=A_N/(1+149^2). Later point labels, moduli, primes and witnesses may depend on N and x; this does not alter the quantifier order exists N0, for every N>=N0, for every x. The changed factors have the stated positive depths at selected primes. Elsewhere the even number of contacts makes each scale's contribution a square, including nonsquare scales. The real q>0 witnesses satisfy the inverse-weight equations.

The pole realization consumes the stated non-CM family with j=35152/9, not the CM curve used for integer labels. Full Selmer corank is needed before any finite-Shafarevich conclusion. This audit does not certify the recursive restricted-converse, Kato or descent foundations. Those are the main remaining source obligations, together with the Gaussian prime-selection and modular-height chains.

### Height averaging

The application selects r separate occurrences of the A-isogeny class over the actual field L', not merely the set of distinct isogeny classes and not an auxiliary evaluation field. Height is additive on the actual product. The small isogeny is then applied to a dimension-two factor in the direction factor-to-A, retaining its height cost. Negative heights do not invalidate the averaging.

A useful sharpening is h_F(C)<=(h_F(J)+3g/2)/r-3. It follows by retaining the dimension g-2r of the complementary product. This is an elementary improvement of the average, not a substitute for the existence of the fixed-field product or the proof of multiplicity. The companion precision paper states the general lemma with all hypotheses.
