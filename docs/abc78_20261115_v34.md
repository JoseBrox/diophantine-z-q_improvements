# One fewer variable in the ordinary-abc quartic

The new construction has **78 total variables**, including the input, and
**70 quadratic equations**. Its sum of squares has exact degree four and
integer content one: the input occurs only in X^2-z2, so its fourth-power
coefficient is exactly one.

The integer target is X^2+3. The large coefficient shift is moved into
B+X^8+T^2, where T is the trace of the recovered elliptic difference.
A parity-sensitive abc argument gives h(X) <= (301/388) h(X^8+T^2)+O(1)
and h(T) <= (301/97) h(X^8+T^2)+O(1). The eight-contact consequence of
ordinary abc gives the strict absorption coefficient 602/679. All constants
in the soundness argument are independent of B.

Completeness uses the same fixed-data prime theorem with the contact lists
Theta and Theta-a, with a=B+X^8+T^2 fixed before prime selection. The local
point, reference, detector and pole equations are unchanged. The input zero
is included by the target 3; no additional factor or coordinate is needed.

The sufficient odd B remains ineffective. The general counts 25 and 13,
the inherited-premise quartic count 200, and the explicit quartic count 260
are not reduced by this result. Finite verification and independent
mathematical acceptance are separate.
