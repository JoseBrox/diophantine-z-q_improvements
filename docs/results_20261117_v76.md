## Contact alignment and the physical normalizers

For the general source, `a=1+rho*T^2` is determined by the root traces.
The relation `lambda=4/(rho*T^2)` makes `a*lambda=lambda+4`, so the two
contact numerators agree. With `s=(lambda+1)/(lambda+6)`, one has
`u1=s^10*u0` and
`w1/w0=(s^5*(1+u0)/(1+s^10*u0))^2`. Thus both weight pairs, and both
actual pole inputs when their traces are nonzero, have the same
rational square class.

The physical denominators remain different. Their unnormalized ratio
is `s^8`; the normalized ratio is
`(s^4*(1+u0)/(1+s^10*u0))^2`. Both are strictly less than one on the
stated domain. The exact normalized equality threshold is greater
than `2/3`, while `u0<rho<2/3`. The
[complete proof](../papers/pole_parameters/contact_alignment_20261117_v76.tex)
and [independent convolution certificate](../computation/pole_parameters/general21_alignment_v75)
distinguish this coefficient obstruction from accidental equality of
full detector products.

Fixing the aligned slope also changes the order of prime preparation.
The inherited theorem does not supply that new arithmetic realization.
Matching an input square class does not match the complement square
class or furnish the actual trace-square conditions.

## Native witnesses force ramification

The intrinsic and general native pole equations exclude negative odd
orders over the geometric discrete valued fields used in the proof.
Consequently, if a finite degree-d curve cover of the physical slope
line carries native witnesses, every ramification index over an odd
contact pole is even. With r distinct such poles,
`g >= 1+d*(r-4)/4`; in particular d is even.

## Prepared abstract products and adaptive scalar criteria

The two classes `Delta(q,V)=(q^2-2q-1)^2+4*q*(q^2-1)*V`, with
`q=2+w*c^2`, and `Lambda(c)=1+w^2*V^4*c^4` preserve the desired
local pole implication. Neither is complete on all prepared positive
native products, even with the added rational scalar c unrestricted.

Two exact products at p=100003 have square reduced pole denominators,
the intrinsic-pole preparation, the older conditions at 2, 3 and 17,
and both real slots close to one. In the first, V is congruent to
one and the trace radicand is a nonsquare unit. The factor Delta is

has valuation -1 while every Lambda has even valuation. The universal
quantifiers follow from proofs over the local field, not a finite
search. Preparation can be strengthened at any fixed finite set of
primes away from the obstruction prime.

These are abstract native products. Their h and u values have not
been realized by the actual elliptic test tuple and physical contact

and [independent exact package](../negative_results/computation/pole_parameters/adaptive_prepared_pairs_v76)
retain that distinction. The second square equation is a genus-one
quartic with a prescribed abscissa class on an explicit isogenous
congruent-number curve; a rank statement alone does not recover that
class.

## The remaining 21/9 assertion

The defining coefficient parameter must be fixed first at one
sufficient value. For every required integer input, a proof must
then supply actual elliptic source points and a rational physical
slope, retain the source scales and every original and whole-character
guard, and make the actual trace-square radicand rationally square.
The general source requires the two square conditions simultaneously
at the same slope. The ordinary-abc source requires its single actual
square condition. The chosen completeness route must also justify
its real, fixed-place and selected-contact conditions.

A rational point on a free trace conic or on a weight cover does not
prove this assertion. The physical slope equations must be solved,
and an intermediate rational squared slope must itself be a rational
square. The exact coupled-weight lifting theorem in v74 checks this
issue without replacing the actual traces by free variables.

The complete coordinate counts therefore remain 25 and 11. The
[machine-readable realization checkpoint](../data/criteria_20261117_v76.json)
keeps the 21/9 assertions false as proved-result flags, and separately
records that none of the new obstructions disproves global realization.
