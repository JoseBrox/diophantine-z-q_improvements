## A sharper height theorem

The [signed mixed-power theorem](../papers/elliptic_heights/signed_mixed_power_height_20261117_v58.tex) assumes the weak rational abc inequality with exponent $\kappa>1$ and constant $K_\kappa$. For even $d$ with $\epsilon=d-\kappa(d/2+2)>0$, it proves

$$
h(x)\le\frac{2\kappa-1}{\epsilon}\,h(x^d\pm y^2)
       +\frac{\log K_\kappa}{\epsilon},
\qquad x^d\pm y^2\ne0.
$$

Both signs and arbitrary rational denominators are covered. The proof clears the minimal common square denominator and retains the square common divisor of the three terms. This controls cancellation without multiplying by the full $d$th power of the output denominator.

For the actual conditional single-cover source, put

$$
\beta=X^2+B,\quad T=\operatorname{Tr}x(A),\quad
H_0=\beta^e+T^e,\quad t=H_0^d+z^2.
$$

The point chart gives $T=2(h_0^2+2h_1^2)>0$, independently of the finite ordinate choices. Thus the inner exponent $e$ may be odd. With $\alpha=e/\kappa-3$, $\gamma=8/\kappa-6$, and $q=(2\kappa-1)/(\epsilon\alpha\gamma)$, the criterion is

$$
q(k+1/2)<1,
$$

where $k=0$ for the fixed exceptional-unit class menu and $k=2$ for the permitted varying menu $\{1,\beta^2+T^2,1+X^4\}$.

## Separate obstructions

The [actual multiplication-surface theorem](../negative_results/papers/rational_fibers/actual_multiplication_surface_20261117_v58.tex) fixes any nonzero integer $n$ and imposes $A=[n]P$, $U=[n]H$ before allowing the slope to be any nonconstant rational function of $H$. On the full restriction-of-scalars surface, none of the stated untwisted or safely twisted trace tests can then be a rational-function square. An odd contact divisor forces an odd divisor of the square test. If all contact divisors were even, six fibers on a generic elliptic curve would require at least $3m$ ramification, while Riemann–Hurwitz permits $2m$.

This excludes a uniform rational identity on that full surface. It leaves **individual rational witnesses, constant slopes, proper subfamilies and ramified covers** open, and does not disprove either target count.

The [two-square height obstruction](../negative_results/papers/contact_heights/two_square_height_20261117_v58.tex) shows that no unrestricted bound $h(M)\le C h(M^d+z_1^2+z_2^2)+K$ holds when $d$ is a power of two. An explicit rational family keeps the output fixed while the denominator of $M$ grows without bound, even with $M$ in a fixed positive interval and both square variables nonzero. It does not impose the actual inner source form $M=\beta^e+T(n)^e$, so it is not a counterexample to a fully specified detector source. It prevents using the one-square height theorem unchanged in a two-square proposal.
