# Corrected 200-variable quartic and cumulative degree improvements

The current table is `data/results_20261115_v33.json`. Every count includes
the input. The inherited-premise quartic has 200 variables, 186 primitive
quadratic squares, degree four, integer content one, and exactly 3221
monomials. Its balanced height is
`max((5898050446009651968*31581660^28)^2,31581660^28*149^(2N))`.
The sufficient defining threshold remains ineffective. N>=57 is only a
coefficient-height condition.

The reduction replaces a separately stored fourth root point with a
recovered elliptic difference. Labels A=2a+2 and b=m+1/2 make
(A-2)b=a(2m+1) a nonzero integer. The full proof includes Lucas growth and
completeness, all reciprocal-pair exceptions, the changed weighted trace,
and uniform local/real preparation. The previous unpublished scalar-shift
formula is corrected and explicitly superseded; see the correction report.

The v30 degree improvements are included without changing their source
premises or sufficient parameters: 25 variables with degree below 2^55,
explicit 27 below 2^58 and height below 2^(2^71), and both 13-variable
families below 2^27. Their exact degree bounds are in the current table.
The abc quartic remains 79 variables and the specified quartic 260.

The reflection obstruction and ordered-squareclass-surface proofs are
separate auxiliary results. They neither provide matching-class completeness
nor impose an absolute lower bound on variables. The external arithmetic
audit and independent non-author mathematical review remain unfinished.

`scripts/verify_release_v27.py`, and `scripts/verify.py --full`. Native
