## The single detector

For the original point roles $A=[n]P$, $H=[h]P$ and $U=[j]P$, retain their six actual field-valued tests $C_i$. Put

$$
\beta=X^2+B,\quad T=\operatorname{Tr}x(A),\quad
M=\beta^4+T^4,\quad a=M^3,\quad t=M^6-z^2.
$$

For the sixteen shifts $c=\pm1,\ldots,\pm8$, define

$$
F(a,z)=\prod_c((a+c)^2-z^2),\qquad
u=-32aF/F_a,
$$

where $F_a$ differentiates with respect to $a$ while $z$ is fixed. In the smaller even presentation,

$$
\mathcal G(A,Z)=\prod_{j=1}^{8}((A+j^2-Z)^2-4Aj^2),\qquad
u=-16\mathcal G/\mathcal G_A=-2\mathcal G/\mathcal H,
\quad \mathcal H=\mathcal G_A/8.
$$

The complete detector uses

$$
D_i=\frac{C_i^2}{1+uC_i^2},\quad
W=\prod_i(1+D_i^2/u),\quad V=\operatorname{Tr}(W^{-1}),
\quad b=\frac{4u}{(1+u)^2V^2}.
$$

At a positive odd valuation of $u$, the intrinsic pole test for $7b$ forces all original $C_i$ to have positive valuation at one place. The retained formal comparison then gives $X\equiv n$. At a negative odd valuation of $u$, the transformed tests instead force both reciprocal products to be one modulo the prime, so $V\equiv2$ and $b$ has positive valuation. Thus the derivative's new denominator primes require no new elliptic-index congruences.

The derivative quotient also recovers simple collisions between the two translated contact products. Under ordinary abc, the discarded multiple prime factors have a height cost independent of the contact degree. The exact constants are

$$
\alpha=\frac{997}{1001},\quad Q=\frac{1002}{995},\quad
\gamma=\frac{997}{200},\quad C=\frac{401}{100},
\qquad
\gamma-Q\left(C+\frac1{2\alpha}\right)
=\frac{87545567}{198403000}>0.
$$

This strict inequality gives height soundness uniformly in the fixed shift. Completeness uses **32 prime positions** of the form $N\pm D^2(a+c)L^2$. The arbitrary fixed finite number of positions is covered by the stated Sun theorem. The proof checks denominator primes, selected primes, fixed places, the two-adic class, real bounds and every point-algebra character. The prepared intrinsic pole theorem supplies the final dense rational witness set.

## Separate fixed-slope finiteness theorem

The [fixed-slope incidence paper](../negative_results/papers/rational_fibers/fixed_slope_trace_finiteness_20261117_v59.tex) is in the separate negative-results directory. For the stated reweighted sources, a fixed input, prepared slope and smooth genus-one trace curve admit only finitely many guarded rational point tuples, even when all three conditional or nine general point roles vary independently. The proof computes the rational endomorphism ring of the restriction-of-scalars surface, proves nonconstancy on every relevant abelian translate, and applies Mordell–Lang.

This excludes infinite propagation at a fixed slope. It does not exclude individual witnesses, variable slopes, point-dependent auxiliary curves or the untwisted conic. No transfer of that theorem to the new derivative source is asserted. Its [exact certificate](../negative_results/computation/fixed_slope_trace_v59) checks the CM scaling and all 518 nonzero direction-parity cases; it does not substitute for the geometric proof.

## Remaining variable targets

**The 21- and 9-variable bounds remain unproved.** The established ordinary-abc bound is eleven. Removing its two pole parameters requires an actual rational realization theorem on its source tuples, or another source with a smaller complete coordinate budget. For the general bound, a proposed merger must replace the ordinary-abc height step by a theorem under the retained general premises. Positive rank, local preparation and a formal variable count do not supply either missing result.
