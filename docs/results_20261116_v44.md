Every count includes the input. Fixed parameters are coefficients. The current
records and their arithmetic hypotheses are listed in the [main table](../README.md)
and [machine-readable specifications](../data/results_20261116_v44.json).

## The ordinary-abc quartic

The [arithmetic theorem](../papers/quartic_existence/quartic_abc76_20261116_v44.tex)
gives an integer polynomial in **76 variables**, of **exact total degree four**,
as a sum of **66 primitive integer quadratic squares**. It has integer content
one and **1,250 ordinary monomials**. The coefficients depend on a fixed odd
integer \(B\ge81\), with exact height

\[
H(Q_B)=\max\{20828275368498373068800,\ B^2+275933278576697172\}.
\]

Under ordinary rational abc and the retained arithmetic premises, every
sufficiently large such \(B\) gives rational input projection exactly
\(\mathbb Z\). The sufficient threshold is ineffective. Substitution of
\(B=81\) is an algebraic verification fixture, not a certified defining member.

### Quadratic detector and pole equations

For each of the two contact blocks, put \(w_2=w^2\). The retained reference
and pair coordinates give
\(C_2=C^2\), \(I_2=I^2\), \(F_H=f_H^2\), \(G_H=g_H^2\),
\(F_U=f_U^2\), and \(F_V=f_V^2\). Replace the two field-factor equations by

\[
w_2 A_1=ww_2+C_2^2+wF_H+w_2F_U,
\qquad
w_2 A_2=ww_2+I_2^2+w\overline{G_H}+w_2\overline{F_V}.
\]

These are quadratic in the retained coordinates. On \(w\ne0\), the normalized
first factor is

\[
T_1=\frac{A_1}{w}
=1+\frac{C^4}{w^3}+\frac{f_H^2}{w^2}+\frac{f_U^2}{w},
\]

with the analogous formula for \(T_2\). The comparison term and the point terms
have distinct valuations modulo four at every relevant odd contact. This prevents
cancellation and gives the formal-parameter precision needed for the integer-label
comparison, on all allowed torsion branches.

The retained pole input is \(\widetilde b=w^2b\). Its quadratic equations are

\[
w\widetilde b=\eta^2,\quad p_2=p^2,\quad
e^2+\widetilde b p_2=1,\quad
uv=e(w_2-\widetilde b),\quad q_2=q^2,\quad
u^2-v^2=\widetilde bq_2.
\]

For \(w\ne0\), the exact map to the original pole variables is
\(p_{\rm old}=wp\), \(u_{\rm old}=u/w\),
\(v_{\rm old}=v/w\), \(q_{\rm old}=q\), and
\(b=\widetilde b/w^2\). It includes zero ordinates. Pole preparation and
realization are applied to this interpreted \(b\).

There is no coordinate for \(w^3\). The remaining point, reference, comparison,
trace-height and nonzero conditions are all included in the 76-variable count.
The exact package independently reconstructs every changed row and authenticates
every retained row.

### Zero contacts and completeness

When \(w=0\), the contact argument belongs to
\(\{-5,-2,-1,0,2,3,4,7\}\). Its height is at most \(\log7\).
Defining the odd radical to be one at those contacts extends the marked-abc
height estimate to the entire rational solution set. The mixed-power height
argument has strict absorption coefficient \(602/679\). This covers the added
boundary without an auxiliary nonzero constraint.

The completeness proof chooses the fixed input, elliptic difference and scaling
data before the fresh primes. It verifies preparation for the revised factors,
the finite local conditions and the positive real range, then applies the retained
pole-realization premise to \(b\) and rescales its witnesses. The full rational
zero set is not asserted to coincide with that of another source, and no previous
numerical defining threshold is imported.

## Rational parametrization of varying pole inputs

The [quadric theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/varying_input_quadric_20261116_v44.tex)
fixes \(D>0\), \(\tau\in\mathbb Q^\times\) with \(\tau^2\ne D\), and
\(e=(\tau^2-D)/(\tau^2+3D)\). For the variable input
\(b=D/(17V^2)\), the second-pole surface is birational to

\[
x^2-Dy^2=e(\sigma^2+3D).
\]

The rational point \((x,y,\sigma)=(\tau,1,\tau)\) gives an explicit
parametrization by lines. On the stated open set the detector coordinate is

\[
V=\frac{\sigma x-Dy}{17(x-\sigma y)}.
\]

The full theorem supplies both inverse maps and the pole ordinates. This
parametrizes compatible coefficient inputs at a fixed common conic parameter.
It does not establish the additional incidence equation
\(V=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal W^{-1})\) for an actual native elliptic tuple.
The rationality of the coefficient surface and rational realization on the
elliptic source are separate statements.

## Separate obstructions and remaining targets

The [abelian-parameter theorem](../negative_results/papers/rational_fibers/abelian_parameter_obstruction_20261116_v44.tex)
excludes a uniform rational square-complement or original-pole identity on an
abelian parameter space when the detector argument is nonconstant. Twist classes
are allowed to vary. The proof uses an odd contact divisor, the two mandatory
reference tests, and rigidity of maps from abelian varieties to higher-genus curves.
It leaves fixed-argument fibers, ramified covers and individual rational witnesses
open. No 24-variable construction follows.

The [fixed-argument ramification theorem](../negative_results/papers/rational_fibers/fixed_argument_complement_ramification_20261116_v44.tex)
concerns the full independent auxiliary product at an allowed fixed slope and
positive detector weight. Its square-complement cover is geometrically ramified,
so a surjective translated abelian homomorphism, including an isogeny, cannot give
a uniform section. The proof restricts to a native elliptic boundary and uses the
fixed pole divisor of three points, each with weight four. Proper coupled
subvarieties and individual rational points are not excluded. Common pole
fibers can also exist without square complements.

The [duplication theorem](../negative_results/papers/elliptic_charts/duplicated_reference_signs_20261116_v44.tex)
gives an allowed reference-index progression where the two doubled local
parameters acquire opposite signs. It is an obstruction to the specified
reference replacement, not a nonintegral solution of the complete quartic.

The next variable targets are a rational common fiber on an actual detector family,
including a suitable proper coupling of the auxiliary indices, and a quadratic reference replacement with a net saving
after detector conversion, the original trace height, square coordinates and
nonzero constraints have been counted. The [research directions](research_directions.md)
state their precise arithmetic obligations.

## Exact reproduction

The [complete coefficient package](../computation/quartic/abc76_v44) contains the
quadratic rows, expanded polynomial family, builder and independent checker.
With Python 3.12 and SymPy 1.14.0, run from the repository root:

```sh
python -B scripts/verify_v44.py --report /tmp/v44-verification.json
```

The verifier reconstructs the 76-variable rows and expansion, their exact
coefficient-height certificate, and the separate obstruction certificates in
temporary files. The manuscript proves the universal arithmetic implications;
finite valuation grids and sample specializations do not replace those proofs.
The retained verification suites and the 126-stage legacy suite authenticate the
underlying constructions and their dependencies.
