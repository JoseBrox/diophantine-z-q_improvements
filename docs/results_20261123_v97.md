## The new source and the resolved integrality cycle

The [complete proof](../undecidability/pell_power_v97/pell_power_replacement.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/pell_power_v97/pell_power_replacement.pdf)) proves
the replacement of `q=b^L`, where `L=5^60`. Put

```math
T_b=b^5-1,\qquad
A=\frac{b^9+8b^5+b-8}{2}+5T_bs,
\qquad m_*=2bA-b^2-1.
```

Introduce integer witnesses delta and alpha-star, and rational Pell
coordinates C-star and D-star, satisfying

```math
C_*=L+\delta(A-1),\qquad
D_*^2-(A^2-1)C_*^2=1,
\qquad
q=D_*-(A-b)C_*-\alpha_*m_*.
```

Add only `q>b^5` and `C_*^2<c^2`, where c is the already retained first
Pell coordinate. Restrict the instance to the cofinite tail `x>32z`.

The parameter A is integral, is 1 modulo 5, and always has absolute
value at least 2, even before the sign of s is recovered. The exact
factorization

```math
m_*=(b^5-1)(b^5+8b+1+10bs)
```

is decisive. The Pell index is a multiple of 5, and the signed Pell
recurrences imply `q=±1 (mod b^5-1)`. Therefore

```math
\lambda=\frac{q^4-1}{b^5-1}
```

is an integer **before** proving `q=b^L`. This resolves the precise
integrality cycle recorded in the earlier fixed-power checkpoint.
The zero Pell index and negative auxiliary parameter are included in
this initial reconstruction.

## Why the exponent is exactly L

On the new cofinite tail, the retained positive slack forces `b>32z`.
Together with `q>b^5`, this makes both coding-prefix coefficients S and
T positive integers. Hence `r>=M`, where `M=q^32`, and in particular
`r>L`. The existing signed binomial and one-quotient Pell theorems now
apply in their established reconstruction order, giving positive w,s,c,
`bw=2^(2r+1)`, and `c=Y_(2r+1)(a)`.

With `U=wM`, their explicit bounds give

```math
U\ge4^r,\qquad s>U^{r-2},\qquad a<s^2,
\qquad A-1>L+6r.
```

The new inequality `|C_*|<c` bounds its Pell index nu by `nu<=6r`.
The congruence `C_*=L (mod A-1)` then forces `nu=L` with the positive
second-coordinate sign. The remaining negative trace branch would
require `q*b^L=-1 (mod m_*)`. But the same growth bounds give

```math
0<q b^L+1<m_*.
```

That branch is impossible, and the positive branch gives `q=b^L` by
the corresponding size bound and congruence.

Conversely, an old source witness has `A<a` and `L<2r+1`, so
`Y_L(A)<Y_(2r+1)(a)=c`. Set C-star and D-star to the canonical Pell
values. The recurrence gives positive integral delta and alpha-star.
Thus the two new inputs are nonzero on constructed witnesses, as needed
by the abc tuple theorem. Both source directions are proved; the new
source does not rely on an unverified exponentiation certificate.

## Relation to the power-circuit lower bound

The earlier 139-coordinate lower bound remains valid. It concerns a
triangular quadratic circuit on a common rational-function branch

certified integer witnesses and uses growth constraints from the rest
of the universal source. It lies outside that model. The lower bound
therefore identified the need for a structural change; it was not
contradicted by a shorter ordinary power circuit.
