## The last reduction: one square performs two jobs

The [half-trace theorem](../undecidability/pell_halftrace_v96/halftrace_guard.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/pell_halftrace_v96/halftrace_guard.pdf)) replaces
the old first-Pell sign equation by

```math
\zeta^2=2\bigl(ad+(a^2-1)c+1\bigr).
```

For the intended odd index B, `d=X_B(a)` and `c=Y_B(a)`, its right side
equals `4*X_((B+1)/2)(a)^2`. For the wrong sign `ad<0`, the same right
side is negative. The exact identity

```math
a^2d^2-\bigl((a^2-1)c+1\bigr)^2=(a^2-1)(c-1)^2
```

proves this after using the norm equation, with the negative-c case
handled directly. Thus the new square selects the same sign.

It also excludes s=0 without the old reciprocal guard. On that branch,
the already integral small-Pell data force `a=p=c=0`, `k=±1`, and
`d=±1`. The new equation would say `zeta^2=2`, which has no rational
solution. This deduction precedes the later reciprocal-i argument, so
the reconstruction is noncircular.

The stored product `ac` makes the replacement residual quadratic:

```math
\zeta^2-2ad-2a(ac)+2c-2=0.
```

It uses the same square witness as before. Removing the now unnecessary
coordinate `s_inverse` and equation `s*s_inverse=1` saves **one variable
and one row** in all three routes. No integer predicate is removed in
this final step.

## What remains open

**The quartic target near 263 is not established.** The dominant remaining
cost is eleven separate integrality certificates. The
[compression-boundary proofs](../undecidability/joint_research_v94/README.md)
give precise limits for the tested power, integer-value, and common-height
strategies. Their hypotheses matter: they do not bound arbitrary systems
or exclude a different universal source.

In particular, the 139-coordinate lower bound now covers arbitrary
triangular quadratic circuits on a common rational-function branch,
including nonmonomial intermediates, divisions, and quadratic equations
whose selected branch is rational. A common-denominator representation
has degree at most doubled by each step; a genuinely quadratic step
selecting a rational branch does not increase that degree. This broader
optimality statement still excludes simultaneously implicit systems and
extra relations supplied by the universal source.

The next serious routes are further integer-quotient compression,
simultaneous certificates with fewer private coordinates, and replacing
the large fixed-power source. Partial attempts must retain their unresolved
steps: denominator coprimality does not follow from formal CRT notation;
the binomial auxiliary s is not freely padded; local reference depths
cannot be fixed independently of all odd-place annihilation requirements;
and the shorter Pell power certificate still has an integrality-order
gap involving lambda and r. None of these unfinished approaches contributes
to the counts above.

The [quotient-merging checkpoint](../undecidability/joint_research_v94/quotient_merging_checkpoint.md)
proves the exact denominator-gcd identity and separates its unresolved
coprimality and numerator-integrality requirements. The
[fixed-power checkpoint](../undecidability/pell_halftrace_v96/fixed_power_checkpoint.md)
records the proved integrality step and the remaining reconstruction
cycle in the Pell replacement attempt. These partial results are
preserved so that the next continuation can start at the actual gaps.
