# An explicit quartic in 74,007 bytes

The [complete manuscript](../papers/quartic/quartic229_compact_20261117_v68.tex) specifies an integer quartic in **229 variables including the input**, with **3,312 nonzero monomials**, integer content one and exact coefficient height **149^30**, a **217-bit** integer. Its [fully expanded decimal text](../computation/quartic/explicit229_compact_v68/quartic229-N15.txt) occupies **74,007 bytes** in the upstream repository's canonical notation. The sufficient arithmetic parameter remains **N=15**, under exactly the retained source premises.

## Exact construction and proof

Let the 207 source quadrics at N=15 be Q_j. The [transformation specification](../computation/quartic/explicit229_compact_v68/transformation.json) gives integer lists a_i and b_j such that

```math
x_i=149^{a_i}y_i,\qquad
R_j(y)=149^{b_j}Q_j(149^{a_1}y_1,\ldots,149^{a_{229}}y_{229}),
\qquad \widehat P(y)=\sum_{j=1}^{207}R_j(y)^2.
```

Every transformed row is an integer quadric of content one. All scaling factors are nonzero rational constants and a_1=0. The coordinate map is therefore a bijection of the full rational affine space fixing the input. Its inverse preserves every source row, guard and boundary stratum. The complete arithmetic body in the manuscript is unchanged except for its intermediate theorem label; the appended normalization supplies the final polynomial and explicit exponent tables.

The source and final polynomials have identical monomial support and coordinate order. Their canonical decimal sizes are 148,701 and 74,007 bytes. Their coefficient heights are 149^32 and 149^30. Thus the reduction changes the coefficients themselves and lowers the maximum by a factor of 22,201. It does not rely on abbreviations, renumbering or a compressed representation. No optimality claim is made.

## Verification

Run `python -B scripts/verify_v68.py`. The [reproducibility package](../computation/quartic/explicit229_compact_v68) contains the source table, every transformed quadric, the full expanded JSON and TXT, fixed exponent lists, the builder, a separate standard-library checker, and an independent SymPy polynomial-ring reconstruction. The latter checks all 207 row identities in both directions, all 3,312 expanded coefficients, support, primitive content, the attained coefficient height, all occurring variables and a strict parse of the entire ASCII expression. It also authenticates both exponent tables printed in the manuscript.

The unchanged [upstream measuring program](../computation/quartic/explicit229_compact_v68/upstream/measure_plain_text.py) gives the [official measurement report](../computation/quartic/explicit229_compact_v68/official_measurement.json). Its strict limit is 100,000 bytes, leaving **25,993 bytes** to spare. This verifies the size condition; it does not assert upstream record acceptance.

The [integration receipt](../data/audits/integration_20261117_v68.json), [rendering receipt](../data/audits/tex_build_20261117_v68.json) and [current records](../data/results_20261117_v68.json) state the scope. The construction's external arithmetic premises are retained, not independently established by these finite checks.
