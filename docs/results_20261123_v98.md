## Recovering the power without an added positive-sign condition


```math

\lambda=\frac{q^4-1}{b^5-1}>0.
```

Pell recovery before the sign of q is known.

With `K=b^(5^60)`, the unwanted branch requires `qK=-1 (mod m_*)`.

`q=K>0`. Thus the separate `q>b^5` condition follows from the other rows;
its three private positivity coordinates and its row can be deleted.

## Absorbing the index bound into the existing integer margin

The old small-Pell condition is `eta=k^2-4(c-kY)^2>0`. Strengthen it to

```math
\eta-C_*^2>0
```

and delete the separate new condition `C_*^2<c^2`. This strengthening
uses the square of an existing coordinate, so it changes only one
quadratic coefficient. Soundness follows in a noncircular order: the
new condition implies the old eta condition; the retained half-trace
excludes s=0; and then `abs(Y)>=16` implies `abs(c)>abs(k)>abs(C_*)`.

For canonical witnesses, `e_0=c-kY` is integral and k is positive.
Factoring the positive integer margin gives

```math
\eta=(k-2|e_0|)(k+2|e_0|)\ge k.
```

The source estimates imply `p>4A^2` and `r>L`, with `L=5^60`. The
elementary Pell recurrence bounds therefore give

```math
C_*^2=Y_L(A)^2<(2A)^{2L-2}<p^{L-1}<p^r<Y_{r+1}(p)=k.
```

Hence every canonical witness satisfies the stronger condition.
This deletes another three private positivity coordinates and one row.
The proof concerns the universal-instance and coding-prefix projection;
private positivity representatives may be chosen anew.
