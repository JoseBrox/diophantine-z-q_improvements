## Main new mathematical reductions

1. [Aggregate height and common contact coefficients](../undecidability/abc_aggregate_contacts_v109/README.md): the common fourth-power tree and the shared eight-shift derivative polynomials replace private contact circuits. This gives 560 variables and 483 residuals (commit db810260cd80). The height margin and 60 symbolic derivative identities were checked exactly.
2. [Theta positivity deletion](../undecidability/positivity_theta_reduction_20261124/README.md): replacement of the two positivity equations and recovery of theta positivity from the retained source saves three variables and one row in every route (c6d16223d3ce). Its [composition with aggregate contacts](../undecidability/abc_aggregate_theta_v112/README.md) gives 557/482 (302465b37ab0).
3. [Selective pole for unrestricted degree](../undecidability/abc_selective_pole_v110/README.md): prime-selective gating with a common derivative quotient gives a simultaneous integer certificate in 3k+8 total coordinates. The rank-sixteen wrapper applied to k=9 gives 36 unknowns (83fdab7a2c5a). The valuation/degree ledger includes 81,862 finite valuation cases.
4. [Direct universal polynomial testing on elliptic indices](../undecidability/direct_index_transfer_v111/README.md): truncated formal logarithms force high-power divisibility of the integer universal-polynomial residual. Height amplification on the trace or both components of the abscissa sum makes that residual zero. The coefficient point for the instance is included in the aggregate, making the amplification exponent independent of the instance. The formula is 4k+8 or 4k+10 unknowns (8fc97409acf5). The certificate checks 8,442 valuation fixtures, the contact list, the pole limit, the log-tail inequalities and all counts.
5. [One native pole for the joint quartic](../undecidability/abc_shared_native_pole_v113/README.md): keep the twelve comparison blocks but replace the twelve pole realizations by one. If u_i are the private contact quotients, use u=12/(sum 1/u_i). On sufficiently many primes both u and the relevant u_i have valuation one, even in the presence of cross-contact collisions. For the retained norm error d_i use A_i=u+u_i^2/u, V_i=d_i^2/(1+A_i d_i^2), W_i=1+V_i^2/u. The one normalized native pole uses alpha=u+(product W_i-1)^2. Active inputs must pass their original local test; inactive inputs automatically give integral factors at the assigned contact primes. The proof covers negative odd valuations and ordered completeness. Removing 120 variables and 96 rows and adding 82 variables and 80 rows gives **519 variables, 466 residuals** (21471fdb7d976). The [independent verifier](../undecidability/abc_shared_native_pole_v113/verify.js) reconstructs all 80 new quadrics, checks every one of the 386 retained rows, and checks 16,583 finite valuation fixtures (a08df0db77e9).

Separately, [joint number-field rationality](../papers/number_fields/joint_rationality_20261124/README.md) gives 7k+3 coordinates for k rationality tests inside K, reducing the previous 110/220 composition bounds to **80/157** for the rational integers and the ring of integers in quadratic K (c093271fd45a). All stated stable-rank, number-field height/abc, pole and underlying source premises are retained. This is not a rational undecidability count.

## Verification and limits

The [current frontier ledger](../undecidability/frontier_20261124_v113.json) pins the exact Git blob identities and audits the full variable support, residual degree and census of all five new complete quartic tables. The stored exact builders and independent v113 audit are reproducible. The older v108 receipt remains a historical replay; its frozen registry pointer is unchanged. New records are appended, not substituted for historical results. No full historical CI rerun is claimed. Finite identities and valuations do not prove the imported arithmetic premises.

The target near 263 remains unproved. The next largest savings should come from reducing the twelve/ten retained integer certificates, rather than only improving their local polynomial charts. In particular, the second-Pell quotient's c^2-divisibility and the two fixed-power congruence quotients remain expensive. The direct-index construction also suggests using high-precision selective tests of more source equations without individual integer certificates; the enormous source degree must be replaced by a genuinely economical quadratic circuit before claiming a quartic bound. Extending the shared-pole gates to the general three-/two-contact scalar normalization and auditing the new Sun source are concrete next steps.
