## The complete 68-variable quartic

The [proof](../papers/quartic_existence/quartic_abc68_20261117_v65.tex) and
[complete coefficient package](../computation/quartic/abc68_v65) give
56 primitive integer quadratic residuals, whose squared sum has 1,078
coordinate monomials, exact degree four and content one.

Set `K=Q(sqrt(2))`, `D=11-6sqrt(2)` and `beta=X`. Four field quadrics in
the six field coordinates `s,t,F,G,h,d` are

```math
d=(h+s+t)F,\qquad D(F+G)=(s-t)h-t^2,
\qquad d=Gh,\qquad \beta^4=d(s-t)-DFG.
```

They imply

```math
DF^2+s^2F=DG^2+t^2G=\beta^4.
```

For nonzero input, `F,G` are automatically units and recover two finite
elliptic points with nonzero abscissas. Translation by `(0,0)` gives a secant
whose third intersection has abscissa `a=h(h+s+t)`. Thus

```math
F=\beta^2/x(U),\quad G=\beta^2/x(V),\quad
s=\beta y(U)/x(U),\quad t=\beta y(V)/x(V),\quad
a=\beta^2x(U-V).
```

Only the rational half-trace of `a` is stored; its other component has no
consumer and is recovered polynomially. The chart covers every allowed
tangency and every finite two-torsion difference, including `(0,0)`. That
last difference has index zero and satisfies the soundness height bound.

The physical comparisons, reference products, scaled difference trace,
height gap and native pole inputs are unchanged. Hence the retained
integer-index extraction and strict absorption `602/679` apply. All prior
nonzero-integer witnesses lift to the new chart. The explicit zero-input
substitution preserves the same gap and pole witnesses. No new denominator
guard or arithmetic premise is introduced.

Independent reconstruction verifies every literal row and invertible row
operation, the complete polynomial expansion, and the exact coefficient
height `B^2+1` for every `B>=81`. The sufficient defining shift remains
ineffective; `B=81` is not claimed to define the integers.

## The actual-trace targets

The [single derivative-trace criterion](../papers/pole_parameters/single_trace_derivative_criterion_20261117_v64.tex)
proves soundness and all finite-algebra guards for a nine-coordinate source
whose extra equation is

```math
Y^2=V^2+w(2-V),\qquad V=\operatorname{Tr}(W^{-1}),\quad w=u/(1+u)^2.
```

Completeness would follow from actual rational square witnesses for all
sufficiently large positive integers at one fixed sufficiently large
shift. Sign symmetry supplies the negative inputs, and finite input
factors add the remaining integers without new coordinates. That witness
existence theorem is open. The general 21-variable route requires
simultaneous realization of two actual guarded fibers under its own
retained height argument.

The [derivative-fiber genus theorem](../negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/derivative_trace_genus_20261117_v65.tex)
gives eighteen simple rational branch contacts after fixing the auxiliary
point, so each normalized slope fiber has genus at least eight. There is
no rational curve through the guarded multiplication locus, even when
the auxiliary point is allowed to vary. The known branch points violate
the nonzero-weight guard. This excludes rational parametrization of that
locus; it does not exclude individual rational witnesses or disprove the
realization criterion.

The [cofinite coupling obstruction](../negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/cofinite_mobius_coupling_obstruction_20261117_v64.tex)
excludes finite input repairs for the stated twelve-symmetry six-contact
architecture at its obstructed coefficient shifts. Other shifts and
other detector architectures are outside that theorem.

## Number fields

The [effective cyclic-sum theorem](../papers/effective_sums/elliptic_sum_heights_over_number_fields_20261117_v63.tex)
holds over every number field and every elliptic curve, for points in a
fixed cyclic group modulo rational torsion, with an effective logarithmic
index loss. Torsion translation also gives reciprocal-abscissa sum bounds
on the stated `j=1728` curves. These are sum estimates; uncontrolled field
traces can still cancel.

The [finite-exception integrality criterion](../papers/number_fields/finite_exceptions_and_integrality_20261117_v63.tex)
reduces the constructive target to a Diophantine set containing almost all
rational integers with bounded-height nonintegral elements. No such
general detector, universal ring-of-integers theorem, or counterexample
has been established here.
