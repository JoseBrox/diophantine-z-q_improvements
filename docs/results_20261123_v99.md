## The identity and its inverse

The corrected source stores `R=sr=r*s*n^2`, `U=w*n^2`, and the rows

```math
f_{\rm sq}=R_2-R^2,\qquad
f_a=a-(U+1)R,\qquad
f_p=p-2UR_2.
```

The square register `R_2=sr2` has only two occurrences, in its own
definition and in `f_p`. Keep `f_a`, delete `R_2` and `f_sq`, and replace
`f_p` by the quadratic row

```math
g_p=p-2aR+2R^2.
```

The exact identity

```math
g_p=f_p+2U f_{\rm sq}-2R f_a
```

proves the forward implication. For the reverse implication, assign
`R_2=R^2`; then `f_sq=0` and `f_p=g_p+2R f_a=0`. This is a unique
polynomial inverse lift on the entire rational zero set. It uses no
division and has no nonzero hypotheses; all zero branches are included.
Every other row is unchanged, apart from coordinate renumbering.

The [one-page proof](../undecidability/pell_product_v99/pell_product_elimination.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/pell_product_v99/pell_product_elimination.pdf))
and [complete coefficient tables](../undecidability/pell_product_v99)
include all three interfaces. The general and abc predecessors are v98;
the specified predecessor is v96. Squaring and summing the new residuals
still gives degree exactly four. No integer predicate or instance range
changes in this step.
