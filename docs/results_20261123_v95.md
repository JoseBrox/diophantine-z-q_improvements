## One quotient enforces both Pell congruences

The [complete proof](../undecidability/pell_crt_v95/one_quotient_pell.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/pell_crt_v95/one_quotient_pell.pdf)) replaces the
old two quotient equations

```math
H=B+jc,\qquad I=d+oF
```

by a single integer quotient J in

```math
H=c+F^2(B-c)+cFJ,
\qquad I^2-(G^2-1)H^2=1,
\qquad B=2r+1.
```

The retained norm gives `F^2=1 (mod c)` and `gcd(c,F)=1`. The new H
therefore satisfies both `H=B (mod c)` and `H=c (mod F)`. A signed
second-coordinate Pell congruence proves that these recover the required
index B. Specifically, for `A>=2`, `0<t<q`, congruence of `Y_v(A)` to
either sign of `Y_t(A)` modulo `X_q(A)` forces `v=±t (mod 2q)`.
The proof folds the index and uses the strict inequality
`Y_q(A)+Y_(q-1)(A)<X_q(A)` to exclude other residues.

The argument covers both signs of the large parameter, the first Pell
coordinate, and the auxiliary trace. A rational last norm root is an
integer by monicity. Positive completeness uses indices `v=B+4qL`:
both congruences persist, and J becomes positive for large L. This also
supplies the nonzero inputs required by the abc tuple construction.

The old register j now stores J. The old source coordinate o disappears.
One new register `CF=cF` keeps the H equation quadratic, so the outer
coordinate count does not increase. The two old rows are replaced by
two quadratic rows. The saving comes from deleting the o integrality
certificate, not from an unsupported assertion that o was automatically
integral.

For the general and specified interfaces, the global contact gauge must
also be changed: its sum now contains **22 / 33** weights instead of

therefore saves **60 / 75 variables**, and **52 / 65 rows**.

eleven active certificates. This does not assert equality with the old
twelve-family normalization or preservation of the old value of j.

## Boundaries established during further reduction work

The [compression-boundary checkpoint](../undecidability/joint_research_v94/README.md)
([proof PDF](../undecidability/joint_research_v94/compression_barriers.pdf))
adds three exact results. The 139-coordinate power circuit is optimal in
the stated triangular quadratic binomial model even with negative and
fractional Laurent powers. A single quadratic integer-value test cannot
recognize an unrestricted integer tuple of dimension at least two.
Finally, an explicit Pell family has unbounded input height while the
proposed common abc height sum stays constant; it invalidates the
suggested scalar height estimate. None of these is a lower bound for
arbitrary Diophantine constructions or the particular constrained source.

The unrestricted-degree counts and their v93 degree improvements remain
**82 / 100 / 52**, with strict ceilings **2^532 / 2^578 / 2^425**.
The quartic target near **263** remains open. Further substantial progress
needs fewer essential integer witnesses, a smaller simultaneous
integrality construction, or a different universal source; merely
reordering the current monomial power circuit cannot provide it.
