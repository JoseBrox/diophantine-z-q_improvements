## 1. One numerator point serves every input

The [complete proof](../undecidability/papers/common_numerator_transfers.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/common_numerator_transfers.pdf)) replaces
private numerator points `[h n_i]P` by one point `[j]P`. It does not require
one rational ratio to approximate different `n_i` at the same place.
Each input has its own disjoint family of fresh contacts.

At a contact assigned to input `i`, let `u_d,v_d` denote the primary and
opposite annihilator orders, divided by four. The all-sign coprimality
clause of the pinned prime supplier yields

```math
v_2(u_d)=4,\qquad v_d\text{ odd},\qquad
\gcd(u_d,v_e)=1,\qquad \gcd(v_d,v_e)=1\ (d\ne e).
```

The reference and numerator indices may therefore satisfy

```math
\begin{aligned}
h&\equiv0\pmod{u_d},& h&\equiv1\pmod{v_d},&h&\equiv p_d\pmod{p_d^2},\\
j&\equiv0\pmod{u_d},&j&\equiv n_i\pmod{v_d},&j&\equiv hn_i\pmod{p_d^5}.
\end{aligned}
```

The prime-power precision can be increased arbitrarily. A two-sided
Hasse cluster keeps the fresh primes coprime to all selected orders.
The existing Gaussian residue at seven automatically avoids seven in
both orders, so the fixed preparation modulus creates no extra assumption.
Independent real returns preserve the compact neighborhoods.

The exact identity

```math
\frac jh-\frac{j-n_i}{h-1}
=-\frac{j-hn_i}{h(h-1)}
```

preserves the formal comparison precision. At prepared fixed places,
the raw comparisons remain bounded and the existing contact weights
make their contributions arbitrarily small. The pole parameters stay
private. The v88 Bhargava--Burungale product completion applies; the old
native-pole converse premise is not reintroduced.

For `k` integer inputs, the joint counts are `7k+18`, `9k+18` and `5k+6`.
The [complete compressed sources](../undecidability/common_numerator_sources)
give all coefficient nodes, guards, finite stages and projection maps.
Their exact replay and degree ledger are checked by
[`verify_common_numerator.py`](../undecidability/verify_common_numerator.py).

## 2. The universal equation and positivity witness become finite generators

The [finite-wrapper proof](../undecidability/papers/finite_wrapper_transfer.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/finite_wrapper_transfer.pdf)) uses the precise
BDHMS mixed-domain construction. Its source polynomial `Q(n)` has degree
eight in the final nonnegative input, with leading coefficient `S^16`,
where `S=2A-5` never vanishes when the other source inputs are integers.

There is a monic polynomial `Pcal(m)` of degree eight satisfying
`Pcal(S^2 n)=Q(n)`. Add the finite relations

```math
\mathcal P(m)=0,\qquad
\xi^2+S\xi+S^2(s^2+t^2)-m=0.
```

Their algebra is free of rank sixteen, with basis `m^j,m^j xi` for
`0<=j<8`. It is generated by `xi` alone. With `S` guarded, recover
`n=m/S^2` and `u=xi/S`, obtaining

```math
Q(n)=0,\qquad n=s^2+t^2+u^2+u.
```

All **nine** integer predicates remain. Consequently the last identity
forces `n>=0`, and every nonnegative integer has such witnesses by the
three-squares theorem. The old point coefficients are independent of `n`;
the terminal pole equations remain monic after explicit powers of `S`
are cleared. The enlarged algebra uses the same single rational encoder.
This saves one ordinary coordinate, giving 82/100/52.

The paper gives the whole source coefficient recipe, a radical-free norm
formula, both directions of the equivalence, a general equation-absorption
principle, and the full degree calculation. The independent
[`verify_finite_wrapper.py`](../undecidability/verify_finite_wrapper.py)
checks the eight-sign identity, the rank-sixteen companion relation,
actual source dependencies and exact integer degree bounds. The enormous
final determinant polynomial is specified by this finite recipe and is
not expanded.

An [executable coefficient compiler](../undecidability/code/build_finite_wrapper.py)
accepts an arbitrary supplied sparse integer polynomial and builds this
whole finite-algebra source. Binary geometric sums and power nodes avoid
expanding the enormous coding constants. Its independent checker replays
every denominator-clearing instruction and verifies the triangular finite
stages. The stored `P(a,z)=z^2-a` example and a 58-variable stress input
are explicitly **nonuniversal** examples. They do not supply or purport to
verify the coefficients of a universal source polynomial. See the
[format and reproduction guide](../undecidability/finite_wrapper_sources/README.md).

## 3. Optimizing the actual combined quadratic system

The [reciprocal rechart](../undecidability/papers/reciprocal_reference_checkpoint.tex)
reuses an existing input-square register as the reciprocal-input unit
witness. It deletes one ordinary coordinate and one row. Its six changed
rows are verified by exact rational substitutions with nonzero monomial
clearing factors.

The [common-point proof](../undecidability/papers/common_points_quartic_checkpoint.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/common_points_quartic_checkpoint.pdf)) then
identifies **54** and **50** physical rational coordinates. This includes
the common reference, scalar/Lucas roles and four coordinates of the input
numerator point. Every listed coordinate is matched to its function before
the contact and scalar normalizations; private gauges are not equated.
The common-index CRT supplies the stronger synchronized depths needed in
the quadratic chart.

From the 251-coordinate outer core the counts are exactly

```math
251+12(163-1)-11\cdot54=1601,\qquad
251+12(179-1)-11\cdot50=1837.
```

The respective 24 and 20 duplicate rows per additional integer block give

```math
219+12\cdot137-11\cdot24=1599,\qquad
219+12\cdot151-11\cdot20=1811.
```

The [complete tables](../undecidability/coefficients/jones1980_common_points)
list every quadratic residual. Their sums of squares have degree exactly
four. The independent checker replays every row, every deleted duplicate,
all shared coordinate maps, the Laurent weights, the coefficient domain,
and the full table hashes.

The earlier source audit is unchanged: the literal later transcription
reduces reversibly from 62 variables and 50 rows to **57 and 45**. This is
not an independent proof of the alleged universal **57/47** claim. The
Jones/Matiyasevich emendation is explicitly recorded and still requires
