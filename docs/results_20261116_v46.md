# Current constructions and verification

## The 74-variable ordinary-abc quartic

The [quartic theorem](../papers/quartic_existence/quartic_abc74_20261116_v46.tex)
gives a defining family under ordinary rational abc and the retained
elliptic-group, simultaneous-prime, trace-height and congruent-twist premises.
It has **74 total variables**, is the sum of **64 primitive integral quadratic
squares**, and has exact degree **4**, integer content **1**, and **1,190
nonzero coordinate monomials**. Its exact coefficient height is

$$
H(Q_B)=\max\{20828275368498373068800,\;B^2+275933278576697091\}.
$$

The fixed odd coefficient $B\ge81$ must be sufficiently large. Its defining
threshold remains ineffective; $B=81$ is an algebraic fixture, not a
certified defining member. The coefficient statements hold for every
positive integer $B$.

### Complete quadratic construction

Put $\beta=X/2$. Two scalar coordinates store $\beta^2$ and $\beta^4$,
giving the argument scale $a=B+\beta^8+T^2$. The complete five-field
reference chart is retained. Rescale the seven coordinates of the paired
points by powers of $\beta$, and divide their two formal-parameter ratios
by $\beta$. Every point and ratio equation stays quadratic. The height
equation recovers the original trace $T=\operatorname{Tr}x(U-V)$.

The normalized local factors have the form

$$
\mathcal T_1=1+\frac{C^2}{w}
 +\frac{(\beta^2 f(H)f(U))^2}{w^2},
\qquad
\mathcal T_2=1+\frac{I^2}{w}
 +\frac{(\beta^2\overline{f(H-P)}\,\overline{f(V)})^2}{w^2},
$$

where $f(Q)=1/x(Q)$, $C=L-\overline{L_0}$ and $I=L-1$.
Their quadratic equations use $w$ directly. No stored coordinate for
$w^2$ is required. If $W=\mathcal T_1\mathcal T_2$, the trace and
native pole equations recover

$$
\tau=\frac{\operatorname{Nm}W}{\operatorname{Tr}W},\qquad
b=w\tau^2.
$$

The common block uses 38 rational coordinates and 32 rows. Each of the
two argument blocks uses 18 coordinates and 16 rows. These totals include
the complete reference chart, every auxiliary square, the original trace
height, and every required constraint.

### Soundness, all branches and boundaries

At a consumed prime of positive odd weight order, the three factor terms
have distinct valuation classes. A product test, together with the complete
ratio equations, proves that a reference and its numerator are both formal
at the identity whenever $v(\beta)\le0$. This includes every possible
torsion branch and proves integrality of $\beta$ at the consumed primes.
For the half-integral elliptic index $n$ of $U-V$, the local residual is

$$\beta(n-\beta)\equiv0\pmod p.$$

Writing $\beta=c/d$ in lowest terms and $m=2n$, the consumed radical
divides $|c|\,|md-2c|$. The ordinary-abc contact estimate and mixed-power
height bound give the strict absorption coefficient **602/679**. The
all-point trace estimate then forces $n=\beta$ for sufficiently large
fixed $B$, hence $X=2n\in\mathbb Z$.

For $X\ne0$, zero contact weight is impossible from the retained point,
ratio and factor equations; no new inverse coordinate is introduced.
The input $X=0$ is already integral and has a separate completeness
construction on the degenerate scaled-point stratum. Thus the proof treats
the whole rational zero set. It is a new detector proof, not a claimed
equivalence of complete zero sets with a differently normalized family.

### Rational completeness

For each nonzero integer $X$, take $A=[X]R_0$ and the same retained
Gaussian-prime congruences. The existing halving lemma makes the order
quotients annihilate $R_0$, so the reference progression works for odd
and negative inputs as well. With

$$H=[h]P,\quad U=[hX]R_0,\quad V=[(h-1)X]R_0,$$

one has $U-V=A$. All fixed point data and avoidance conditions are chosen
before prime selection. The actual weight is an exact product of the
selected primes divided by a square. Its selected-prime factors all lie
in the class $1\bmod8$, and the local factor calculations give positive
input valuations there. Deep two-adic denominators and large real
arguments supply

$$b>1,\qquad b\in\mathbb Q_2^{\times2},\qquad v_2(b)\le-4,$$

with square reduced denominator. The paper proves a pole-realization
lemma on precisely this locus using the retained simultaneous-twist
premise. The sign of $1-b$ is included in the rational witness formulas.
For $X=0$, all seven scaled point coordinates vanish, $L=L_0=1$, and
each block has $W=1$ and $b=w/4$; the same pole lemma completes the witness.

### Exact computational specification

The [coefficient package](../computation/quartic/abc74_v46) contains every
quadratic row and the full expanded quartic. The independent checker
imports no producer code. It reconstructs the 56 non-reference rows from
literal equations and binds the eight reference rows to their authenticated
complete source. It independently expands all 64 squares, proves uniform
row primitivity and the exact height and support, and checks the coefficient
of $X^4$ is one.

Additional exact certificates cover eleven rescaling identities, the
physical pole formula, zero-weight ideal identities, and all 54 non-pole
rows of the zero-input specialization. The two remaining pole blocks are
covered by the arithmetic realization lemma, rather than a finite test.

## Finite complement classes and the 21/9 target

The [finite-class theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/finite_complement_classes_20261116_v46.tex)
allows a finite set of levels $a_m=17m^2$ and a finite set of positive
square classes supported on the source's **original** exceptional prime
set $S$. For each actual detector input, it is sufficient to realize

$$d_i a_{m_i}(a_{m_i}-b_i)\in\mathbb Q^2,$$

with $m_i$ and $d_i$ chosen from those fixed finite sets. At every required
prime outside $S$, such a square excludes a negative odd valuation of
$b_i$. At all characters of the prepared point algebra, each $a_m-b_i$
is automatically a unit: otherwise the odd valuation at 17 would make
$a_m/w_i$ a square in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$.

Separate finite-algebra encodings for the finitely many choices, followed
by their product, implement this finite union with no new retained
coordinate. This also handles coincident radicands from different choices.
**If actual realization holds for every required integer input**, the
resulting counts are **21**, or **9** in either conditional source.

Taking the single level 17 and all exceptional square classes reduces
the missing realization condition to: both positive actual complements
$17(17-b_i)$ have even valuation at every prime outside $S$. This is a
weaker requirement than rational squareness. The set $S$ cannot be enlarged
to include a prime used by source soundness.

The paper also gives an infinite, exactly verified family of prepared
free inputs with nonsquare complement class 409. That class is allowed only
when $409\in S$. The family demonstrates the weaker coefficient condition;
it is not an actual native detector realization.

**The realization is unproved.** The established general counts remain
**25 and 13**. A common rational pole fiber without square complements
remains a separate sufficient route to **24**.

## Reproduction

With Python 3.12 and SymPy 1.14.0, run from the repository root:

```sh
python -B scripts/verify_v46.py --report /tmp/v46-verification.json
```

The verifier reconstructs the tables and certificates in temporary files
and compares them byte for byte. The retained suites and the 126-stage
legacy verifier authenticate the dependencies. The mathematical manuscripts
prove the infinite implications under the explicitly stated premises.
