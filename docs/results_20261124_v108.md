## The Pell classification closes the c-integrality gap

This is the principal new arithmetic improvement.

Suppose \(a\in3\mathbb Z\), \(c,d,\zeta\in\mathbb Q\), and

\[
d^2-(a^2-1)c^2=1,\qquad
T=d-(a-2)c\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
\zeta^2=2\bigl(ad+(a^2-1)c+1\bigr).
\]

Then **\(c,d\) are integers**. The theorem treats positive, negative, and zero \(a\).

Put \(D=4a-5\) and \(L_a=2a^2-2a-1\). The norm and shifted trace give

\[
(Dc-(a-2)T)^2=(a^2-1)T^2-D,
\]

so \(Dc\) is integral. Thus the reduced denominator \(N\) of \(c\) divides \(|D|\). The half-trace square is

\[
\zeta^2=2L_ac+2aT+2,\qquad 8L_a+3=D(4a+1).
\]

Since \(3\mid a\), \(D\) is odd and prime to three, hence \(\gcd(2L_a,N)=1\). The right side therefore has reduced denominator exactly \(N\). As it is a rational square, \(N\) must be a square.

The exact orbit classification now excludes every nonintegral possibility:

- For positive \(a\in3\mathbb Z\), the sole fractional orbit has denominator \(4a-5\equiv3\pmod4\).
- For \(a=-A<0\) with \(3\mid A\), the sole fractional orbit has denominator \(4A+5\equiv2\pmod3\). A possible additional orbit would require \(4A+5\) to be a square, excluded by this congruence.
- For \(a=0\), the only nonintegral values of \(c\) are \(\pm3/5,\pm4/5\).

All these denominators are nonsquare. Thus \(c\), and then \(d\), is integral.

### The source supplies \(3\mid a\) without circularity

The retained preliminary source reconstruction gives integer \(q,S,T_0,w,s\), before using c-integrality or the eventual power-of-two identity. Its prefix is

\[
n=q^{16},\quad M=q^{32}=n^2,\quad
r=S(M-n)+T_0(M-1),\quad a=(wM+1)rsM.
\]

If \(3\mid q\), then \(3\mid M\). Otherwise \(n\equiv M\equiv1\pmod3\), so \(3\mid r\). Hence \(3\mid a\) in both cases.

For the general and abc routes, preliminary integrality of \(q\) comes from the separate auxiliary power block with retained integer inputs. It does not require the first-Pell classification. The specified route uses its explicit integer power circuit.

Canonical completeness is also exact. The positive source witnesses have \(c=Y_{2r+1}(a)\) and \(d=X_{2r+1}(a)\), so

\[
ad+(a^2-1)c=X_{2r+2}(a)=2X_{r+1}(a)^2-1.
\]

Consequently \(\zeta=2X_{r+1}(a)\) supplies the new square. The literal row is quadratic because \(A_c=ac\) is already stored:

\[
\zeta^2-2ad-2aA_c+2c-2=0.
\]

The specified system already retained this row; only the general and abc systems need to restore it.

## Alternative source checkpoint

The [unary-source checkpoint](../undecidability/unary_outer_checkpoint_v109/README.md) proves a new outer compiler with three exponential values. For nonnegative integers A and M and sufficiently large P, put U=2^P, V=2^M and W=2^{P(2A+1)}. Divisibility of the central binomial coefficient by V is equivalent to four quadratic equations with an externally certified integer y and six further rational auxiliaries. The proof controls the rounding error and the exact two-adic residue needed for three squares, including A=M=0.

This is a proved outer lemma, not a complete new undecidability system. The lower exponential circuits, all three power graphs and the integer domain of y remain external. A separate algebraic-independence observation explains why a unary exponent variable does not automatically make the book's many exponential values rational functions of one value. Its scope is the stated arithmetic field model, not a universal lower bound on existential encodings. Compiling those remaining graphs is the next concrete task for this alternative source.
