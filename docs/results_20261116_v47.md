## Uniform norm-compatible preparation

The [norm-compatible scalar theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/norm_compatible_scalar_20261116_v47.tex)
uses the real quadratic field F=Q(√10657). Its exact input map has degree
eight and reflects rational integrality:

$$
U(X)=1+\frac{X(X-1)(X-2)(X-3)}{24},\qquad
\beta_*(X)=AU(X)^2+BU(X)+C,
$$

where the three explicitly specified positive integers A,B,C make all
coefficients integral. At a denominator prime of X, one term has a unique
negative valuation, including the exceptional two-adic case. Therefore
β*(X) is integral exactly when X is integral.

Four exact square identities give

$$
r_4(\beta_*(X))=
\beta_*(X)(\beta_*(X)-1)(\beta_*(X)-2401)(32\beta_*(X)+343)
\in N_{F/\mathbb Q}(F^\times).
$$

The scalar is

$$
\rho_{*,L}(X)=
\frac{34^2\,76249^L M_0\,r_4(\beta_*(X))}
{\left(1+102\cdot34 M_0(76249^{(L+1)/2}+1)r_4(\beta_*(X))\right)^2},
$$

with the fixed positive norm M₀=r₄(C). It is an explicit norm for every
rational input. Its valuation at 76249 is L for integral inputs there,
and L−8+32e when v₇₆₂₄₉(X)=−e<0. Thus this required consumed prime is
never made exceptional. Integer inputs have square reduced denominator,
local square classes at 2,3,17, and exact valuations (4,8,2).

The four finite contacts and the infinite contact of the source height
argument are retained. The fixed prime divisors of M₀ may be omitted
from the contact radical at the cost of a fixed height constant; 76249
is not among them. The source transfer keeps 25 coordinates and the same
strict height exponents, with a sufficiently large ineffective odd L.
No degree or coefficient record is assigned to the larger input map.

Compatible prime preparation makes every selected prime a norm from F.
The exact contact weight is a rational square times their product and
the norm scalar. The two bare conics

$$ u_j^2-10657y_j^2=4w_j/17 $$

are consequently rationally soluble for every prepared integer input.
The conditional sources already have a square-times-prime-product weight
and admit the same norm-conic preparation without replacing their scalar.

The required first coordinate is **mⱼ Tr(𝒲ⱼ⁻¹)**, with 𝒲ⱼ the actual
detector product and mⱼ from fixed admissible levels. The theorem does
not prescribe this coordinate. This is the outstanding arithmetic
incidence required for 21/9; solving the bare conics leaves it open.

## An exact actual prescribed-trace benchmark

The [admissible-level and native-trace theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/admissible_levels_native_traces_20261116_v47.tex)
allows fixed rational levels a with nonnegative even valuations outside
the original exceptional set S. If d has even valuations outside S,
then a rational solution of q²=d a(a−b) excludes every negative odd
valuation of b outside S. Whole-algebra complement guards must be proved
for these general levels; they are not asserted to be automatic.

An exact conditional-source tuple has X=1, B=3, A=P, H=U=2P and

$$
\lambda=\frac{32003072}{10621537},\qquad
t_1=125012.
$$

The second argument equals the actual root-height scale times λ. Both
unchanged detector products are squares in Q(√2). The identity

$$
2a(a-b)=
\left(\frac{2wN(W)W_1}{W_0}\right)^2,
\qquad a=wN(W),\quad b=\frac{4w}{\operatorname{Tr}(W^{-1})^2},
$$

gives two admissible levels, whose exact constants are frozen in the
certificate. Fixed positive S-unit rescalings γⱼ convert those levels
to 17mⱼ² and yield

$$
\left(m_j\operatorname{Tr}(W_j^{-1})\right)^2-2Y_j^2
=\frac{4\gamma_jw_j}{17}.
$$

The traces and coupled arguments remain the actual source values.
All 64 geometric characters, every source denominator, and all 128
complement guards are checked. A sign involution gives X=−1 with the
same constants. Exact rational-pair, number-field and polynomial-quotient
implementations independently verify the data.

This is a two-input benchmark for a fixed rescaled source. B=3 is not a
certified defining shift, and these constants have not been proved to
work for all integers. The required uniform rational realization remains
unproved.

The separate [polynomial-level local theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/polynomial_levels_local_20261116_v47.tex)

## Quadratic reference and numerator covers

The [reference-cover theorem](../papers/elliptic_charts/reference_covers_20261116_v47.tex)
uses a=e²=x(P), c=y(P)/x(P), D=a(a−c²). Two quadrics in three field
coordinates describe an étale square-root cover:

$$ z=rr',\qquad a(r^2+r'^2)+2cez+Dz^2=1. $$

It is polynomially isomorphic to the four-coordinate presentation

$$
s^2+DR^2=1,\qquad t^2+DQ^2=1,\qquad
ce\,st=-a+D(R+Q)+aDRQ.
$$

Both reciprocal abscissas R,Q are squares at every field-valued solution.
The inverse formulas have constant denominators and include all four
points over H=O or H=P. Under the retained elliptic group premise, its
K-points cover exactly 2E(K). Homogeneous input scaling keeps every row
quadratic and identifies the full zero-input fiber.

Three field coordinates per numerator give the physical weighted product
F=β²f(H)f(U), its auxiliary square and the ratio through quadrics. The
two detector factors remain quadratic with those products. A separate
Jacobi chart expresses x(U−V) as a square and recovers its original
rational trace with one additional rational helper. Its zero-scale fiber
consists exactly of two opposite torsion configurations.

The two numerator points have different reference scalings. Their full
difference equations have not been encoded within a saved coordinate
budget. These local algebraic improvements therefore do not establish a
complete quartic below 74. All recovery coordinates, square helpers and
boundary conditions remain part of that unresolved count.

## Separate obstruction and next arithmetic target

The [signed finite-class obstruction](../negative_results/papers/pole_parameters/finite_nonsquare_class_obstruction_20261116_v47.tex)
is recorded only in the negative-results collection. For the original
general scalar, every finite set consisting entirely of nontrivial
rational square classes fails on an arithmetic progression of integer
inputs. This applies to arbitrary guarded rational auxiliary tuples and
all rational multipliers mⱼ in levels 17mⱼ², uniformly in the odd source
exponent. The three contact root fields, Chebotarev and CRT supply the
progression. The rational-square class and the redesigned scalar are
outside this obstruction's conclusion.

The two precise research obligations are uniform prescribed-trace
incidence for the norm-compatible preparation, and a complete quadratic
pairing of the differently scaled numerators with the original trace.
A common rational pole fiber remains the separate sufficient route to24.
No number-field counterexample or theorem for every number field is
asserted.

## Reproduction

With Python 3.12 and SymPy 1.14.0, run from the repository root:

```sh
python -B scripts/verify_v47.py --report /tmp/v47-verification.json
```

The verifier reconstructs every new table and certificate in temporary
files and compares the bytes. The retained verification suites continue
to check the complete defining-polynomial constructions and their pinned
dependencies. The mathematical manuscripts prove the infinite statements;
finite certificate checks do not replace those arguments.
