# Bounded-variable undecidability: v89

## Current result and scope

The new [joint-certificate theorem](../undecidability/papers/shared_integer_transfers.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/shared_integer_transfers.pdf)) gives rational
solvability undecidability transfers in **99**, **117**, and **69** unknowns.
The first two require no additional abc hypothesis. The last uses ordinary
rational abc. Every route retains its source's stated arithmetic premises;
this work does not independently establish all those premises.

| Route | Rational unknowns | Degree strictly below | Parameter qualification |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| General two-detector source | **99** | `2^526` | Sufficient fixed odd exponent is ineffective |
| Specified three-detector source | **117** | `2^591` | Specified source exponent `L=143` |
| Ordinary-abc source | **69** | `2^377` | Sufficient fixed odd coefficient is ineffective |

These are fixed finite-degree results, not quartic results. The new
[quartic systems](../undecidability/papers/shared_quartic_checkpoint.tex)
reach **2086 / 2278 / 846**, but their undecidability interpretation has
an additional, explicitly unresolved source-universality premise `U_em`.
The target of a quartic bound near 263 remains open in this work.

## Mixed-domain transfer and the first reductions

Sun's Theorem 1.1(i) represents a fixed nonrecursive recursively enumerable
set with eight arbitrary integer unknowns and one nonnegative integer
unknown. The instance is a coefficient parameter and is not counted.
The rational polynomial

```math
L(s,t,u)=s^2+t^2+u^2+u
```

is at least `-1/4`, so its integral values are nonnegative. Every nonnegative
integer is represented: apply the three-square theorem to `4n+1`, where
exactly one square is odd. Substituting `L` both in the universal equation
and in its integrality certificate costs three rational witnesses and
removes the original nonnegative input.

The [initial proof](../undecidability/papers/transfer_general_degree.tex)
gives `N=9m+2`, hence **227 / 245 / 101** for the 25-, 27- and 11-coordinate
predicates. More generally, `r` integer inputs and `s` nonnegative inputs
cost `(r+s)m+2s`. The old common degree bound is

```text
3362086470453239832602365249023837055668275467414816896671079680
```

This is `2*eta(58,4)`, using the explicit mixed-domain construction of
Bayer–David–Haßler–Matiyasevich–Schleicher and the proved universal pair
`(58,4)`. Their qualification about the smaller pair `(32,12)`, whose full
classical proof was not supplied, is preserved. The old construction
remains a useful lower-degree tradeoff.

## One guarded encoder, including all zero-root cases

The [finite-algebra theorem](../undecidability/papers/joint_finite_algebra_encoder.tex)
constructs one polynomial in a base tuple and one extra rational coordinate
that detects a rational character of a finite free algebra at which a
designated product guard is nonzero. It works for nonreduced algebras,
including several independent nilpotent directions.

The proof uses multiplication matrices for the guard and its products with
algebra generators. Characteristic polynomials of tensor-difference matrices
bound every nonzero difference of character values. After stratifying by
the last nonzero coefficient, an explicitly large weighted sum separates
all permitted characters from each other and from zero. A rational root of
`det(I-uM)` then selects a rational character by Galois descent. A product
of sums of squares encodes the coefficient strata without new variables.

Denominators are guarded only at the selected character. For a monic
finite-root equation, replacing a root by its common denominator times the
root gives a monic polynomial over the preceding algebra. Denominators
may vanish at other characters. This distinction is essential for a
correct argument with repeated roots.

Merging the nine independent encoders first lowers the count to `9m-6`,
giving **219 / 237 / 93**. For the abc source, feed each finite-root system
`X_i^2+1` first, so its external zero-input factor is unnecessary. No ordinary
witnesses are shared at this intermediate stage.

## Simultaneous witnesses give 99 and 117

The general sources have nine point roles. Seven can be shared:
`S,T,Z,H,US,UT,UZ`. Only `A_i` and `UA_i` remain private. Their common
scalar and reference choices are proved by preparing the finite union of
required supports and then taking a common refinement of the finitely
many local and real conditions.

One contact coordinate suffices after `lambda_i=lambda+100*i`. Each original
normalized contact lies in `(0,13)`, so the translated intervals are disjoint.
The source prime-pattern premise already covers any fixed finite rational
contact family with finite avoidance and compatible annihilation. The new
proof explicitly controls signed contacts: selected primes lie in
`(3u/4,5u/4)`, while their even elliptic reduction-group orders lie below
`3u/2`, making each selected prime coprime to every such order. This is the
compatibility needed for the common reference refinement.

All pole parameters remain private. The construction uses the **v88
class-seven scalar and Bhargava–Burungale product poles**, and does not
reintroduce the former native-pole converse premise. The one finite-algebra
encoder permits all terminal zero roots. The coordinate counts are

```math
k+14+4k+1+2ek+1=(5+2e)k+16.
```

With two or three detectors this is `9k+16` or `11k+16`. At `k=9`, adding
the ternary wrapper's net two coordinates gives **99** or **117**.
The simultaneous theorem includes zero and repeated integer inputs.

## Ordinary-abc strengthening to 69

The abc source has an external zero-input factor, so the joint source
first replaces its input by `y_i=X_i^2+1`. This is everywhere positive,
and a rational `X_i` is integral exactly when `y_i` is integral.
Only the reference point is shared; two point roles per input remain private.

Its contact midpoint is input-dependent and unbounded, so a translation
alone does not prove separation. The correct substitution is
`z_i=a_i*(z+100*i)`. The normalized contact positions then lie in disjoint
intervals `(-100*i-2,-100*i+2)`. The midpoint is a two-adic square on the
prepared integer witnesses, which preserves the required local preparation.
The partial derivative defining the original detector is taken before
this substitution, with the original contact variable held fixed.

The total is `k+2+4k+1+2k+1=7k+4`. The mixed-domain transfer at `k=9`
therefore uses **69** rational unknowns. This route requires ordinary
rational abc and the remaining non-pole source premises.

## Exact degree ledgers and fixture qualifications

The complete [source circuits](../undecidability/joint_sources) contain
42,307 / 59,119 / 13,802 coefficient nodes and 651 / 921 / 222 retained
guards. The joint algebra ranks are `2^68`, `2^77`, and `2^47`.
The finite-algebra matrices need not be materialized to certify degree:
exact operator-degree induction through the circuit bounds every entry.
Only two separating generators are needed for each point block because
`W=U^2/2-A` on every rational fiber.

| Route | Separating list size, including guard | Entry-degree bound for guard products | Mixed degree ceiling |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| General | 69 | 2,302,122 | `<2^526` |
| Specified | 78 | 4,126,278 | `<2^591` |
| Ordinary abc | 48 | 3,309,980 | `<2^377` |

The [verifier](../undecidability/verify_joint_sources.py) reproduces each
compressed specification byte for byte, checks all finite-root dependencies,
verifies the point multiplication table symbolically and recomputes the
integer bounds. Separate exact encoder checks cover a quadratic field with
no rational character, a nonmonogenic nilpotent algebra, a nilpotent guard,
and a denominator that vanishes only at excluded characters.

The stored general `L=143` and abc `B=3` members are **algebraic fixtures**.
They do not certify a sufficient defining member. The 117-variable route
uses the specified sufficient `L=143` source. The degree estimates are uniform:
`L` occurs only in coefficient constants with the same odd-parity extraction
pattern, and `B` is a degree-zero coefficient parameter. A defining member
must be selected under the source theorem before interpreting the fixture's
circuit as an undecidability reduction.

## Quartic compilation and the source correction

The [direct compilation](../undecidability/papers/jones1980_quartic_transfer.tex)
proves a reversible rational quadratic construction from an explicitly
emended 28-variable positive-integer system. It uses twelve integrality
requirements. Other source values become integers through polynomial
identities, rational roots of monic integer equations, or a geometric quotient.
A reciprocal chart, a cofinite restriction `x>2z`, a Pell gap and powers of
an integral Pell unit remove unnecessary coordinates and positivity witnesses.

The source's huge fixed exponent `5^60` is computed by a 169-step signed
addition chain. The generic outer core has 255 coordinates and 223 rows.
Removing a product register and sharing three existing input-square registers
with the actual integrality blocks gives **2195 / 2387 / 911**.
A further simultaneous-reference theorem gives:

| Integer interface | Shared quartic checkpoint | Quadratic residuals |
|:--|--:|--:|
| General 163 | **2086** | **1776** |
| Specified 179 | **2278** | **1944** |
| Ordinary-abc 56 | **846** | **728** |

All sparse residuals are supplied and verified. Their sums of squares have
exact degree four. However, **universality of the precise emended source is
not yet verified**. The original Jones 1980 display prints
`4*alpha*gamma`, with Greek alpha, whereas the builder uses `4*a*gamma`.
Matiyasevich's author-written 1999 chapter supports Latin a but prints the
impossible Pell relation `(p^2-1)k^2=1=tau^2` in place of the corrected
`(p^2-1)k^2+1=tau^2`. The independent Pell congruence supports the intended
correction but does not prove the full source equivalence.

The additional premise **`U_em`** is stated in the manuscripts, all tables,
all certificates and the registry. Without it, the algebraic construction
and source equivalence remain proved; the undecidability label does not.
This premise is unrelated to abc. The earlier **2539 / 2779 / 934** counts
were committed first as historical source-audit checkpoints.

The [literal MT audit](../undecidability/papers/mt_source_audit.tex) independently
proves **62 variables / 50 quadratics → 57 / 45**, with positive reconstruction.
Its final Pell coefficient is `G-1`, while the classical form is `G^2-1`,
and other substitutions also require explanation. This is not an independent
reproof of the historically quoted universal **57/47** result.

## Further proved limitation and next tasks

The [integrality obstruction](../undecidability/papers/polynomial_integrality_obstruction.tex)
now applies to arbitrary rational-coefficient polynomial integer-value tests:
fewer than `r` such tests cannot recognize all of `Z^r` in `Q^r`.
A positive-dimensional fiber, a branch at infinity, infinitely many
local embeddings and rational approximation give counterexamples with
unbounded prime-power denominators. It does not forbid shared existential
witnesses or additional equations of a particular source system.

The [further-reduction checkpoint](../undecidability/checkpoints/further_reductions_20261123.md)
records a specific squareclass obstruction to a proposed common-pole shortcut.
The next concrete tasks are to obtain and audit the complete Jones 1982 proof,
prove a further simultaneous realization theorem for private point or pole
parameters, and compile any resulting combined system directly as quadratics.
