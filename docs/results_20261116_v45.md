# Current constructions and verification

Every count includes the input. Fixed parameters are coefficients. The
[main table](../README.md) and [current specifications](../data/results_20261116_v45.json)
state the arithmetic hypotheses and effective parameters. The inherited
arithmetic foundations are not declared independently audited.

## The 195-variable quartic

The [quartic theorem](../papers/quartic/quartic195_20261116_v45.tex) gives a
sum of **175 primitive integer quadratic squares** in **195 variables**.
It has exact degree **4**, integer content **1**, and **2,956 nonzero
monomials** for every fixed $C=149^N$, $N\ge1$. Its exact height is

$$
H(P_N)=\max\{126616122944180358034867798936,\;149^{2N+2}\}.
$$

The first term dominates for $N=1,\ldots,5$; the second dominates for
$N\ge6$. This crossover is a coefficient fact, not a sufficient defining
parameter. The sufficient defining threshold remains ineffective.

### Algebraic mechanism and complete domain

The source Lucas residual $F$, its second component $F'$, and the
stored square components $S,S'$ satisfy

$$
S=C(F^2+8F'^2),\qquad S'=CFF'.
$$

The triangular affine change

$$
F=\widehat F+C^{-1},\quad
S=\widehat S+2\widehat F+C^{-1},\quad
S'=\widehat S'+F'
$$

centers the square and removes the corresponding constant and cross terms
from the detector. A certified Laurent coordinate change then eliminates
the inverse-weight square. The literal equation $qw=1$ remains in the
final system, so every rational solution lies in the domain of the inverse
coordinate map. All other equations are retained through exact identities.
The input coordinate is unchanged.

A further change $y_i=C^{k_i}z_i$, with fixed integer exponents, makes
every quadratic row linear in $C$. The coefficients multiplying $C$
have absolute value at most 149. This coordinate change is invertible for
every fixed nonzero $C$.

Thus the complete rational zero sets are in input-preserving bijection.
The arithmetic premises, completeness witnesses, and sufficient threshold
are exactly those of the specified source. No additional abc assumption or
nonzero constraint is introduced.

### Exact specification and independent verification

The [coefficient package](../computation/quartic/affine_laurent195_v45) contains
the full quadratic rows, expanded quartic, and transformation certificate.
The source is authenticated by its SHA-256 digest. The independent checker
imports neither the producer nor the optimization or Laurent-conversion code.
It reconstructs all 175 source-to-final identities, checks the deleted
identity and literal inverse guard, and independently expands the sum of
squares. It also verifies that every coordinate occurs and that the
coefficient of $X^4$ is one.

Uniform row primitivity follows from exact gcd certificates. No coefficient
profile vanishes at an allowed parameter: after translating $C=149+t$,
each signed profile has positive constant coefficient and nonnegative other
coefficients. For $N\ge6$, the differences between $149^2C^2$ and both
signs of every coefficient have nonnegative coefficients after translating
$C=149^6+t$. Exact checks of the five remaining integer exponents complete
the height certificate.

## The explicit 257-variable quartic

The [explicit theorem](../papers/quartic/quartic257_20261116_v45.tex) gives
**257 variables**, **229 primitive integer quadratic squares**, exact
degree **4**, integer content **1**, and **3,679 nonzero monomials**.
Its sufficient fixed parameter is **N=9682**, with exact coefficient height

$$H(P_{9682})=149^{19366}.$$

For every $N\ge1$, the full height formula is

$$
H(P_N)=\max\{484991541504995159662206970923766531374664106904986910720,
149^{2N+2}\}.
$$

The second term dominates for $N\ge13$. This coefficient crossover is
separate from the sufficient defining value $N=9682$.

The affine centering now uses an existing inverse coordinate $q$:

$$
F=\widehat F+q,\qquad
S=\widehat S+2\widehat F+q,\qquad
S'=\widehat S'+F'.
$$

The literal inverse row $qw=1$ permits a Laurent elimination of the
stored coordinate $w^2$. A subsequent fixed-parameter coordinate scaling
reduces the coefficient height. The input and the complete rational zero
sets are preserved, so the effective sufficient parameter and all arithmetic
premises transfer exactly from the specified source.

The [coefficient package](../computation/quartic/affine_laurent257_v45)
contains all quadratic rows, expanded quartic coefficients, coordinate maps,
and an independent checker. That checker reconstructs all 229 final rows
directly from the authenticated source, verifies the deleted identity and
inverse guard, and proves the support and height uniformly for all $N\ge1$.
It imports neither the producer nor an optimization routine.

## Actual rational square complements

The [complement theorem](../papers/pole_parameters/guarded_complement_reduction_20261116_v45.tex)
proves a new guard statement. At an actual native point tuple, the entire
finite point algebra splits over $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$. Write the guarded
detector input as $b_i=w_iS_i^2$, where $w_i\in\mathbb Q^\times$ is
independent of the point-algebra character. Preparation gives even
$v_{17}(w_i)$.

If $17(17-b_i)$ vanished at any character, then $w_i/17$ would be a
square in $K$. A rational square in $K$ has rational square class 1 or
2, both with even valuation at 17. The odd valuation of $w_i/17$
contradicts this. Both complement norm guards are therefore automatic.

A rational square complement itself excludes negative odd valuations of
$b_i$ outside 17. Consequently, **if the two actual complements can be
realized as rational squares for every required integer input**, both
retained pole pairs can be removed. The guarded finite-algebra construction
then has **21 variables**, or **9** in either conditional source. The
paper proves this implication and accounts for the two finite square roots,
all denominator and point guards, and the single encoder coordinate.

The native realization assertion remains unproved. These counts are
conditional research targets and do not replace the established 25/13
records. A common rational pole fiber without square complements remains
the weaker sufficient route to 24 variables.

The same paper proves that the free-input quadric section gives infinitely
many prepared inputs at one fixed common pole parameter. This includes
square reduced denominators and all local preparation conditions. Matching
these free inputs to the actual native reciprocal traces is still required.

## A stronger height budget for reference tests

The [mixed-power theorem](../papers/elliptic_heights/mixed_power_height_20261116_v45.tex)
proves, under its explicitly stated fixed weak-abc inequality,

$$
h(x)\le
\frac{n+\kappa(n-2)}{n(2n-\kappa(n+2))}
h(x^{2n}+y^2)
+\frac{\log K}{2n-\kappa(n+2)}
$$

for every integer $n\ge3$ and $1\le\kappa<2n/(n+2)$. The proof gives
a prime-by-prime radical majorant with no omitted valuation case.

At $n=8$, $\kappa=101/100$, the input-height coefficient is
$703/2360$. With the ordinary-abc eight-contact bound, a product residual
$\beta(n_0-\beta)$, $\beta=X^2+3$, has strict absorption coefficient
$1406/2065<1$. The scale $B+X^{16}+T^2$ requires one additional shared
quadratic coordinate. A replacement reference test must therefore save at
least two coordinates elsewhere to produce a net improvement, while proving
the stated local divisibility, all-point height, and rational completeness.
The theorem supplies this height budget; it does not supply that missing
reference test.

## Reproduction

With Python 3.12 and SymPy 1.14.0, run from the repository root:

```sh
python -B scripts/verify_v45.py --report /tmp/v45-verification.json
```

The current verifier reconstructs all new tables and certificates in
temporary files. The retained suites and the 126-stage legacy verifier
authenticate their dependencies. The mathematical manuscripts prove the
infinite implications; computational checks establish the identities and
coefficient assertions they explicitly test.
