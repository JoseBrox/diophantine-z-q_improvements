# A 59-variable abc quartic and the remaining reductions — 19 November 2026

The ordinary rational abc quartic is reduced from **62 to 59 total variables**, including the input. The new polynomial is a sum of **51 squares of integral quadrics**, has **873 coordinate monomials**, exact degree four and integer content one. Its exact coefficient height is

```math
H(F_B)=\max\{22838305425411,\;B^2+4092\}
```

for every positive integer `B`. Every sufficiently large fixed odd `B` is defining **under ordinary rational abc and the explicitly retained parent arithmetic premises**. The sufficient defining threshold remains ineffective. The height formula alone does not give such a threshold.

The [complete proof](../papers/quartic_existence/single_derivative59_20261119_v79.tex), [quadratic equations](../computation/variable_reductions_v79/abc/quartic59/quadrics.json), [expanded coefficient table](../computation/variable_reductions_v79/abc/quartic59/polynomial.json), and [independent checker](../computation/variable_reductions_v79/abc/check59.py) specify the construction. ## Why the conditional quartic loses three variables

The construction uses one derivative contact in place of two contact arguments and two pole blocks. With the retained scaled trace, set

```math
M=B+X^8+T_{\mathrm{sc}}^2,\qquad a=M^4,\qquad
x=a-z,\quad y=a+z,
```

and

```math
G(t)=\prod_{r\in\{-8,-7,-2,-1,3,4,5,6\}}(t+r),\qquad
F=G(x)G(y),\qquad u=-16F/F_a,\qquad c=1/u.
```

The source detector retains its shifted norm equation

```math
D\operatorname{Tr}(W)=\operatorname{Nm}(W-1).
```

The derivative quotient can acquire negative odd valuations at primes dividing its denominator. A direct use of `1+uD²` would leave a completeness gap when the subsequently chosen elliptic points acquire denominators at those primes. The corrected construction uses

```math
g=\frac{1+uD^2}{1+u^3D^2},\qquad
b=\frac{u g^2}{(1+u)^2}.
```

For any odd valuation `μ=v_p(u)`, writing `d=v_p(D)` gives

```math
v_p(g)=\min(0,\mu+2d)-\min(0,3\mu+2d).
```

If `μ<0`, this is nonnegative, so `b` has positive odd valuation regardless of the actual point denominators. If `μ>0`, the native pole obstruction forces `d>−3μ/4`. The norm selector then makes the four original elliptic comparison tests positive at a good slot. This recovers local integrality and the congruence `X(m−X)=0 mod p` used in soundness.

The ordinary-abc derivative estimate and signed mixed-power height bound give the exact positive margin

```math
\frac{193}{100}-2\frac{501}{997}
=\frac{92221}{99700}>0.
```

Together with the retained trace-index bound, this excludes noninteger inputs for sufficiently large fixed `B`. The same derivative estimate supplies the eventual guard `u≠−1` before the pole argument is applied. The cleared normalizer equations therefore recover both of their rational denominators without extra inverse coordinates.

For completeness, the retained simultaneous prime-pattern input supplies sixteen simple contacts. At real infinity and the prepared finite places, `u∼z²/a`, `c∼a/z²` and `D=O(c²)`, so `g→1`. These conditions give the actual native input `b` a square reduced denominator, the required two-adic square class and `0<b<1`. The retained small-input pole lemma then gives rational witnesses. The proof includes the zero-input fiber and all allowed finite torsion differences.

Finally, the contact polynomial has leading coefficients `a₈=1`, `a₇=0`, `a₆=−102`. The two identities

```math
tG'(t)-8G(t)=P_6(t),\qquad
(4t^2-102)G'(t)-32tG(t)=Q_6(t)
```

have right sides of degree at most six. They recover the derivative from the coordinates `t,t²,t⁴,G,G′`, including `t=0`, because their derivative coefficients cannot vanish simultaneously. This removes the two cubic-power registers. Two coordinates implement the uniform normalizer. The net count is **59**, not the incomplete 57-coordinate intermediate calculation.

## The other requested reductions

The general polynomial remains at **25 variables**, the general quartics at **165 and 180**, and the unrestricted-degree abc construction at **11**. No smaller complete construction in these four cases is established here.

For the general quartics, the [new parity theorem](../papers/quartic/hyperbolic_parity_20261119_v79.tex) explains why one proposed helper deletion fails. A retained point equation forces equal integral weights for the full hyperbolic pair `U,V`, while a detector equation would require

```math
1+2w(U)=2+2w(B).
```

The [four-row parity certificate](../computation/variable_reductions_v79/quartics) gives `0=1` modulo two. This excludes the specified retained chart and termwise integral diagonal Laurent compilation, with any number of stored unit powers. It does not exclude other elliptic charts, variable-dependent combinations of equations, nonlinear changes of coordinates or smaller quartics by different methods.

The next constructive targets are:

1. For **25 variables**, merge the two contact conditions while proving a suitable bound for the loss of odd prime support. An uncontrolled product can cancel odd valuations and does not preserve the argument.
2. For **165 and 180 variables**, find a smaller scalar elliptic chart that supplies both its reciprocal values and the required logarithmic ratio, or share helpers across blocks through a new exact compilation.
3. For **11 variables**, realize the actual intrinsic pole fibers with one fewer independent rational parameter, or replace an elliptic point role while preserving index recovery. Local solubility and a point on a freely chosen auxiliary fiber do not supply this realization.
4. For **59 variables**, examine further sharing between the contact circuit and the normalizer while retaining every denominator guard and both directions of the defining equivalence.

The broader 21/9 targets remain unproved. No global variable-count minimum is asserted.
