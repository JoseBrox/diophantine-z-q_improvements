# One contact gauge and an independent Pell reduction

This checkpoint lowers the complete general quartic systems from
**1601 / 1837** variables to **1176 / 1346**, with **1092 / 1238** quadratic
residuals. The ordinary-abc checkpoint falls from **846** to **837**, with
**725** residuals. The instance input remains a coefficient parameter.

These are exact algebraic checkpoints. Their undecidability interpretation
still requires `U_em`, universality of the precise emended positive-integer
source, and the chosen integer predicate's retained arithmetic premises.
The first two introduce no additional abc hypothesis. This is not a claim
that every inherited arithmetic premise has been independently established.

## The two reductions

The [global-gauge proof](../undecidability/papers/global_gauge_quartic_checkpoint.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/global_gauge_quartic_checkpoint.pdf)) replaces
the twelve private contact sums by one sum over all contact families. The
private aggregate traces and pole witnesses remain independent. The proof
establishes the required valuation bounds for every rational solution,
before any disjointness of contacts is invoked. Disjoint contacts are used
only in constructing witnesses for integer inputs. At a foreign contact,
the altered comparison factor enters as a square, preserving the parity
needed by the local pole condition. Fixed-place limits are controlled
directly; no height bound for the new common sum is assumed.

That change permits more common physical coordinates. An exact polynomial
identity also removes a private field from every input-point chart, without
dividing by a coordinate that might vanish. Reordering three common
comparison factors removes further duplicate products. The fully compiled
counts are **1185 / 1355** variables and **1095 / 1241** rows.

The [Pell-index proof](../undecidability/papers/pell_index_optimization.tex)
([PDF](../undecidability/papers/pell_index_optimization.pdf)) is independent
of source universality. It proves the exact three-Pell certificate from
descent, a square-divisibility step, and a trace-residue lemma. It replaces

```math
G=a+F^2(d^2-a)
```

by the existentially equivalent bridge

```math
G=a+F^2(c+1-a).
```

The first norm can use the monomial `d^2` directly, so the old separate
square register disappears. A signed-witness version and the remaining
Pell inequality prove that the positivity wrappers for `I` and `c-(2r+1)`
are unnecessary. Finally the identity

```math
X_{2r+1}(a)-1=(a-1)(Y_{r+1}(a)+Y_r(a))^2
```

permits the single equation

```math
zeta^2=(a-1)(d-1)
```

to replace the three-coordinate positivity wrapper for `gamma`. The other
equations then imply `gamma>0`. The saving is
`1+3+3+(3-1)=9` coordinates and three rows. All twelve integer predicates
remain; positive Pell witnesses can be reconstructed in the reverse
direction. The theorem also has a version for arbitrary prescribed indices
at least two.

## Findings that prevent invalid shortcuts

The [negative-input proof](../undecidability/papers/negative_mixed_witness.tex)
gives, for every mixed universal source polynomial and every instance, a
specialization with

```math
Q(n)=25^8(n+9)^4(n+7)^4.
```

Thus the remaining eight integer conditions do not force the ninth input
to be nonnegative. The published 82/100/52 finite-wrapper theorem keeps the
ninth integrality and nonnegativity requirements.

The [point-chart paper](../undecidability/papers/point_chart_research.tex)
proves the coordinate elimination used above. It also gives precisely
scoped obstructions to identifying distinct alternate numerator points and
to covering the relevant elliptic orbit by a finite chart over one
input-independent rational parameter. Those statements do not rule out
input-dependent charts or establish a global variable lower bound.

The source audit now isolates a further discrepancy. The older printed
parameter is `p=2*w*s^2*r^2*n^2`, whereas the natural identification with the
modern binomial bridge gives `p=2*w*s^2*r^2*n^6`. Every table in this
checkpoint preserves the former expression literally. The local Pell
optimization works with either exponent; that fact does not settle
universality of the whole source. The alleged universal 57-variable,
47-quadratic system has not been independently reproved. Its literal
62/50-to-57/45 reduction remains a separate qualified audit result.
