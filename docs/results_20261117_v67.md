## Two exact obstructions

For a shared special factor, the two physical contact directions force a residual unit-exponent difference `(0,-3)`. This difference is absent from both stated unit bases, excluding that shared allocation.

The stronger obstruction uses one literal rational reference row and three field factors. Projecting a Laurent exponent `(a,b)` to `a+b`, their quadratic terms force

```math
r_{01}-r_{00}=12.
```

The reference row and the first factor imply `r00>=0`. The constant in the second factor implies `r01<=6` for the original unit basis and `r01<=2` for the modified basis. Both contradict the forced difference. Full-square monomials force equal real and imaginary row multipliers, so that convention does not restrict the allowed diagonal transformations.

The proof permits arbitrary real scaling weights and uses no bounded search. Ten nonnegative integer multiples of necessary degree inequalities give the exact contradictions `0<=-12` and `0<=-20`. The selected factor monomials are reconstructed from their physical formulas and cannot cancel against the distinct factor outputs.
