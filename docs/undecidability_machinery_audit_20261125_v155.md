# Transfer audit: integer-definition machinery for the undecidability compilers

Date: 2026-11-25. This is a focused compatibility audit of the current integer-definition constructions and the arithmetic interfaces of the v150 undecidability compiler, not a new independent audit of every historical arithmetic premise.

| Machinery examined | Transfer decision |
|---|---|
| Weighted abscissa sums and coefficient bounds (`computation/weighted_explicit178_20261124/README.md`) | Retain the effective specified comparison bound already imported in the general construction. Do not substitute a trace-only height bound without its hypotheses. |
| Asymmetric contacts and the reciprocal gate (`computation/asymmetric_contact54_20261124/README.md`, `computation/reciprocal_gate53_20261124/README.md`) | The reciprocal native pole is already used in v150. These motivate exact elimination of the remaining affine seed and contact register, implemented in v155. |
| Class-seven BB pole and finite algebra (`docs/bb_direct_route_k_replacement_20261124.md`, `papers/few_variables25/bb_product_degree_transfer_20261123_v88.tex`) | The degree-seven pole and the finite-algebra compression are useful at unrestricted degree. A small number of free coordinates in these high-degree formulas does not imply an equally small quadratic circuit. No uncompiled quartic saving is credited. |
| Derivative contact and radical estimates, v63/v126 | Strengthened in v152 to a split dihedral degree-twelve pencil with arbitrarily many disjoint rational contact groups. Rescale the derivative coefficients before coupling the moments. The height center can then use fourth instead of twelfth power. v155 further retains denominator primes of the height root and eliminates a derivative register. |
| Adaptive joint quotient, shared charge and cap (`papers/elliptic_divisibility/adaptive_joint_quotient_20261124/README.md`) | The adaptive quotient mechanism can force exact divisibility under its height and CRT assumptions. It is not yet a replacement for independently integrality-certified unbounded quotient labels: the uniform logarithmic height cap needed for universal-source soundness remains open. No saving is booked. |
| Direct index v127 and coefficient anchor v129 | Preserve the existing unrestricted transfer using integral elliptic labels and degree-dependent precision. A quartic improvement needs an explicit arithmetic circuit at the required precision, not just substitution into a high-degree representation. |
| Sun, [arXiv:2609.01594v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01594v2), §§3–7 | Retain the project's previously accepted ten-integer coding theorem. The eight-input intermediate proposition with divisibility and square conditions has integral-domain hypotheses. Rational witnesses for square roots of integral radicands are automatically integral; the quotient for divisibility is not. The variable in the congruence construction may be chosen in the indicated residue class, not prescribed arbitrarily. No completed replacement source or lower quartic count follows merely by interpreting the eight inputs as rational. |

The new dihedral pencil construction is proved in [v152](../undecidability/abc_dihedral_contact_v152/README.md). It preserves the 52 Jones source rows and twelve integer targets, including the common pole and full local-to-global argument, with stronger radical estimates. The exact affine substitutions and improved valuation analysis are in [v155](../undecidability/affine_elimination_v155/README.md). All arithmetic assumptions of the parent compilers remain retained, and the general choice of large coefficient remains ineffective.

For the shared Pell question, the new [v153 bilinear obstruction](../undecidability/bilinear_pell_obstruction_v153/README.md) closes the product and shifted-product candidates even for positive quotients. The monic two-moment repair is sound but spends the original two integer predicates. The [v154 power-sum construction](../undecidability/high_genus_pell_v154/README.md) preserves canonical completeness and prevents the infinite rational-point deformation on each fixed fiber by raising its genus. Neither finiteness nor the local tests establish joint integrality on the specialized packed-source fibers. A separate arithmetic argument for their remaining points is necessary before replacing either preferred source.

## What was not changed

The defining-polynomial bounds for Z in Q are separate from the undecidability bounds. No change to the defining-polynomial claims, unrestricted undecidability counts, arithmetic premise list, or external accepted theorem status is made here. The present audit is a focused transfer audit, not a certificate for the entire history of the project. Full repository CI has not been run.
