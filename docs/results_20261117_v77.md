## Every real point character has a positive complement

Keep the actual v75 tests, full auxiliary points, point and difference
guards, physical contact map, balanced product W and reciprocal trace V.
At either real embedding every elliptic abscissa satisfies `x>-5`.
For `u>=10`, the first four balanced tests obey
`0<1/(x+u)<2/u`; the two comparison tests lie in `[0,1/u)`.
Consequently, at every one of the 64 point characters,

```text
1 < W < (1+4/u^3)^6
2/(1+4/u^3)^6 < V < 2
0 < eta=28u/((1+u)^2 V^2) < 147/242 < 1.
```

The literal denominator `1+u/x` may be negative when x is negative.
Its nonvanishing follows from the positive cleared denominator `x+u`.
No positivity assumption on arbitrary full auxiliary abscissae is used.

The existing physical choice `z>a+10` forces `u>10` for both contact
sets. The exact lower bounds are `1199520/98251` for seven contacts
and `15120/1093` for nine contacts. Thus the bound is compatible with
the original order of completeness choices and uses no new coordinate.

If the selected complement is square, the identity

```text
c^2=1-d  =>  P_d(1,c)=[2d(1-d)]^2
```

supplies the native pole witness `(1,c,2d(1-d))`. With `d=eta`, the
new complement and terminal ordinate are units at every character.
One finite quadratic root replaces the two free pole parameters.
The sound source has nine total coordinates and finite rank 128.
Its soundness requires ordinary rational abc and the retained source
premises, with a sufficiently large fixed odd defining shift.

The missing assertion remains the existence of actual H, U and rational
physical z with `V(H,U,z)^2-28w(z)` rationally square, at one fixed
sufficient defining shift for every required integer input. Positive
real values and the local preparation do not prove this rational square.

## A constant normalizer aligned at the reference trace

The replacement

```text
w*=16u/(4+7u)^2,   b*=4w*/V^2,   eta*=7b*
```

keeps the actual tests, detector and physical u unchanged. Retain all
old guards and add `4+7u!=0`. Outside the existing exceptional primes
two and seven, positive odd valuations of u retain their valuation in
w*, negative odd valuations become positive, and all even cases stay
even. Fresh primes in the new denominator contribute even valuations.
The local extraction and square-denominator preparation therefore
transfer. At two and seven, arbitrarily deep existing preparation
uses the square leading term `w*~16/(49u)`; the added depths are fixed
before the same prime-pattern theorem is applied.

For `u>=10`, the full auxiliary real estimate improves to
`0<eta*<294/1369<1` at every character. The unchanged intrinsic-pole
theorem gives a complete eleven-variable source variant under the same
premises. No new degree or coefficient-height bound is claimed for its
changed circuit, and it does not replace the best compiled v75 record.

At the formal reference value `V=2`, its complement is exactly
`((4-7u)/(4+7u))^2`. On the chosen real completeness domain `u>=10`,
every finite guarded actual tuple has `V<2`.
Thus this identity supplies no actual witness. The corresponding
nine-coordinate criterion still requires

```text
S^2 = V^2 - 448u/(4+7u)^2
```

at the actual trace and physical slope. This global assertion remains
unproved, as does the original trace-square assertion.
