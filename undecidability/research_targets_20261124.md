# Undecidability research targets — correction, 24 November 2026

The project owner clarified that **163**, not 263, is the target for the quartic route with no additional ordinary-rational-abc hypothesis. The other targets are aligned with the corresponding currently recorded integer-definition witnesses. These are research goals, not attained undecidability bounds, lower bounds, or implications of the existing integer-definition results.

| Route | Quartic target | General-degree target |
|---|---:|---:|
| No additional abc, general family | 163 | 25 |
| No additional abc, specified member | 178 | 27 |
| Ordinary rational abc | 54 | 11 |

The counts for integer-definition polynomials include their distinguished input. The counts for undecidability witnesses count existential rational unknowns only; the instance is in the coefficients. Matching a count across the two problems is a goal rather than a transfer theorem. The term "unconditional" in this target is understood as the no-additional-abc route; no assertion that all retained arithmetic premises have independently been discharged is made.

The corrected target replaces the historical 263 goal in prospective research. Frozen older reports and certificates are preserved as chronological records.
