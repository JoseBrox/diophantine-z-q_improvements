# Interpolated mean and shorter selector (v125)

**303 variables, 252 quadratic residuals, twelve certified integer inputs and one native pole.** This is an unconditional algebraic improvement of the construction of v124, retaining its arithmetic hypotheses, including ordinary rational abc. Soundness does not assume the existence of good witnesses.

## 1. Exact mean, no lifted-mean coordinates

The preceding shifted abscissa chart has \(d=2y_Uh_{H-R_0}\), \(D=d^2\), \(x_*=x(U)-d\nu\), \(e_i=(x(A_i)-x_*)/D\). It uses three coordinates for \(\nu=\mu+a/(c+a^2)\), where \(\mu\) is the normalized derivative mean of v124. Replace \(\nu\) by \(\mu\). The private error chart, its point reconstruction and the trace-square failure fractions of v123 remain unchanged. Every rational zero obeys the old local error-chart implications, now with the lift zero. We must prove that no lift is needed for completeness; the following uses the valuation-isometric interpolation of the supplied Sun audit (section 3).

Fix a nonzero integer tuple as in v123/v124, the corresponding points \(A_i=[X_i]R_0\), the height tree, selected slope and selected prime set. At a selected simple contact prime \(p\) of group \(i\), exactly that group is active and \(\mu\equiv X_i\bmod p\). Write \(r_+,r_-\) for the primary/opposite reduction orders of \(R_0\) there. Use the simultaneous prime supplier and the CRT conditions of the common-numerator lemma of v103, with the same finite avoidance (in particular avoiding nonunit coordinates of the fixed nonzero points and factors of the nonzero labels). All selected primes are coprime to all these orders and the auxiliary fixed congruence modulus \(M\), by the same short-interval/Hasse avoidance. The two opposite order families and primary/opposite cross families have the exact compatibility required by v103.

Choose the reference index \(h\) by its ordinary CRT conditions \(h\equiv0\bmod r_+\), \(h\equiv1\bmod r_-\), the fixed congruences, and additionally \(h\equiv2\bmod p\) at every selected prime. This is compatible by the avoidance just stated; real recurrence still applies on the resulting progression. In the two slots the formal depths of \(H=[h]R_0\) and \(H-R_0\) are precisely those of \([r_+]R_0\) and \([r_-]R_0\), because \(h,h-1\) are \(p\)-units. Write \(\ell=v_p(h_{H-R_0})>0\) in the opposite slot. It is finite and equals the latter formal depth.

The ordinary numerator congruences \(j\equiv0\bmod r_+\), \(j\equiv X_i\bmod r_-\) and the fixed congruences yield a progression \(j=j_0+Rn\), with \(R\) divisible by all the orders but prime to every selected \(p\). In the opposite slot \(V=[j-X_i]R_0=U-A_i\) runs through a formal coset. Its formal logarithm divided by \(p^\ell\), as a function of \(n\in\mathbb Z_p\), is affine with unit linear coefficient: \([R]R_0\) has depth \(\ell\) and the base coset point has depth at least \(\ell\). The formal logarithm and its inverse are valuation-isometries on this good odd-prime disk. Thus its local parameter divided by \(p^\ell\) is a bijective isometry of \(\mathbb Z_p\).

On the disk of \(A_i\), the analytic function
\[
 F(V)=\frac{x(A_i)-x(A_i+V)}{2y(A_i+V)}
\]
has integral power series in the standard parameter \(z(V)=-x(V)/y(V)\), with constant term zero and linear coefficient \(-1\), by the invariant differential and the unit denominator. It too is a bijective isometry of \(p^\ell\mathbb Z_p\) onto itself (Hensel, or contraction of the higher terms). Consequently the opposite-slot quotient \(a_i=F(V)/h_{H-R_0}\) can be prescribed modulo \(p^{\ell+1}\), in particular
\[
 a_i\equiv-\mu\pmod{p^{\ell+1}}.
\]
Modulo \(p\) this quotient equals \(-(j-X_i)/(h-1)\) by the formal-log ratio, so this prescription also gives \(j\equiv X_i h\bmod p\). It therefore gives the required primary-slot ratio \(h_U/h_H\equiv X_i\bmod p\). There is no competing higher-precision primary constraint. The conditions on \(n\) are independent residue conditions at distinct primes, and CRT followed by the same real recurrence produces the global numerator. The shifted error in the opposite slot is \(e_i=(a_i+\mu)/d\); it has positive valuation since \(v_p(d)=\ell\). Primary errors are positive at the selected primes by the unchanged v123 primary estimate and avoidance of nonzero-label factors. Thus the original failure and normalized moment constraints hold locally. The finite-place preparations, height/mean bounds, and real pole realization are unaltered. This proves completeness with the exact mean.

## 2. Rational unit test and reduced formal selector

Use the existing reciprocal abscissas \(f_H,f_U,f_{H-R_0}\) and reciprocal ordinates \(h_H,h_U,h_{H-R_0}\). Put \(s=\operatorname{Tr}(h_U)=2h_{U,0}\), introduce one rational inverse \(s'\) and one rational square \(t_2\), and impose \(ss'=1,\ t_2=(s+s')^2\). Once \(U\) is formal in the primary slot, its reciprocal ordinate has positive valuation there. Hence \(v_p(s)=0\) iff the opposite ordinate is a unit: if the latter is nonpositive its valuation determines the trace, and otherwise both are positive. For \(v_p(u)=1\), \(v_p(ut_2)>0\) exactly when \(s\) is a unit; otherwise \(v_p(ut_2)=1-2|v_p(s)|<0\).

With \(c=1/u\), replace the nested selector by
\[
 R=c f_U+c^2 f_H^2+\overline{f_{H-R_0}}^2,\qquad
 A=R^2+ut_2,\qquad V=A^2+c(C_0-\mu)^2,\qquad
 \chi=\frac{u}{u+V^2}.
\]
Reciprocal abscissas have even valuation at the retained good places (including infinity via the zero value). The three terms in \(R\) have pairwise distinct valuation classes (odd, 2 mod 4, 0 mod 4). Therefore \(v(R)>0\) iff all three formal conditions hold. The two terms in each of \(A,V\) have opposite parity whenever nonzero, so no cancellation can disguise a failed condition. The last fraction is integral and is 1 modulo the prime iff \(V\) has positive valuation; otherwise it is 0. This also handles valuation-zero intermediate registers. A successful slot therefore has the same formal, unit and ratio properties used in the v123 global argument. Inert primes cannot satisfy both reference formal conditions; at a split prime at most one slot succeeds. The trace of this selector and the unchanged packed residuals force one success before using the scalar failure bounds. All v123/v124 global soundness estimates apply verbatim, including the nonzero-label exclusions.

For completeness choose the preliminary real and fixed-place numerator neighborhoods also avoiding \(s=0\). The independence of the conjugate generators on the common twist (v27 weighted trace theorem) implies Zariski density of the paired multiples in the product of the two elliptic curves: any proper connected abelian-subvariety closure would give a nontrivial endomorphism-linear relation modulo torsion. The rational trace of the reciprocal ordinate is a nonzero function on that product (the first summand has a pole independent of the second). Thus a preliminary allowed multiple, including on the required finite-index progression, can avoid its zero locus. Equivalently choose the initial point before imposing the selected-prime congruences, as in v103. Real and fixed-place recurrence retain these open conditions. Since \(s\) is rational nonzero, \(s+s^{-1}\ne0\). With bounded nonzero \(t_2\) on those neighborhoods, as the slope tends to infinity the earlier \(c\to0,\ u\to\infty\) give bounded \(R\), \(A\sim ut_2,\ V\sim A^2,\ \chi\to0\). The former real and prescribed-place limits of the packed obstruction and final pole therefore persist. At each selected prime the interpolation gives success in the primary slot. The even valuation of the pole square factor and the negative-odd-\(u\) normalizer are unchanged. No new pole-realization or prime-pattern premise is used.
