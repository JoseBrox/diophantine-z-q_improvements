\begin{abstract}
A retained scalar square identity expresses each squared input
comparison through two common field coefficients. The comparison
field itself can then be removed from every ordinary integrality
block. This saves 20 rational variables and quadratic equations in
the general system and 16 in the specified system. Applied to the
frozen v102 tables, it gives 948 variables and 803 equations, or
1127 variables and 978 equations. Applied after the contact
compression and BB-native pole replacement, it gives preferred
complete systems with 943 variables and 798 equations, or 1124
variables and 975 equations. The present change is an exact
bijection of rational zero sets; it adds no arithmetic assumption.
The 948/1127 construction is independently reconstructed, rather
than recovered from the unavailable original files.
\end{abstract}

\section{The identity behind the saving}

Work in $K=\Q(\sqrt2)$, so each field quantity has two rational
components. Let $\rho,\epsilon$ be retained rational coordinates
with the existing guard
\begin{equation}\label{guard}
                         \rho\epsilon=1.
\end{equation}
The fields $S,s$ below are already common to all the ordinary
integer blocks. For each block $i$, the table contains rational
coordinates $Y_i,P_i$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{pair}
 2601Y_i^2+255102\epsilon Y_i+6255001\epsilon^2
                                      -4\epsilon P_i=0.
\end{equation}
The literal row uses the existing square register $\epsilon_2$ in
place of $\epsilon^2$; its retained guard is
$\epsilon_2-\epsilon^2=0$, and the checker pins both guards.
Its private input-comparison field $I_i$ is defined by
\begin{equation}\label{input}
 \epsilon I_i+\epsilon(2504s-S)+51sY_i=0.
\end{equation}
In every other occurrence $I_i$ appears only through its square,
in the field equation
\begin{equation}\label{square}
                         \epsilon M_i+L_i-2I_i^2=0,
\end{equation}
where $L_i,M_i$ are other retained fields. This occurrence statement
is checked against every row of each complete predecessor table.

Introduce just two common fields $B,C$ by
\begin{equation}\label{common}
              \epsilon B=(S-3s)s,\qquad \epsilon C=s^2.
\end{equation}
Use the quadratic expression
\begin{equation}\label{A}
                         A=S^2-5008Ss+15015s^2;
\end{equation}
$A$ is not a new coordinate. Delete $I_i$ and its defining equation
\eqref{input}, and replace \eqref{square} by
\begin{equation}\label{replacement}
           \epsilon M_i+L_i-2A+204BY_i-8CP_i=0.
\end{equation}
The two rational components of \eqref{replacement} are ordinary
quadratic residuals, including after the expression \eqref{A}
is expanded. The common definitions \eqref{common} are quadratic.

\begin{lemma}\label{identity}
Under \eqref{guard}--\eqref{common},
\begin{equation}\label{evaluation}
                          I_i^2=A-102BY_i+4CP_i.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $w_i=51\rho Y_i+2501$. Equation \eqref{pair}, multiplied
by $\rho^2$, is precisely $w_i^2=4\rho P_i$.
Equation \eqref{input} becomes $I_i=(S-3s)-w_i s$. Therefore
\begin{align*}
 I_i^2
  &=(S-3s)^2-2(2501+51\rho Y_i)(S-3s)s+4\rho P_i s^2\\
  &=S^2-5008Ss+15015s^2-102BY_i+4CP_i.
\end{align*}
\end{proof}

For an entirely polynomial certificate, suppress the subscript and
put
\[
\begin{aligned}
 a&=S-2504s,\qquad U=\epsilon I-\epsilon a+51sY,\\
 R&=2601Y^2+255102\epsilon Y+6255001\epsilon^2-4\epsilon P,\\
 U_B&=\epsilon B-(S-3s)s,\qquad U_C=\epsilon C-s^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
With $A$ exactly as in \eqref{A}, the identity is
\begin{equation}\label{polynomial}
\begin{split}
 \epsilon^2(I^2-A+102BY-4CP)
   ={}&U(\epsilon I+\epsilon a-51sY)+s^2R\\
     &+102\epsilon YU_B-4\epsilon PU_C.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The independent checker expands this in unrelated symbolic
variables and obtains zero discrepancy. A field identity holds
componentwise over $\Q$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact reconstruction]\label{bijection}
For $k$ ordinary blocks of the stated form, replacing their private
comparison fields by \eqref{common} and \eqref{replacement} gives
a bijection between the complete old and new rational zero sets.
It saves $2k-4$ rational coordinates and $2k-4$ quadratic residuals.
The only division in the maps is by the already nonzero $\epsilon$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
From an old solution, define $B=(S-3s)s/\epsilon$ and
$C=s^2/\epsilon$, and delete the $I_i$. Lemma~\ref{identity}
gives every new row. Conversely, reconstruct uniquely
\begin{equation}\label{recover}
                   I_i=S-2504s-51\rho sY_i.
\end{equation}
The old input definition follows from \eqref{guard}. Identity
\eqref{polynomial}, the retained pair relation, and the common
definitions give \eqref{evaluation}; hence \eqref{replacement}
recovers the old square row. No other row contains a deleted
coordinate. The added fields and deleted fields are unique in
both directions, so the maps are inverse.

There is no nonzero requirement on $S,s,I_i,Y_i$, or $P_i$.
In particular $P_i=0$, a vanishing comparison, or any allowed
point-chart boundary is retained. The construction deletes $k$
two-component fields and adds two such fields. It deletes $k$
two-component definitions, replaces the same number of square
equations one for one, and adds two two-component definitions.
Both savings are consequently $2k-4$.
\end{proof}

\section{Independent reconstruction and the preferred composition}

The user reported earlier checked counts 948 and 1127. Those
original files and commits were not recovered from the available
workspace and repository history. The present construction
independently recovers the reported counts by applying
Theorem~\ref{bijection} to the pinned v102 endpoint tables. The
separate preferred tables apply the same operation to the complete
BB-native v104 tables, which already include the v103 contact
denominator compression. The exact counts are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Stage & Interface & Old $N$ & New $N$ & Old rows & New rows\\
\midrule
Reconstructed v102 & General &968&948&823&803\\
Reconstructed v102 & Specified &1143&1127&994&978\\
Preferred v104 & General &963&943&818&798\\
Preferred v104 & Specified &1140&1124&991&975\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The changed input rows and coordinates are disjoint from the
contact-denominator and BB-native pole changes. The preferred
tables keep every BB-native pole row and every contact-compression
row, with only the required coordinate renumbering. The present
bijection is with each specified predecessor. The earlier
BB-native migration preserves the integer-input projection under
its retained arithmetic premises; it does not claim a bijection
with the old non-CM pole zero set.

The compiler produces all four complete coefficient tables and
their metadata certificates. The independent checker imports no
compiler code. It derives field components by reduction modulo
$z^2-2$, checks \eqref{polynomial} symbolically, pins the existing
inverse-gauge guard and every retained scalar-pair row, and audits
every occurrence of each deleted coordinate. It compares every
retained row through named monomials, checking literal coefficients
independently of renumbering. Its row totals are

\end{center}
It also verifies the complete variable support, coefficient
rings, degree bounds, source metadata, inherited BB-native
metadata, and SHA-256 file hashes. The sum of the squared
residuals is a quartic with the stated number of variables and
exactly the same rational zeros as its quadratic system.

No height, prime supplier, common-point choice, pole realization,
or integer-coding assertion is added by this reduction. The
reconstructed v102 systems retain their old arithmetic premises;
the preferred systems retain the BB-native premise set of
\cite{bb}. These bounds remain above the requested quartic target
263. The proven saving comes from sharing the square of a
comparison rather than storing the comparison itself.
