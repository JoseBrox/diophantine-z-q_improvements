k(r)=(1-d)(d+r^2)/(d-r^2).
Every fiber y^2=d(k(r)^2-s^4) is everywhere locally soluble.
Using the retained simultaneous-twist theorem, infinitely many rational r in (0,sqrt(d)) have infinitely many rational points in their fibers. All E_(dk(r)) can have one fixed rational isomorphism class of rank one.

Two independently varying points on E_n and E_(n(1-d)) give the construction. The projected witnesses are Zariski dense over Qbar, so all retained character guards can be avoided, including other characters with d=1. The cleared radicand has degree 9, 12 monomials and coefficient height 4.
