# Independent audit of the Pell-label proof

Status: PASS for the complete proof supplied in messages after the workspace outage. This audit did not reopen the unavailable local TeX.

Write a=s^2 and z=(a-2)b. Then F_s=t^2-a t z+z^2-1 is the norm form for omega=(a+sqrt(a^2-4))/2. The discriminant in the b variable is 4+(a-2)^3(a+2)b^2, nonsquare for |s|>=2. Thus a nonzero remainder p+tq has nonzero polynomial norm.

Clearing y denominators bounds norm coefficients by O(1)(1+|s|)^D exp(2d h(y)); the integer-root bound yields the stated upper bound on log|b|.

The positive norm-one units of this quadratic order are generated by omega. A norm-one unit strictly between 1 and omega would have its z coefficient strictly between 0 and 1, impossible. The integral coefficient recurrence has U_j(a) congruent to j modulo a-2. Therefore b nonzero implies |j|>=a-2. The recurrence gives
|b| >= (a-1)^(a-3)/(a-2),
which is the claimed logarithmic growth.

This contradicts a subquadratic label-height bound unless the specialization is identically zero on the conic. A generic nonconstant rational label has only finitely many constant specializations by a nonzero coefficient minor. For arbitrary fixed nontrivial G, an unbounded s fiber at fixed y requires every reduced coefficient in s,b to vanish at y. A nonzero coefficient polynomial gives only finitely many such y.

The conclusion is scoped to the retained norm-one Pell source. It does not assert that all redesigned norm equations, scalar-only labels or nine-variable definitions are excluded. No variable reduction is proved.
