# Exact Jacobian obstruction for a direct CM identification

For the mirrored trace product, put `x=V-2` and
`A=-w^2+8w-8`. Its quartic is

`y^2=x^4+A x^2+16`.

On the nonsingular locus `A^2 !=64`, its Jacobian has

`j=(A^2+192)^3/(A^2-64)^2`.

Exact factorization gives

`j-1728=A^2(A-24)^2(A+24)^2/(A^2-64)^2`.

Consequently a rational `w` giving `j=1728` would satisfy one of

`w^2-8w+8=0`, `w^2-8w-16=0`, `w^2-8w+32=0`.

Their discriminants are32,128,-64; none is a rational square.
Thus no nonsingular rational-weight fiber is isomorphic over the
algebraic closure to the fixed source curve of invariant1728.

The other rational CM invariant suggested for comparison also gives

`j-287496=(A^2-72)(A^2-528A-4032)(A^2+528A-4032)/(A^2-64)^2`.

The first factor has roots `±6 sqrt2`; the other roots are
`±264±192 sqrt2`. There is no rational `A` in this case either.
This calculation excludes those two invariants directly. A general
isogeny exclusion would additionally require the classification of
rational CM invariants and is not asserted solely from this algebra.

The factorization

`x^4+A x^2+16=(x^2+4-(w-4)x)(x^2+4+(w-4)x)`

is exact. None of these statements excludes a varying elliptic-surface
coupling involving the source point coordinates, nor individual
rational witnesses on the Jacobi fibers.
