# Independent audit of the trace-only square test

Let `w` be the physical weight, `V=Tr(W^-1)`, and
`b=4w/V^2`. Consider

`C=V^2+w(2-V)`.

At a consumed odd prime, the physical normalization has `nu=v(w)>0`
odd. If `b` has a forbidden negative odd valuation, then
`2v(V)>nu`, so `V` is nonunit and `2-V` is a unit. The two summands
in `C` have valuations `2v(V)>nu` and `nu`, respectively. Therefore
`v(C)=nu` is odd. A rational square root of `C` excludes the pole.

At every prepared point-algebra character, `w>0` and `0<V<=2`.
Thus `C>0` even at `V=2`, where `C=4`. No additional character
guard is needed beyond the existing nonzero denominators. At fresh
split contacts, the actual residue `V=1` or `2` gives square residue
`C=1` or `4`. At fixed places, the preparation `W->1` makes
`C/V^2=1+w(2-V)/V^2` arbitrarily close to one, including the required
two-adic square neighborhood. This audit uses the existing ordered
preparation and does not impose a new prime-pattern statement.

Two exact parametrizations of the fixed-weight conic are

`V=[2(t-1)^2-w]/(t^2-1)`, `Y=2+t(V-2)`,

and

`V=(2-k^2 w)/(1+2k)`, `Y=V+k w`.

The second relation equivalently requires
`(1+2k)V+k^2w=2` for the actual prescribed trace and weight.
These formulas parametrize the conic when its trace is free; they do
not prove that the actual source trace attains the required value.

The proposed local test, positivity, finite-character guards and
conic formulas pass. Global rational realization, with the actual
point tuple and physical contact argument, remains unresolved.
