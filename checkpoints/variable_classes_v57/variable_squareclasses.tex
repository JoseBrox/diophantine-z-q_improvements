\title{Variable square classes with controlled height}
\author{}\date{17 November 2026}

\begin{abstract}
The rational square class allowed by a native elliptic detector may
vary with its input and root point. We bound the contact primes lost
to this class and give explicit height conditions preserving
conditional soundness. Positive even homogeneous forms provide
classes with automatic guards and a uniform height budget. Both
the two-detector construction and the single-cover construction
retain a nine-coordinate census under their stated realization
hypotheses. Those rational realization hypotheses are not proved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Square-class support}

For a finite set of primes $S$ and $a\in\Q^\times$, let
\[
 r_S(a)=\prod_{\substack{p\notin S\\v_p(a)\text{ odd}}}p.
\]
This is the positive squarefree support of the class of $a$ outside
$S$; negative odd valuations are included. Put
$h_2(x,y)=h(1:x:y)$.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:budget}
Let $G\in\Z[Z_1,Z_2]$ be homogeneous of even degree $k\ge0$.
For $G(x,y)\ne0$,
\[
 \log r_S(G(x,y))\le k h_2(x,y)+\log\|G\|_1.
\]
Multiplying $G(x,y)$ by an $S$-unit does not change $r_S$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $x=a/c$, $y=b/c$ with primitive integer coordinates
$(a,b,c)$ and $c>0$. Then $G(x,y)=G(a,b)/c^k$.
Since $k$ is even, its odd valuations occur only at primes
dividing $G(a,b)$. Consequently
\[
 r_S(G(x,y))\le |G(a,b)|
 \le\|G\|_1\max(|a|,|b|,c)^k.
\]
This argument includes cancellation in the reduced denominator.
An $S$-unit has zero valuation outside $S$.
\end{proof}

\section{The two-detector criterion}

Retain the actual three-point conditional source of
\cite{Conditional,Reweighted,Native}, its subgroup and trace-height
premises, and every point and difference guard. Write $A=[n]P$ and
\[
 \beta=X^2+B,\quad T=\Tr x(A),\quad E_h=h_2(\beta,T),\quad
 t_0=\lambda,\quad t_1=(\beta^e+T^e)\lambda.
\]
Here $B\ge3$ is fixed and odd, $e$ is positive and even, and
$\lambda\ne0$. Let $S$ be the unchanged global exceptional set.
For a fixed weak rational $abc$ exponent $\kappa>1$, put
\[
 \alpha_e=e/\kappa-3,\qquad \gamma=8/\kappa-6.
\]
The retained height estimates, when $\alpha_e,\gamma>0$, are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:heights}
 \alpha_e E_h\le h(t_0)+h(t_1)+O(1),\qquad
 \gamma h(t_j)\le\log M_j+O(1),\qquad
 n^2\le A_0h(T)+J_0,
\end{equation}
where $M_j$ is the squarefree nonexceptional odd contact radical.
The constants are independent of $B$ and of the source tuple.

Use the actual fast products and native radicands
\[
 \mathcal R_j=
 \left(1-\frac{V_j}{1+w_j}+\frac{J_j}{(1+w_j)^2}\right)
 \left(1+\frac{1-V_j/2}{w_j}\right).
\]
The letters $V_j,J_j,w_j$ have exactly their actual detector
meanings in \cite{Native}; they are not freely prescribed traces.
Let $G_j\in\Z[Z_1,Z_2]$ be fixed homogeneous forms of even degrees
$k_j$, positive on the prepared real domain, and set
$\delta_j=G_j(\beta,T)$. Fix finite nonempty sets $\mathcal D_j$
of positive $S$-units. Replace rational squareness by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:classes}
 \mathcal R_j\in\bigcup_{a\in\mathcal D_j}a\delta_j\Q^{\times2}
 \qquad(j=0,1).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:two}
If
\begin{equation}\label{eq:twoineq}
 \alpha_e\gamma>1+k_0+k_1,
\end{equation}
then, under the retained height and local premises, every guarded
rational source solution of \eqref{eq:classes} has $X=n\in\Z$
for every sufficiently large fixed odd $B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Outside $S$ and the odd support of $\delta_j$, condition
\eqref{eq:classes} makes $v_p(\mathcal R_j)$ even. The native
valuation proof excludes a negative odd detector pole at every
such prime. At a contact prime not in that support the unchanged
formal comparison therefore gives $X-n\in p\Z_p$.

Suppose $X\ne n$, and let $N$ be the absolute value of the reduced
numerator of $X-n$. The preceding assertion gives
\[
 \frac{M_j}{\gcd(M_j,r_S(\delta_j))}\mid N,
 \qquad M_j\le N r_S(\delta_j).
\]
Also $N\le e^{h(X)}(1+|n|)$ and $2h(X)\le h(\beta)\le E_h$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:budget} consequently yields
\[
 \log M_j\le(1/2+k_j)E_h+\log(1+|n|)+O(1).
\]
Summing and applying \eqref{eq:heights} gives
\[
 (\alpha_e\gamma-1-k_0-k_1)E_h
 \le2\log(1+|n|)+O(1).
\]
The coefficient on the left is positive. The trace-height bound
then bounds $n$ and $E_h$ independently of $B$. But
$E_h\ge h(\beta)\ge\log B$, a contradiction for large $B$.
\end{proof}

In particular both classes may be the same varying value
\begin{equation}\label{eq:example}
                       \delta=\beta^2+T^2.
\end{equation}
The following exact choices satisfy \eqref{eq:twoineq}:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\kappa&e&\alpha_e\gamma&(1+k_0+k_1)/(\alpha_e\gamma)\\\hline
36/35&6&136/27&135/136\\
16/15&8&27/4&20/27\\
121/100&14&76738/14641&73205/76738
\end{array}
\]
The class in \eqref{eq:example} may have prime factors outside $S$.
Its height contribution, rather than a fixed support restriction,
is what is controlled.

The same conclusion permits a fixed finite collection of positive
even homogeneous forms in each block: use the largest degree as
$k_j$ and the largest coefficient norm in the constant. In particular,
each block may independently allow either an original $S$-unit class
or that class multiplied by $\beta^2+T^2$. This includes the original
fixed-menu realization target.

\section{A single actual cover}

Use the single-cover source of \cite{Single}:
\[
 H_0=\beta^e+T^e,\qquad t=H_0^d+z^2,
\]
where $e,d$ are positive and even, and $z\in\Q$ is unrestricted.
For
\[
 \varepsilon=d-\kappa(d/2+2)>0,
 \qquad C_{\kappa,d}
   =\frac{(4+\kappa)d-6\kappa}{(2-\kappa)d-4\kappa},
\]
its established height estimate is
\[
 E_h\le q\log M+O(1),\qquad
 q=\frac{C_{\kappa,d}}{\alpha_e\gamma}.
\]
Let $G$ be as in Lemma~\ref{lem:budget}, of even degree $k$, positive
on the prepared real domain. Allow
\[
 \mathcal R\in\bigcup_{a\in\mathcal D}aG(\beta,T)\Q^{\times2},
\]
with a fixed finite positive $S$-unit menu $\mathcal D$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:single}
The single-cover source remains sound for all sufficiently large
fixed odd $B$ provided
\[
                         q(k+1/2)<1.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $X\ne n$, the same divisibility proof gives
$\log M\le(k+1/2)E_h+\log(1+|n|)+O(1)$.
Substitution into the displayed height estimate gives
\[
 (1-q(k+1/2))E_h\le q\log(1+|n|)+O(1).
\]
The trace-height inequality bounds $n$ and $E_h$ independently
of $B$, contradicting $E_h\ge\log B$ for large $B$.
\end{proof}

For the varying class \eqref{eq:example}, exact admissible parameters are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
\kappa&e&d&q&(5/2)q\\\hline
251/250&14&8&20601327/51566684&103006635/103133368\\
16/15&24&8&128/351&320/351\\
121/100&64&12&40453083/103643216&202265415/207286432
\end{array}
\]
The degrees of these height arguments in the retained coordinates
are respectively $224,384,1536$. These are not degrees of the
complete defining determinant.

Ordinary rational $abc$ supplies the first row of each table.
Within these even-power criteria the ordinary-$abc$ choices have
the smallest two-cover exponent and single-cover argument degree.
Indeed $\alpha_e\gamma<2(e-3)$ for $\kappa>1$, so the two-cover
condition requires even $e\ge6$. For one cover,
\[
 \frac{\partial C_{\kappa,d}}{\partial\kappa}
 =\frac{2d(3d+2)}{((2-\kappa)d-4\kappa)^2}>0,
\]
while $\alpha_e\gamma$ decreases with $\kappa$. At the best
limiting exponent $\kappa=1$, strict absorption therefore requires
\[
 d\ge6,\qquad e>\frac{37}{4}+\frac{35}{2(d-4)}.
\]
For $d=6,8,10$, the smallest even $e$ is respectively $20,14,14$.
For $d\ge12$, even $e\ge10$ already gives $ed\ge120$.
Thus $ed\ge112$, attained by the first single-cover row.
This is an optimization of the displayed height criterion, not a
lower bound on the degree of defining polynomials.

\section{Guards, census and arithmetic realization}

For \eqref{eq:example}, $\delta\ge\beta^2>0$ at every prepared
real character. The native radicands are also strictly positive
at every such character. Thus the additional quadratic radicand
$a\delta\mathcal R$, after multiplication by the square of its
guarded denominator, is a unit of the complete point algebra.
All new denominators and radicand norms are consequently compatible
with the existing one-coordinate encoder. The condition
$\mathcal R\in a\delta\Q^{\times2}$ is equivalent to
$a\delta\mathcal R\in\Q^{\times2}$.

Compile each fixed menu branch by the existing quadratic encoder
and take the finite product over the choices, identifying all
retained coordinates. This introduces no free rational coordinate.
The same finite product includes the choices of homogeneous form;
the set $\{1,\beta^2+T^2\}$ is an allowed example.
The two-cover algebra has rank $256$ per menu branch and the
single-cover algebra rank $128$. In either case there are nine
retained coordinates: the input, six point coordinates, one slope
or square parameter, and one encoder coordinate.

These are conditional construction criteria. Completeness requires,
for every nonzero integer input $X$, actual guarded points with
$A=[X]P$ and a rational slope or square parameter realizing the
displayed varying square classes. That assertion is not proved here.
For the two-cover criterion require the prepared real and
whole-character guard locus, with $\lambda>1$ so that both $t_j>1$;
the single-cover argument is already greater than one. Every original
point-algebra guard must hold. The preceding positivity proof then
supplies the new radicand guards. Assigning free trace and norm values
is insufficient. The usual final factor $X$
includes zero once the nonzero realization assertion holds.

The height argument here uses weak rational $abc$. It does not
establish an unrestricted 21-variable construction or transfer these
variable classes to the unconditional general source.
