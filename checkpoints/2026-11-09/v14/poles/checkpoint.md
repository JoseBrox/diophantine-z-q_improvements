# Pole compression research: V14 checkpoint

## Scope

Work is confined to `work/v14/poles`; publication and GitHub are unchanged. The source preparation hypotheses are: b>0, b<17, square reduced denominator, b locally square at2,3,17, and v2(b)>=3, v3(b)>=1, v17(b)>=2. The zero-preparation pole equations are p²=B(1-e)(1+3e), Bq²=(s²-eV)(s²+3eV), where B=17b and V=B(B-b²). The current finite-root source additionally requires q nonzero. The inherited realization theorem chooses e; it does not prescribe it.

## First candidate counterexample to fixed e=-1/3

For b=(204/55)²=41616/3025, all preparation hypotheses hold. The specialized quartic is

    B q²=(s²+V/3)(s²-V).

With t=204/55 and d=55²-2448=577, it maps to E_N:y²=x(x-N)(x+3N), N=-51d=-29427, with nonzero abscissa in squareclass17. Preliminary PARI/cypari2 results are:

    ellrank(E_N)=[0,0,2,[]]
    elltors(E_N)=[4,[2,2],[[-29427,0],[0,0]]].

Thus E_N(Q) has only its four two-torsion points if the exact rank certificate is accepted. Their nonzero abscissas are -29427 and88281=9*17*577, neither a positive rational square times17. The quartic consequently has no rational point, including its q=0 strata. The map and a fully auditable certificate are being developed; do not yet cite this as a completed elementary proof.

The first tested value b=(204/53)² gave N squareclass-51 and rank0, but V was a rational square, so q=0 supplied a point. The denominator55 example avoids that degeneracy (577 is nonsquare).

## Stronger obstruction: every fixed rational e

The same b=(204/55)^2 also defeats e=1. The specialized quartic becomes v^2=17(u^2-577)(u^2+1731), mapping to E_9809 by (x,y)=(17u^2,17uv). PARI returns exact rank1 and the point (-20449,2356926), whose x-squareclass is -1. The elementary x-coordinate squareclass homomorphism has image size at most |E(Q)/2E(Q)|=8. The rational two-torsion points supply classes -3 and9809, so that known point supplies all eight classes generated by -1,-3,9809. Class17 is absent. Thus the e=1 quartic has no rational point either.

This rules out every constant rational e as a complete specialization. For e notin{1,-1/3}, the first conic fixes the squareclass of B=17b. Two explicit prepared inputs have distinct classes: b0=(204/55)^2 gives class17, and b1=409*(204/1001)^2=17020944/1002001 gives class17*409. The number409 is square locally at2,3,17 (residues1 at8,3,17); b1<17 since17020944<17034017. Hence any fixed nondegenerate e fails at least one of these inputs. The two degenerate e values both fail at b0 by the preceding computations.

Official PARI documentation explicitly states that ellrank's 2-Selmer rank, 2-torsion rank, and Cassels pairing rank are unconditional. Debug output for E_-29427 has Selmer rank4 and Cassels pairing rank2, giving upper rank0. For E_9809 the ranks are5 and2, giving upper rank1; the displayed non-torsion point attains it. Full reproducible certificates and the polished mapping proof are being written.
