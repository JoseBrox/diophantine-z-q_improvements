function of the two six-contact values can retain odd valuation whenever
either contact has positive odd valuation. It includes the exact scale
a(X)=(2X^2+B)^8+T^8, half-integral X, a p-unit input residual, and a positive
slope that is a sum of two integer squares. No full nonintegral witness to
the defining polynomial is claimed.

function Psi(X,lambda), including direct dependence on the input and scale.
Its proof uses the formal coordinates
u=lambda+1, v=a(X)lambda+1 at a simple root alpha of a(X)-1.
The full strengthened paper and independent audit are being prepared as
the next checkpoint. The original proof is already complete.
