Two-point elliptic divisibility route: checkpoint, 17 November 2026

POSITIVE ARITHMETIC INPUT

The independently audited paper
checkpoints/general_scalar_v56/simple_primitive_denominators_v59.tex
proves, for fixed E_d/Q and P in 4E_d(Q), under weak rational abc with
exponent 4/3, that every sufficiently large denominator term B_h has
a SIMPLE primitive prime outside any fixed finite set. Thus odd denominator
support inclusion from H=hP to U=jP forces h|j. The ordinary-abc
odd-primitivity background is already in Silverman1988 and
Cornelissen--Zahidi2007; the explicit weak exponent and simple-valuation
proof are the current useful refinement. Duplication gives the simple
support coefficient 2/kappa-1; the elementary old-support upper bound is
21739/44100, leaving gap311/44100 at kappa=4/3.

LOCAL SOURCE POSSIBILITY

On E_d/Q, retain q_H=sqrt(x(H)) as the one free point coordinate and its
quartic ordinate as a finite quadratic generator. The weight u=1/q_H
is positive odd exactly when q_H has negative odd valuation, i.e. at odd
denominator valuations of H. Numerator primes have negative odd u and
are automatically harmless for the balanced transformation
D=C^2/(1+u C^2). At an active prime, adding f(H), f(U) and a formal-ratio
comparison among the tests would force U formal. This is a possible
support-inclusion block; it is NOT a completed definition.

EXTERNAL INPUT GAP

Even after h|j, m=j/h is an unbounded integer. Congruences X=m modulo
the denominator support of H do not force X=m. They can be faked by m
of size exponential in h^2. The exact perturbation theorem in
negative_results/square_rescaled_weight_perturbation_v59.tex makes this
failure rigorous for any weight whose square class is fixed as X varies
with the retained points held fixed. A closed coupling or genuinely
X-dependent odd-prime support is required.

Adding the current derivative source with a scale involving heights of
H,U is not automatically complete: its Sun prime choices force the order
of the base point to be comparable to the contact prime, while a scale
depending on those auxiliary point heights can demand primes far larger
than their fixed indices. The old source avoids this by fixing a separate
root point A=nP before selecting the growing auxiliary indices.

GROWTH IDEA AND REMAINING REPRESENTATION PROBLEM

There is an elementary useful integer inequality: if h|j, h>1, m=j/h,
|m|>=2, and r^2-j^2+h^2=1, then |m|<h. Indeed r<|m|h and
(|m|h-r)(|m|h+r)=h^2-1, whose first factor is a positive integer.
Conversely for any integer |m|>=2, Pell equations
r^2-(m^2-1)h^2=1 have arbitrarily large h. This could supply a growth
condition compatible with all true inputs. However an elliptic point
R=rP does NOT impose the quadratic relation on indices by its curve
equation. No low-coordinate existential encoding of this relation is
provided. Addition/divisibility alone does not existentially define
integer multiplication (its existential theory is decidable).

RATIONAL-CURVE BUDGET

Using E_d/Q would cost one retained rational coordinate per point plus
finite square roots, not two as for the current quadratic-field curve.
This leaves room below nine for a new scalar or point growth mechanism.
No numerical improvement is asserted from this budget alone.

REJECTED TWO-RATIO SHORTCUT

At opposite formal contacts on the quadratic-field source, ratios
R=zeta(H)/zeta(U), R'=conj(zeta(H-P)/zeta(U)) satisfy
j(R-R')=1 modulo p. The test X(R-R')-1 would read the integer j directly.
But completeness would fix U=XP while requiring it to carry arbitrary
large Sun primes. It cannot replace the root-point source.

CURRENT STATUS

The scaled eleven-variable source has a saved independent completeness,
whole-character guard and census audit. Its separate height proof is
owned by the other agent/root. The EDS route proves a useful arithmetic
lemma but does not yet yield nine or twenty-one variables.
