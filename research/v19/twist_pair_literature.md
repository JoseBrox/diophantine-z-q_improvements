# Rational pairs on quadratic twists: source check

Primary source: Andrew Granville, *Rational and integral points on quadratic twists of a given hyperelliptic curve*, author PDF https://dms.umontreal.ca/~andrew/PDF/hyperelliptics.pdf (19 pages), inspected 2026-11-10.

Section 7, pp.11–13, studies exactly the surface `z^2=f(x)f(y)`. It proves that the surface is of general type for genus at least two and studies rational-curve parametrizations. It distinguishes the conclusions available from rational abc from the stronger Bombieri–Lang input. In particular:

- A degree-one parametrization in either x or y must give a contact automorphism, but degree-two correspondences may be genuinely different.
- The examples on p.13 produce non-automorphism rational families by Möbius maps preserving all but one branch contact and then parametrizing the residual conic.
- Section 8's rational-point uniqueness result, Theorem5(ii), assumes both abc and an abcd conjecture, with a separate special-polynomial hypothesis. It cannot be imported as a consequence of the project's weak rational abc assumption.
- A Faltings theorem for each fixed twist does not give the needed uniform control as the squareclass varies.

The exact six-contact automorphism enumeration settles the degree-one graph families only. It cannot by itself justify a product-height inequality on positive slopes or on slopes above1. The positive-slope source remains a research direction, not a variable-count result.
