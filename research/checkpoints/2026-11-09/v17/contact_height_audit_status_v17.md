# Contact-height source audit, 9 November 2026

The exponent-18 refinement of the pinned original proof is independently verified. It keeps the totient factor in the scalar character order and cancels the modular-index Euler product. It adds no conjecture to the retained source premises.

The proposed exponent-12 proof has a complete derivation from the stated modular delta bound of Jorgenson--Kramer (2009), Theorem 5.6 and Remark 5.8. A subsequent deeper audit raised a specific issue in the published proof: Proposition 5.3(c) and Proposition 5.5 treat natural maps between compactified modular curves as unramified isometric covers, although these maps can ramify at cusps. The argument therefore needs clarification or a replacement estimate. This observation is not a counterexample to the stated delta theorem, nor an assertion that the theorem is false. Until the issue is resolved, exponent12 and prospective exponent10 are not being promoted as independently verified contact-height improvements in the main publication.

A replacement through the finite-place different estimate and the non-Weierstrass-point argument in Javanpeykar is being investigated. Its hypotheses and constants remain to be checked; no improved exponent from that route is yet claimed.

The new 261-variable quartic uses a balanced contact normalization and only the previous exponent19 input. Its validity is independent of the unresolved modular-height strengthening. The new 97-variable quartic explicitly assumes ordinary rational abc and is also independent of this issue.
