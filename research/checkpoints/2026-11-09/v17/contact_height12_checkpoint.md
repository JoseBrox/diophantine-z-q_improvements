# Five-contact logarithmic height with exponent twelve

Status: proof derived and independently checked against the primary sources on 2026-11-09; polished publication proof in preparation. The result uses the same five-contact abelian-surface construction and modularity premises as the pinned upstream theorem. No abc or Szpiro conjecture is added. A universal constant is proved to exist; its numerical value has not been calculated.

## Statement

Let the fixed contacts be infinity, 0, 1, 2401, -343/32. Let S consist of the primes at most 76176. For a rational parameter a away from these contacts, let M(a) be the product of primes outside S where the local intersection multiplicity with one of the five contacts is odd. There is an absolute C12 such that

    h(a) <= C12 M(a)^12.

Here h is logarithmic height. The same argument gives exponent 12/r for a union of r disjoint fractional-linear copies of this contact set, by the existing contact-amplification lemma.

## Modular Jacobian estimate

For every N with g = genus X1(N) >= 2, there is an absolute C_J such that

    h_F(J1(N)) <= C_J N^6.

The height is the original stable Faltings height; changing the conventional normalization only adds an absolute multiple of g.

Let Gamma=Gamma1(N), and choose epsilon=(2+sqrt(3))^(-3/2). Let Y be the inverse image in Gamma\H of the complement of the standard epsilon cusp disc in SL2(Z)\H. Let F_Gamma be the unnormalized canonical density, so the canonical probability measure is F_Gamma/g times hyperbolic measure.

Bruin, Explicit bounds on automorphic and canonical Green functions of Fuchsian groups, eq.(7.1), Theorem 7.1(a), and Section 8.2 give

    sup_Y F_Gamma <= 25.7,
    sup_X F_Gamma <= max(25.7, 0.0126 N^2),
    zeta_Gamma = (1/g) integral_X F_Gamma mu_can - 1/vol(Gamma)
               <= sup_X F_Gamma / g,
    S <= max(172, 3.79 N) / sqrt(g),
    sup_{Y x Y} gr_can <= 15800 + 2S.

The last bound follows since the added local kernel L_delta in Theorem 7.1(a) is nonnegative on its indicated domain. Bruin's final numerical theorem discards 1/g; here it is retained directly from eq.(7.1). Gamma1(N) has no ineffective central stabilizer for N>=5, so there is no factor-of-two ambiguity in the canonical Green function.

There is a rational cusp O given over Z by Tate(q^N) with the N-torsion section q. Its ramification index is N and its integral cotangent line is freely generated by dq. Bruin's thesis, Chapter III Section 5.1, printed pages 96--97 (PDF pages 104--105 in one-based numbering), proves for all N:

    h_omega(O) <= 2*pi/(N*epsilon)
                + (sup_Y F_Gamma/g) N^2 epsilon^2/(4*pi)
                + 2*pi sup_{Y x Y} gr_can.

Consequently

    h_omega(O) << 1 + N^2/g + N/sqrt(g).

The same page of the thesis proves the stable self-intersection bound

    e(X1(N)) <= 4g(g-1) h_omega(O)
             << g^2 + g N^2 + g^(3/2) N.

This uses only the smooth integral cusp and semistable base change; it does not assume N squarefree. Equivalently, use Javanpeykar Theorem 2.4.1 after observing that the minimal stable canonical height is at most the height computed at the integral smooth cusp.

Jorgenson--Kramer, Bounds on Faltings's delta function through covers, Theorem 5.6 and Remark 5.8 (printed page 35), prove

    delta_Fal(X1(N)) << g

uniformly in all N of genus at least two. Their squarefree condition occurs only in the subsequent arithmetic asymptotic Section 6.1, which is not used.

Javanpeykar Proposition 2.4.8 gives

    h_F(J1(N)) = h_Fal(X1(N))
       <= ((2g-1)(g+1)/(4(g-1))) e(X1(N))
          + delta_Fal(X1(N))/4 + 20 g^3.

For g>=2 the coefficient of e is at most 2g. Therefore

    h_F(J1(N)) << g^3 + g^2 N^2 + g^(5/2)N.

Since g <= N^2, each term is O(N^6). No lower bound for g is needed.

## Passage to the contact surface

Use the pinned original manuscript quartic_v9.tex, commit 14e8f601fe0d9173d4a5f084aedc3e07066a7688. Write

    B = product_{p<=76176} p, R=B M(a), C0=210^300.

Its five-contact construction supplies an abelian surface A satisfying

    h(a) <= 2 max(0,h_F(A)) + 128.

The CM case already satisfies h_F(A)<854R. Otherwise the source supplies an extension L'/Q of degree d'<=2304R, a weight-two newform of level N<=C0 R^2, and an L'-isogeny factor A of J_H where X_H=X1(N)/ker(epsilon). The pullback J_H -> J1(N), followed by a homomorphism from A into J_H with finite kernel, gives an L'-abelian subvariety A0 of J1(N), of dimension two, L'-isogenous to A.

Remond, Proprietes de la hauteur de Faltings (2022), Corollary 1.2 and its stable-height observation, gives

    h_F(A0) + h_F(J1(N)/A0) <= h_F(J1(N)).

Bost's dimension-linear lower bound gives

    h_F(A0) <= h_F(J1(N)) + (3/2)g << N^6.

Apply Gaudron--Remond, Nouveaux theoremes d'isogenie, Theorem 1.9(1), to the two-dimensional source A0 over L'. An isogeny to A exists with half the logarithm of its degree at most

    8 [ 32 log 14 + log d'
        + log max(1,log d',h_F(A0)) ].

The standard isogeny-height inequality now yields

    h_F(A) <= h_F(A0) + O(1 + log d' + log max(1,h_F(A0)))
            << N^6 + log R << R^12.

Absorb the fixed B^12 and 128 to conclude h(a)<=C12 M(a)^12. Using a full-dimensional isogeny between J_H and A times its complement would unnecessarily introduce a dimension-dependent polynomial; the fixed-dimensional comparison is essential.

## Numerical status

The displayed argument proves an absolute-constant height estimate. The constant in the Jorgenson--Kramer delta bound has not been extracted or certified here. Thus no explicit numeral for C12, or new effective coefficient-height record, is asserted at this stage. This limitation does not obstruct the existing ineffective-exponent construction. Exact exponent arithmetic is elementary; no computational claim replaces any analytic theorem above.
