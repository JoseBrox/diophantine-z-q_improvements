# Few-variable structural research — V13 checkpoint

Current verified baseline: 27 total variables in an ineffective family; 29 total variables with specified coefficients. Their v12 degree bounds are <2^151 and <2^157, respectively. No V13 variable improvement has yet been proved.

## Architecture and the <10 target

The 27-variable source retains 18 rational point coordinates for nine points on E over Q(sqrt(2)), six rational pole parameters (three independent pairs), one slope, and the input. One final finite-algebra coordinate is added. An unchanged finite-root compiler cannot remove any of those 26 independent base coordinates.

The Weil restriction A=Res_(Q(sqrt(2))/Q) E is a simple abelian surface over Q; the existing elliptic-chart paper proves this by unequal reduction counts 20 and 16 at 17. Any finite collection of algebraic presentations finite over b retained rational parameters and mapping onto arbitrary r-tuples from the cyclic subgroup generated by P must have b>=2r: the cyclic subgroup is Zariski dense in A, its r-fold Cartesian power is dense in A^r, and a dominant image cannot have dimension greater than b. This is an obstruction to recovering arbitrary independent point tuples, NOT to arbitrary definitions or to a specially selected arithmetic witness subset. Reaching fewer than 10 total variables within a one-coordinate finite-algebra framework would permit at most three independent elliptic points even if every pole and slope coordinate were removed; a major replacement of the arithmetic architecture is required.

## Pole sharing and exceptions

For two pole first-conic equations p_i^2=B_i(1-e)(1+3e) with p_1 p_2 nonzero, sharing e forces B_1/B_2=(p_1/p_2)^2. The independent comparison and scalar normalizers do not have an established common squareclass. Degenerate values e=1 and e=-1/3 have p_i=0 and escape this argument. The current single conic chart excludes e=1 but permits e=-1/3 at tau=0. No completeness theorem for either fixed degenerate specialization has been proved.

Sharing the rational chart parameter tau is a different proposal: e_i=(tau^2-B_i)/(tau^2+3B_i) varies with i, so the squareclass obstruction does not apply. However simultaneous solvability of all resulting fixed elliptic twists is not supplied by the existing two-twist realization lemma. That lemma chooses a fresh squarefree n using a controlled Selmer matrix; it does not allow an arbitrary prescribed conic parameter. Requiring common tau would currently strengthen the arithmetic hypothesis.

The fixed-e=-1/3 specialization would remove one retained parameter per pole, giving a candidate 24-variable existence architecture, but its completeness is open under the inherited inputs. It must not be advertised as a proved reduction.

## Effective one-scalar height: a concrete failed shortcut

The odd-product height identity only gives common weak valuation signs at EACH place. It does not prove the signs agree at the two conjugate places, so taking the field norm of the product can lose height. Conversely, taking the trace can have cancellation when both conjugate abscissas have negative valuation. Opposite-parity reduction orders do not remove this problem at every prime.

Exact computation for all split good primes below 3000 found 72 of 209 primes where the orders of P at BOTH conjugate reductions are odd; first examples are p=23 (orders 3,3), 103 (13,13), and 113 (25,25). Thus it is false that odd labels force at most one conjugate point into the formal group. The reproducible script search_reduction_parities.py and reduction-parity-certificate.json record this check. These are counterexamples to that shortcut, not to the proved height theorems.

## Literature findings and limits

1. H. Pasten, Bivariate polynomial injections and elliptic curves, arXiv:1811.02416 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02416). The primary abstract states an unconditional regular injection X(k)->k on rational points of a suitable affine surface X=V x V, where V is an open elliptic curve of positive rank. This is potentially relevant to scalar measurements. It does NOT provide finite geometric fibers, rational inverse formulas, or a one-parameter finite-algebra presentation, and therefore does not by itself compress these witnesses. Full paper retrieval pending; author URL https://www.mat.uc.cl/~hector.pasten/preprints/PolyInj.pdf returned a temporary retrieval error.
2. B. Poonen, Multivariable polynomial injections on rational numbers, Acta Arith. 145 (2010), 123–127, https://math.mit.edu/~poonen/papers/QQ.pdf. Primary full text verified: Theorem 1.1 assumes a Bombieri–Lang-type nondensity statement; Remark 1.3 needs an algorithm determining relevant rational-point closures to construct the injection. This cannot be silently imported as an unconditional compression theorem. Even a set-theoretic rational injection does not make a positive-dimensional algebraic fiber finite.
3. Q. Liu, J. Yang, K. Feng, theta-Congruent Numbers, Tiling Numbers and the Selmer Rank of Related Elliptic Curves: odd n, arXiv:2010.09238 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09238). Primary abstract verified: it classifies zero Selmer rank for E_n:y²=x(x-n)(x+3n) and its opposite-sign companion in certain squarefree families. This is relevant for auditing proposed fixed pole parameters; it does not yet supply their required prescribed-squareclass completeness.

It removes the real approximation restriction on S-P, but makes no variable-count improvement.
